Cookie Policy

Clear information about how PlotNua uses cookies.

This Cookie Policy explains how PlotNua uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience, understand how visitors use the website and ensure the site functions correctly.

PlotNua uses Cookiebot to manage cookie consent in accordance with applicable privacy legislation.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website function properly, remember your preferences and understand how the site is being used.

Cookies don't harm your device, and most of them are deleted automatically after a while or when you close your browser.

2. How PlotNua Uses Cookies

PlotNua uses cookies to:

  • ensure the website functions correctly;
  • remember visitor preferences, such as cookie consent choices;
  • understand website usage through Google Analytics; and
  • improve website performance and future content.

PlotNua does not sell personal data collected through cookies.

3. Types of Cookies

Cookies used on the PlotNua website generally fall into the following categories.

Necessary

Required for the website to function correctly.

These cookies cannot be disabled.

Preferences

Remember visitor choices, such as cookie consent preferences.

Statistics

Help understand how visitors use PlotNua. Used anonymously where possible.

Includes Google Analytics.

Marketing

PlotNua currently does not use advertising or marketing cookies.

If this changes in future, visitors will be asked for consent before these cookies are activated.

4. Managing Cookies

Visitors can:

  • change their Cookiebot preferences at any time;
  • clear cookies through their browser; and
  • revisit their consent choices whenever they like.
