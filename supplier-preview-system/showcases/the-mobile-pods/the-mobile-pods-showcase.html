The collection

Built, delivered, lived in.

What the price covers

Published starting prices exclude foundations (ground screws, concrete poles, slab or blocks, depending on the soil), transport to the site and connection to water, sewage and electricity. Changes to windows, finishes or layout can change the price.

Planning

The supplier's pages state that no building permit is required up to 25 m². Whether planning permission is needed depends on how the pod will be used and where it sits: Ireland's exemption for garden buildings does not extend to use as a separate home. Check your plans with your local authority before you commit.
