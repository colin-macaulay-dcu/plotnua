Ways to make a garden retreat

There are a few ways to make a garden retreat. What suits you depends on how you want to use it, the space you have and your budget.

  • A summerhouse

    A small room in the garden you can sit in. It’s the cheapest and simplest of the four.

    What your garden needs A level base. No electrics needed.

  • A sauna

    For short sessions, usually fifteen or twenty minutes. Electric heaters need to be wired in. Wood-fired ones need a stove and a flue.

    What your garden needs A level base, plus wiring or a flue.

  • A wood-fired hot tub

    An outdoor place to relax. It takes the most setting up of the four.

    What your garden needs A base that takes the weight when full, water, and room for the flue.

  • The whole garden

    A room, a sauna, a hot tub and planting, like the photo at the top of this page. Most people build it up over a few years.

    What your garden needs Room for several structures, and a budget to match.

We don’t think one of these is better than the others. The questions below only cover the garden room, because that choice mostly comes down to the shape and size of your space.

Which garden room would suit your space

Answer three quick questions about your garden.

Where would it go?
What would you use it for?
What is the ground like there?

What still needs checking

  • Whether you need planning permission. It depends on where you live and what you’re putting up, so check with your local authority.
  • The final size, once you pick doors and windows. You do that on the Power Sheds website.
  • How it gets into the garden, if the only way in is through the house.

Why we’re telling you this. PlotNua doesn’t sell garden buildings and doesn’t take payment. We help you work out what would suit your garden, then link you to the supplier.
