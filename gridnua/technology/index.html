The Technology Gallery.

Residential computing and computing heat, entry by entry, checked against official sources.

No residential computing installation or programme is currently available through PlotNua in Ireland. GridNua follows how it is developing, here and abroad.

How we label technology

Two separate labels, because a technology can be mature abroad and still be absent in Ireland.

Maturity

  1. Concept: An idea or design described in public, with no working equipment shown.
  2. Prototype: Working equipment built and tested, but not yet placed in real homes or sites.
  3. In trials: Running in a limited number of real homes or sites, as a test.
  4. Operating commercially: Offered to customers as a service or product, in at least one country.
  5. Widely available: Readily available from several providers or installers.

In Ireland

  • Here today: homes in Ireland can get it now, shown by an Irish source.
  • Being worked on in Ireland: publicly under way here, not yet available.
  • Operating elsewhere, not in Ireland: running abroad only.
  • Large sites in Ireland, not homes: used here at district or commercial scale.

    heata

    heata · United Kingdom

    A small cloud server fitted to a home's hot-water cylinder. Computing jobs run on it, and the heat goes into the stored water, so the existing boiler or immersion runs less often.

    Where the heat goes: Hot water

    Tallaght District Heating Scheme

    Heat Works, owned by South Dublin County Council · Ireland

    Ireland's first large-scale district heating network. Waste heat from a nearby data centre is upgraded and piped to council buildings and TU Dublin's Tallaght campus. Homes are planned for a later phase.

    Where the heat goes: District heating

    • MaturityOperating commercially
    • In IrelandLarge sites in Ireland, not homes
