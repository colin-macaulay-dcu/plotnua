What PlotNua is

You asked us to explain the platform before you decide anything. This page does that — what PlotNua is, how a homeowner actually uses it, and the one point at which a supplier would ever be involved.

It is written to be read once, in a few minutes, and it is deliberately specific about what exists today and what does not.

One

An independent decision platform, not a marketplace

PlotNua helps Irish homeowners work out what their own property could do — and what would actually suit it — before they contact anyone. It does not sell anything, take payment, quote, or hold stock. There is no basket and no checkout.

The homeowner's question is usually not "which company?" It is "is this even possible here, and what would it involve?" PlotNua exists to answer that first question properly, so that the second one gets asked by someone who already knows what they want.

Ten Discovery subjects — things a property could do, each researched from published sources, eight of them with their own property-specific check.

Published today

Atlas, PlotNua's product-evidence base: 516 records held, of which 477 across 65 organisations are governed for recommendation in the garden-buildings journey.

Published today

Two live journeys through the same property engine — a garden room, and a home in the garden — each ending in a result the homeowner can save and compare.

Two

How a homeowner uses it

This is the architecture as it is built. The first six stages happen inside PlotNua. Everything a supplier is good at happens after them.

  • PlotNua
    Discover

    A homeowner arrives on a Discovery — say, a garden room — and reads what the idea actually involves in Ireland: the conditions, the constraints, what is settled and what is not.

  • PlotNua
    Property Check

    They enter their Eircode. PlotNua reads the property, and works through size, boundaries, remaining open space and the published conditions, then tells them plainly whether it looks workable and what still has to be established on site.

  • PlotNua
    Atlas · verified product information

    Only then are buildings shown. Each one carries what Atlas has established — supplier, published floor area, price where a real price is published — and, just as prominently, what still needs checking. Where something is not known, PlotNua says so rather than filling the gap.

  • PlotNua
    My Plot

    The homeowner saves what interests them to their own plot, alongside the property answer it was measured against.

  • PlotNua
    Compare

    They compare saved options side by side on the same evidence — and on the same gaps. Nothing is ranked by who pays.

  • PlotNua
    The homeowner chooses

    PlotNua's work ends with a decided homeowner: someone who understands their property, has weighed the alternatives, and has chosen what they want to pursue.

  • Proposed
    Attributable handoff →

    This stage does not exist yet. It is the point at which PlotNua would introduce that homeowner to a supplier, with the homeowner's permission, by a route both sides could see and count. Nothing has been built, chosen or agreed. See section four.

  • Supplier
    Consultation · quotation · sale · fulfilment

    Specialist product advice, configuration, survey, pricing, the sale and the installation stay entirely with the supplier. PlotNua does not advise on a supplier's products, does not quote, and does not take payment.

Three

Where Superior Pergola could fit

Illustrative · nothing agreed

The garden-buildings and outdoor-structures journey is the one that already exists and already ends in a homeowner who has decided. Superior Pergola Ireland is not in Atlas today and no Superior Pergola product is represented anywhere on PlotNua.

If it ever were, it would sit exactly where every other product sits: described from what the supplier itself publishes, with the unknowns stated, next to the alternatives, and judged by the homeowner.

What PlotNua would need from a supplier

  • Published, checkable product facts — what it is, what it is made of, the real sizes
  • A published price, or a clear statement that pricing is on enquiry
  • Confirmation of where in Ireland it is supplied and installed
  • Written permission before any supplier photograph is used

What PlotNua would never do

  • Describe a supplier as approved, recommended, preferred or a partner
  • Let payment or a commercial arrangement change what a homeowner is shown
  • Invent a specification, a price or a claim the supplier has not published
  • Use a supplier's imagery without explicit written permission
Four

What a "better-qualified enquiry" would mean

You put it well in your reply:

"…the idea of helping homeowners understand what they actually want before approaching a provider makes a lot of sense from our perspective too." Neringa McLoughlin, Superior Pergola Ireland · 21 September 2026

Concretely, a homeowner arriving from PlotNua would already have been through a property assessment and a comparison. The questions a cold enquiry usually opens with would already be answered — by the homeowner, for themselves, before they made contact.

Five

What PlotNua does — and what stays with you

PlotNua, today
  • Researches what a property could do, from published sources
  • Assesses the specific property against published conditions
  • Holds and governs product evidence in Atlas
  • States what is known and what is still open, on every product
  • Lets the homeowner save, compare and decide
  • Owns the homeowner decision journey, end to end
The supplier, always
  • Specialist product advice and configuration
  • Site survey and technical feasibility
  • Quotation and pricing
  • The sale and the contract with the homeowner
  • Manufacture, installation and fulfilment
  • Aftercare and warranty

PlotNua has no ambition to do any of the right-hand column. It is not equipped to, and a homeowner is better served by the company that actually builds the thing.

Six

Independence, stated plainly

Several options, always

A homeowner is shown the suitable options PlotNua can evidence — not one. Atlas holds many organisations in the garden-buildings journey, and the comparison exists precisely so no single product is the only answer.

Payment does not decide

No commercial arrangement changes what a homeowner is recommended or how it is ranked. If that ever ceased to be true, PlotNua would be worth nothing to a homeowner and, before long, nothing to a supplier either.

The homeowner is in control

They choose what to save, what to compare, what to pursue and whether to be introduced to anyone at all. PlotNua does not pass anyone on without them.
