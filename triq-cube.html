TRIQ CUBE by TRIQBRIQ AG

A 9 m² garden office from TRIQBRIQ AG in Stuttgart, built from solid timber rather than panels. Below is what TRIQBRIQ publishes about it, and what PlotNua hasn't yet been able to establish for a buyer in Ireland.

From the manufacturer

Factual product information

PlotNua publishes what the manufacturer publishes, attributed to the manufacturer. Everything below is taken from the official TRIQ Office 3×3 product page. PlotNua adds no interpretation, no rating and no opinion on the product.

Published price

€22,700.00

Complete kit of TRIQBRIQ timber blocks. The published price states that it includes VAT and logistics costs.

The product page also lists three configurable options at published additional prices: PV system, battery and lighting +€2,300.00 · air-to-air heat pump +€1,300.00 · interior desk and shelf +€1,600.00.

This is the price as published on the German site. PlotNua does not restate it as an Irish delivered price, because no Irish price has been established.

What PlotNua has not established

PlotNua states gaps as plainly as it states facts. These are questions for TRIQBRIQ, and PlotNua will not answer them on TRIQBRIQ's behalf.

  • Irish availability is not yet confirmed. PlotNua holds no confirmation that the TRIQ CUBE can be supplied to the Republic of Ireland.
  • Delivery to Ireland is not established. Atlas records where a building is made, not who will deliver and install it here. Those are different facts and PlotNua will not treat one as the other.
  • No Irish price. The published price is a German-market price stated as including VAT and logistics. What an Irish buyer would pay, and under which VAT treatment, is not established.
  • Installation. Whether the kit is self-assembled or installed, and by whom in Ireland, is not established.
  • Configuration. Whether the published headline price includes the three listed options is not stated unambiguously on the page, so PlotNua does not assert either reading.
