Garden Room · Garden office

TRIQ QUBE by TRIQBRIQ AG

A 9 m² garden office from TRIQBRIQ AG in Stuttgart, built from solid timber rather than panels. Below is what TRIQBRIQ publishes about it, and what PlotNua hasn't yet been able to establish for a buyer in Ireland.

Built differently

A garden office built from solid-wood blocks

The TRIQ QUBE isn’t a timber frame clad in panels. Its walls are stacked from solid-wood building blocks that TRIQBRIQ calls BRIQs. Here is how they come together, as TRIQBRIQ describes it.

  • Solid softwood blocks
  • Beechwood dowels
  • No glue, no mortar, no screws
  • Can be taken apart and reused
  1. 01 The building blocks

    Solid wood, cut to fit

    Each BRIQ is a block of solid softwood. TRIQBRIQ makes them partly from timber that would otherwise mostly be burned for energy, such as storm-damaged wood and surplus construction timber, on a robot-assisted line that cuts every block to a precise fit. The BRIQs leave the factory stacked on pallets.

  2. 02 How they connect

    Wood locked into wood

    The BRIQs fit together through studs and blind holes, and beechwood dowels lock them in place. There is no glue, no mortar and no screws: every connection is wood in wood.

  3. 03 How the structure comes together

    Laid like bricks, with no drying time

    The BRIQs are laid course by course, like bricks, into a solid-timber wall, and there is nothing to dry or cure. TRIQBRIQ says a TRIQ QUBE arrives as a kit and can stand within a single day. Who would assemble it in Ireland is not yet established; see below.

    Because nothing is glued, TRIQBRIQ says a structure built this way can later be taken apart and its timber reused.

  4. 04 The finished TRIQ QUBE

    The blocks are the building

    The result is a 9 m² garden office whose solid-timber walls are its structure. TRIQBRIQ says the solid wood helps regulate indoor temperature and humidity. Its published price, specification and what PlotNua hasn’t established for Ireland follow below.

Source: TRIQBRIQ’s own TRIQ QUBE and TRIQBRIQ-System pages on triqbriq.de, read 13 October 2026. PlotNua reports what TRIQBRIQ says and has not tested these claims.

From the manufacturer

Factual product information

PlotNua publishes what the manufacturer publishes, attributed to the manufacturer. Everything below is taken from the official TRIQ Office 3×3 product page. PlotNua adds no interpretation, no rating and no opinion on the product.

Published price

€22,700.00

Complete kit of TRIQBRIQ timber blocks. The published price states that it includes VAT and logistics costs.

The product page also lists three configurable options at published additional prices: PV system, battery and lighting +€2,300.00 · air-to-air heat pump +€1,300.00 · interior desk and shelf +€1,600.00.

This is the price as published on the German site. PlotNua does not restate it as an Irish delivered price, because no Irish price has been established.

What PlotNua has not established

PlotNua states gaps as plainly as it states facts. These are questions for TRIQBRIQ, and PlotNua will not answer them on TRIQBRIQ's behalf.

  • Delivery to Ireland. International delivery is available in principle. Delivery to Ireland is subject to individual confirmation by TRIQBRIQ AG based on the customer’s delivery address. Contact the manufacturer to confirm delivery arrangements and obtain a quotation.
  • No Irish price. The published price is a German-market price stated as including VAT and logistics. What an Irish buyer would pay, and under which VAT treatment, is not established.
  • Installation. Whether the kit is self-assembled or installed, and by whom in Ireland, is not established.
  • Configuration. Whether the published headline price includes the three listed options is not stated unambiguously on the page, so PlotNua does not assert either reading.
