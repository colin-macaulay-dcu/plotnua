PlotArt · Landscape paintings

Landscape paintings for sale in Ireland.

Paintings and mixed-media works of landscape, coast and place, by artists based or working in Ireland: from closely observed woodland and riverbanks to coastal scenes and abstracted views of the land. Every work is shown whole, with the artist’s own dimensions and asking price.

This is a curated selection. PlotArt chooses each work from its title, the artist’s own description and the image, and the page changes as works are sold or published. Some works stay close to a place the artist knows; others move towards abstraction, holding on to its light, colour and structure.

Sizes vary from small framed works on paper to larger paintings. Check a work against your wall with the free art size calculator, or see large artworks of 100 cm or more. When a work stays with you, enquire directly with the artist. PlotArt’s Spaces gallery, a curated home for work about place, is in preparation.

Before you choose.

  • Think about the light. A landscape changes with the light in the room. Ask the artist how the work is finished, and keep works on paper out of direct sun.
  • Measure the wall. Compare the stated dimensions with the wall and the distance the work will be seen from.
  • Ask about framing. Check whether the work is framed or glazed, and whether the size given includes the frame.
  • Get it in writing. Confirm the work, the total price and the arrangements with the artist before you pay.
