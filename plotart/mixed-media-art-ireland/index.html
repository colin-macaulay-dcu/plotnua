PlotArt · Mixed media & collage

Mixed media and collage art.

Works that combine materials: acrylic with collage and resin, spray paint with oil stick and pencil, raw silk with oil, print with ink and pen. By artists based or working in Ireland, each shown whole with the artist’s own account of how it was made.

Mixed media lets an artist build a surface in layers: paint over paper, handwritten text over gesture, resin poured over collage to seal it under a glassy skin. The medium line under each work names the artist’s materials, and the artwork page often explains the process in their own words.

Every work is shown with the artist’s own dimensions and asking price. When a piece appeals, enquire directly with the artist.

Before you choose a mixed-media work.

  • Read the materials. They shape the care a work needs: resin and varnished surfaces are robust, while paper and collage elements need protection from damp and sun.
  • Check the support. Canvas, wooden panel or paper? Works on paper usually need a frame with glazing; panels may be cradled and ready to hang.
  • Look at depth and edges. Layered surfaces and deep panels cast shadows. Decide whether the work hangs as it is or in a frame.
  • Think about the light. Texture changes with the direction of light. A side light brings a layered surface to life; direct sun fades paper and pigment.
  • Keep it dust-free. Clean only with a soft, dry brush, and ask the artist about anything else.
