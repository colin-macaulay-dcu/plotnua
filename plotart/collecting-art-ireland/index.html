PlotArt · Starting a collection

How to start an art collection in Ireland.

A collection begins with one work you want to live with, then another. This guide covers the practical side: setting a budget, finding artists, choosing between prints and paintings, and caring for and recording what you buy. It does not treat art as an investment. Buy what you respond to.

See where to start →Browse All Artworks →

Set a budget, and a pace.

Decide what you are comfortable spending on a single work and over a year. Original prints and small works on paper are a common way in; larger paintings can follow. The selection below shows where each artist’s available work starts.

Get to know the artists.

Read each artist’s statement and biography in their gallery, look at the rest of their work, and follow how it develops. Many collections are built around a handful of artists followed over years. Ask questions: artists are usually glad to talk about how a work was made and what it means to them.

Prints or paintings?

An original print, made by the artist through a printmaking process, is an original work; it may be one of a small edition. A painting or mixed-media work exists once. Both belong in a collection. Read more about original prints and editions.

Buy for the wall, not for a return.

PlotArt makes no claim about what any work may be worth in future. Choose work you want to see every day, at a price you are comfortable with, and judge it by how it holds your attention over time.

Care and records.

Keep a simple record of each work: title, artist, medium, dimensions, year, price, date of purchase and the invoice. Photograph the front and the back. Hang works away from direct sun and damp, and keep any care advice from the artist. If you insure your home contents, ask your insurer how artworks should be listed.
