PlotArt · For artists

Your PlotArt Studio.

Your Studio is where you look after your PlotArt gallery: your profile, your artworks, their availability and the opportunities you’re open to.

Open My Studio

Sign in with the email address your Studio is registered to, the one in your invitation email. We’ll email you a one-time code; there’s no password.

Getting started

  1. Sign in to your Studio

    Choose “Open My Studio”, enter your registered email address and select “Send login code”. Enter the code from the email to reach your Studio.

  2. Set up your gallery

    In “Your profile”, add your portrait photograph, your practice and a short statement. Changes save as you go and stay private until your gallery is published.

  3. Add your artworks

    In “Your works”, choose “Add an artwork”, add its main photograph and fill in the details: title, year, medium, size and price.

  4. Submit for approval

    When you’re ready, open “Review and submit” and choose “Submit for review”. Before your gallery is first published, you’ll be asked to read and accept your artist agreement. PlotArt checks each submission, including the image details, before it goes live.

  5. Keep availability up to date

    Mark each work as Available, Reserved, Sold or Not for sale. Changes to availability, and to the order of your works, go to your live gallery without a new review.

  6. Choose further opportunities

    In “Opportunities & delivery”, say which PlotArt opportunities you’d be open to, such as the Living Gallery, Art for Hire, the Mobile Gallery or exhibitions, and whether they may be shown on your public gallery. To receive commission enquiries, switch on “Open for commissions” in your profile.

Having trouble?

The code hasn’t arrived
Check your spam or junk folder. Codes are only sent to the email address your Studio is registered to, so check that you typed it exactly.
The code doesn’t work
Each code lasts 10 minutes, and asking for a new code cancels the previous one. Use the most recent code only, or request a new one.
“We couldn’t find an active PlotArt Studio account”
You’ve signed in with a different email address, even if it’s another one of your own. Sign out, then sign in again with the address shown in your invitation email.
Your email address has changed
Email hello@plotnua.ie from your registered address, and we’ll move your Studio to the new one.
Still stuck?
Email hello@plotnua.ie with your name. Never send us your login code.
