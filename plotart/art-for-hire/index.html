How Art for Hire works

Three steps, agreed directly between you and the artist. Renting for a workplace? See Business rentals.

  1. Discover an artist

    Browse the artists open to Art for Hire and their galleries, and find work that suits your production, interior or space.

  2. Make an enquiry

    Tell the artist which work interests you, your dates, where the work will go and how it will be used. Your enquiry goes straight to the artist.

  3. Agree the arrangements

    The fee, dates, insurance, transport, installation and return are agreed directly between you and the artist, in writing.

  • PlotArt introduces you to the artist. We’re not a party to the artwork hire and we don’t collect artwork rent or deposits. Any PlotArt fee requires a separate agreement.
  • Hire prices are agreed directly with the artist. Every work is price on request.
  • Transport and insurance are arranged between you and the artist.
  • Photography and screen use are not automatically included. Any permission must be agreed in writing with the artist.
  • Film and television screen use can’t yet be arranged through PlotArt.

For businesses

For rent.
Still for sale.

Bring original Irish art into your office, hotel or shared workspace. Buying is the priority. Rental is another way to enjoy a work while it remains available for sale.

The rental programme is being prepared. These are the standard terms proposed for participating works, subject to each artist and business signing the completed agreements. No works are currently listed for rental, and an enquiry does not make a booking.

How it works

Six months, with replacements. The initial arrangement is six months. If a painting sells, we seek a suitable replacement. A replacement does not restart the six months.

What it costs

A clear monthly charge. The standard monthly charge is 4% of the agreed artwork sale-price basis: 3% to the artist and 1% to PlotArt, a 75% / 25% split of the combined charge. Each artist approves the work, price basis and actual amounts before listing.

Calculations illustrate the standard rate; they are not available artwork listings. Transport, insurance, any deposit and any separately applicable VAT are not included. Your written proposal must show the actual total and tax treatment before you commit.

Who pays for what

The business funds the practical costs. The renting business pays approved packing, delivery, hanging, removal, return and the required artwork insurance. Neither artist nor PlotArt is expected to front these costs.

If the work sells

If a painting sells, we follow the agreed notice and changeover procedure and seek a suitable replacement approved by the business and its owner. If none is agreed, charges for the affected work stop and unused prepaid fees are refunded.

The standard sale-recall notice is 30 calendar days, with a shorter period possible by agreement. A buyer must be told the release timetable. Replacements and their costs need approval. If none is agreed within seven days after removal, the affected placement can end without penalty; unused prepaid charges are refunded within 14 days. No rent is charged for an uncovered gap.

Insurance, paid for by the business

The renting business funds suitable artwork insurance for loss or damage at the agreed value, covering packing, collection, transport, installation, display, removal and return. Its existing policy may be used if suitable cover is confirmed; ordinary business cover or a courier’s cover is not automatically sufficient. Evidence of cover, limits, exclusions and excesses must be agreed before movement, with no gaps between the business’s and handlers’ policies.

PlotArt does not provide or guarantee insurance, and the artist is not expected to purchase placement cover. Each party remains responsible for its own acts and negligence.

Delivery, collection and installation

We are investigating delivery partners and aim to share useful contacts as these are confirmed. Businesses and artists may already have their own couriers or art handlers; either can propose a provider. The artist must approve the packing, handling and transport arrangements, and suitable artwork insurance must be confirmed before any work moves. Large, fragile or high-value works may require specialist art handlers.

The renting business pays the approved packing, outward delivery, installation, collection and return costs, including agreed sale changeovers and specialist handling where needed. These costs are quoted separately from the monthly rental and approved in writing before booking. Sale-changeover costs and limits are agreed upfront. Artists and PlotArt are not expected to advance these costs. Any contacts we share are introductions; you agree the transport service directly with the provider.

The artist does not need to attend installation unless their involvement is specifically agreed. Before collection, the business appoints a receiving contact and a competent installer approved by the artist. Delivery includes unpacking or hanging only when those services are expressly booked.

The artist supplies handling, hanging and care instructions and arranges collection handover personally or through an authorised representative. Packing, loading, unloading and installation are carried out by the people agreed in the booking; the artist is not automatically expected to lift or load the work.

The business ensures condition records and dated photographs are made on arrival and after installation, including a photograph of the final position, and shared with the artist. Any required artist attendance, travel and fee must be approved in writing beforehand and paid by the business. PlotArt provides contacts and introductions; the parties agree the services directly.

Payments and agreements

You hire directly from the artist and pay the artist and approved service providers directly. PlotArt’s fee is separately agreed and invoiced. Any negotiated change to the standard charge needs fresh agreement. Delivery onward to a purchaser is a separate sale arrangement. Existing artist gallery and founding terms are unchanged; general Studio preferences alone never authorise a placement.

How to enquire

Start with your location, wall sizes, approximate number of works, budget and preferred dates. We can begin by email or phone. Artwork, terms, care and costs are confirmed individually.
