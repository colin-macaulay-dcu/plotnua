Sizes for working spaces.

Offices, hotels, restaurants and clinics each ask something different of a work of art.

Where it hangs

Receptions and lobbies
A large work holds a wall that people see from across the room. See large artworks of 100 cm or more.
Meeting and consulting rooms
Works seen from a table or a chair can be smaller, and quieter.
Corridors, restaurants and hotel rooms
A run or a group of smaller works can carry a long wall or a series of rooms.

What to check

Measurements
Every size on PlotArt is the artist’s own, in centimetres. Compare a work with your wall in the free art size calculator.
Light, heat and footfall
Keep works out of direct sun and away from heaters, kitchens and busy doorways. Works on paper are usually framed behind glass.
Weight and fixings
Ask the artist about a work’s weight, frame and hanging, and check what the wall will take.

The details.

The full terms, for when you need them.

If a work sells

Buying is the priority: every work stays for sale while it is on rent. The standard notice is 30 calendar days. We propose a replacement for you to approve. If none is agreed within seven days of removal, that placement ends without penalty, and unused prepaid charges are refunded within 14 days. No rent is charged for an uncovered gap, and a replacement does not restart the six months.

Insurance

The renting business pays for artwork insurance at the agreed value, covering packing, transport, installation, display, removal and return. Ordinary business cover or a courier’s cover is not automatically enough. Cover is confirmed before any work moves. PlotArt doesn’t provide insurance, and the artist is not expected to buy placement cover.

Delivery and installation

The renting business pays the approved packing, delivery, hanging, removal and return, quoted in writing before booking, including agreed sale changeovers. Neither artist nor PlotArt is expected to advance these costs. Large or fragile works may need specialist art handlers; either side can propose a provider from Art Services & Providers. The artist approves the handling arrangements and doesn’t need to attend unless agreed. Condition photographs are taken on arrival and after hanging.

Payments and agreements

PlotArt prepares the paperwork and coordinates the placement. The hire agreement itself is between you and the artist, and PlotArt’s fee is a separate agreement with PlotArt. The artist’s share and PlotArt’s fee are invoiced separately, on the same schedule. Each artist approves the work, price basis and actual amounts before any proposal. Any change to the standard rate needs everyone’s written agreement. Existing artist gallery and founding terms are unchanged.

Project hire, film, television and photography

Project hire is quoted per project, and the hire terms are agreed with the artist. Hire doesn’t include permission to film, photograph or reproduce a work: any photography permission is agreed in writing with the artist. Film and television screen use can’t yet be arranged through PlotArt.
