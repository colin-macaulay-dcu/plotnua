Art for Hire

Original art,
on hire.

An original work by an Irish artist can set the tone of a room, give a shoot its character or make a staged home feel lived in. The works here can be hired for productions, photography, property staging and interiors, and every hire is agreed directly with the artist.

ForProduction designers and set decorators, photographers, advertising teams, property stagers and interior designers.

How hire works

Simple, direct, between you and the artist.

  1. Choose a work

    Each work here has been offered by its artist and checked by PlotArt. Sizes and media are shown with every work.

  2. Send an enquiry

    Tell the artist about your project: the dates, where the work would go and how it would be used. Your enquiry goes straight to the artist.

  3. Agree the hire together

    The fee, dates, insurance, transport, installation and return are agreed directly between you and the artist, in writing.

Good to know

  • PlotArt introduces you to the artist. We’re not a party to the hire and we don’t take payment.
  • Hire prices are agreed directly with the artist. Every work is price on request.
  • Transport and insurance are arranged between you and the artist.
  • Photography and screen use are not automatically included. Any permission must be agreed in writing with the artist.
  • Film and television screen use can’t yet be arranged through PlotArt.

For PlotArt artists. Taking part is optional. Once Art for Hire opens to artists, you’ll be able to choose works to offer from your PlotArt Studio account. Nothing is listed without your consent.
