About Daniel

Daniel Chester is a visual artist living in County Leitrim. He obtained a Masters in Visual Arts Practices from IADT in 2009, previously to that he received a Degree in Fine Art from Sligo IT in 2001. Chester has exhibited throughout Ireland and abroad, with a number of recent solo and group shows such as”Beyond the Stile” SolartGallery “The Land is Ours” Luan Gallery, Athlone. Beyond the Plains Couthouse Gallery, Ennistymon, “The Ethereal Space” Hamilton Gallery, Sligo. “Identity” Westival Westport Co Mayo, Seeing is believing, Solartgallery Dublin. Daniels work has been selected for the RUA awards every year for the last four years and he recently was awarded the cairde visual and the model award residency for his painting “Mayo Marshlands”. Daniel has been awarded a number of artist bursary awards from Leitrim Coco

Daniels work has been purchased by the Google Ireland, OPW, Leitrim Coco,Sligo coco, Sligo IT, Danske Bank and in numerous private collections throughout the world
