About Sarah

Sarah Fitzgibbon is an Irish visual artist and printmaker based in Dublin. She graduated from Limerick School of Art & Design with a BA in Fine Art Printmaking. Her practice is rooted in screen printing. She layers translucent halftone colour and bold geometric forms to build images that seem to shift, vibrate and fold in on themselves. Drawing on the language of op art, her prints play with movement, depth and optical illusion. Overlapping inks mix into new colours, and flat shapes read as impossible three-dimensional structures. Her work is produced in small, hand-pulled editions.

Alongside her studio practice, Sarah has over six years’ experience across printmaking, studio work and education. She was a Studio Technician at Damn Fine Print in Dublin and a Print Tutor Demonstrator at IADT. She is now a Teaching Assistant in Visual Art Education at Dublin City University’s School of Arts Education and Movement.
