About Naomi

Naomi Taitz Duffy is an abstract painter whose practice is rooted in geometric abstraction with a focus on color relationships. Using heavy body acrylics, inks, and raw pigments on wood panels, Naomi explores the fragmented nature and interconnectivity of objects, developing bold, textured compositions that begin as visual puzzles and are developed into unique and harmonious forms.
