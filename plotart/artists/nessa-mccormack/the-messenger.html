Nessa McCormack Founding Artist

The Messenger

€1,500

Medium
Acrylic on Fabriano Paper
Materials
Acrylic, pencil & caran d’ache neo pastel on fabriano paper,
Size
70 × 50 × 3 cm (27.6 × 19.7 × 1.2 in)
Year
2026

This painting is part of the Humming Light collection, a celebration of an energy of life, and exploration of the mysticism of the Otomi people of Mexico, in particular the hummingbird, during my time on artist residency in Ajijic, Lake Chapala, Mexico. I am thrilled to have had this painting selected as finalist in the John Richardson French Residency award by Hambly & Hambly Gallery, Enniskillen.
