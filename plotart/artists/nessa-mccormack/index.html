About Nessa

I am an abstract painter and teacher living in County Wicklow, Ireland.

My work is a celebration of the energy of life.

The vitality, movement, colour, rhythm and interconnectedness that exist within the natural world and within human experience.

I am inspired by the many ways different cultures express this life force through their textiles, customs, rituals, patterns, adornment and surroundings. These expressions reveal a shared human desire to celebrate existence, honour place and communicate a sense of belonging and connection. It is this vitality that I seek to engage within my own practice. I respond through a felt sense of place rather than seeking an exact depiction of what I see. Paint, printed patterns and drawn imagery become ways of building surfaces. Layer upon layer, marks and forms accumulate, echoing lived experience.
