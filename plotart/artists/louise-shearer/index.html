About Louise

Louise Shearer is an Irish contemporary artist based in County Wicklow, where she has developed her distinctive painting practice over the past two decades. Having studied at IADT Dún Laoghaire and University College Dublin (UCD), she has exhibited widely, including at the Royal Hibernian Academy (RHA) and Royal Ulster Academy (RUA). Her work is held in private collections internationally, including that of acclaimed novelist John Banville. Moving fluidly between abstraction and representation, Shearer’s paintings explore the profound connections between nature, human experience and the unseen forces that shape our world.
