About Lena

Lena is an emerging abstract artist based in Dublin. As a recently divorced and busy mother of two she cherishes her art practice as a healing opportunity to reflect on life where she lets her inner child take the stage. With a love for bold colours, shapes and marks , she likes to layer her paints and uses various scratching techniques to create unique textures. Lena says: “I never know where a panting is going to take me. I feel as though the canvas has its very own mind and letting go of the need for control is something I find to be a very useful practice. To be in that playful flow state while painting is the only thing that helps me switch off my busy mind and I think that is priceless. Seeing my work resonating with people is truly special for me and I hope to be in the industry for the long haul.”
