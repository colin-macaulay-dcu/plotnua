    "Sacred Words" explores the power of language to both heal and harm. A dark figure faces a vulnerable heart, connected through a forceful black line suggesting the impact of spoken words. Religious symbolism, expressivemarks and vivid splashes of color create a tension between devotion, tenderness and emotional injury.
