Disconnected Head

€900

Medium
Mixed Media on Paper
Materials
Acrylic, Spray Paint, Oil Stick, Acrylic Stick snd Pencil on Paper
Size
42 × 29.7 × 0.1 cm (16.5 × 11.7 × 0.0 in)
Year
2025

"Disconnected Head" explores the fragmentation of identity and the psychological tension between internal thoughts and outward expression. A distorted face emerges from layers of gestural marks, fragmented language and contrasting colors. Partially obscured by a dominant black form, the figure suggests vulnerability, isolation and a struggle to communicate what lies beneath the surface.
