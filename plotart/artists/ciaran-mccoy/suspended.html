PIGSY Founding Artist

Suspended

€7,000

Medium
Mixed Media on Canvas
Materials
Acrylic, Spray Paint and Oil Stick on Canvas
Size
81 × 130 × 5 cm (31.9 × 51.2 × 2.0 in)
Year
2025

"Suspended" explores the psychological tension of being caught between opposing forces, unable to move forward or retreat. Two dark silhouettes hold a striking red form between them, creating an uneasy balance of control, vulnerability and resistance. The stark composition and expressive black marks reinforce a sense of confinement, while the suspended central form suggests a moment of unresolved judgment.
