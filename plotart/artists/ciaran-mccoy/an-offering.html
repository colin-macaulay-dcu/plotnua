PIGSY Founding Artist

An Offering

€7,000

Medium
Mixed Media on Canvas
Materials
Acrylic, Spray Paint and Oil Stick, on Canvas
Size
81 × 130 × 5 cm (31.9 × 51.2 × 2.0 in)
Year
2025

"An Offering" explores sacrifice, vulnerability and the complicated negotiations that shape human relationships. Two dark silhouettes stand on opposite sides of the composition, holding a suspended, head-like form between them. The central figure appears vulnerable, caught between opposing forces, while the surrounding layers of pink, blue and black create an atmosphere of emotional tension.
