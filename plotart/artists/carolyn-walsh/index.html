About Carolyn

Carolyn Walsh is a Dublin-based contemporary artist originally from Cahirciveen, Co. Kerry. Working across painting, printmaking, photography and collage, her practice explores places, memories and a sense of belonging. Inspired by the Irish landscape, coastal settings and familiar everyday scenes, she creates vibrant, layered artworks that capture the character and atmosphere of meaningful places. Her work is recognised for its bold use of colour, playful compositions and distinctive mixed-media approach. With a background in textile design and graphic design, Carolyn brings a strong sense of colour, texture and composition to her work. She exhibits widely in Ireland, with work held in the OPW collection.
