Introducing PlotArt

Original Irish art.
A gallery for every artist.

PlotArt brings artists and art lovers together. Discover original artwork, explore artists' collections and connect directly with the people who create it.

For artists

A gallery of your own

Each artist will present a curated collection of original work in their own gallery, with their biography and enquiries that come straight to them. It begins with the Founding 25.

For buyers

Original work, shown properly

When galleries open, you'll be able to discover artists, see work at its true size in a room view, and enquire with the artist directly. PlotArt doesn't sell artwork or take payment.

For homeowners

Live with original art

Through the Living Gallery pilot, homeowners may one day host original work for a while. It's being developed, and nothing is arranged yet.

The Virtual Gallery

Every artist,
a gallery of their own.

The Virtual Gallery is PlotArt's permanent home for original art. Each artist has a gallery of their own: a portrait, a biography and a curated collection, with enquiries going straight to the artist. It opens with the Founding 25 and is built to grow to many more artists.

The first artists are being selected through the Founding 25, so no artists or artworks have been published yet.

Explore the Virtual Gallery →

The Living Gallery

Real art. Real homes.

We're developing a pilot in which original work is shown in participating private homes, where it can be seen as it's meant to be lived with, while the artist offers it for sale. Homes would never be public galleries and would never be identified.
