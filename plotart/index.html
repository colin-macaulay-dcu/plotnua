About PlotArt

A new home for
original Irish art.

This is the experience PlotArt is building: discover original artwork by artists working in Ireland, explore their collections, find something you love, and connect directly with the artist.

PlotArt is being built by PlotNua as an artist-first gallery, beginning with the Founding 25. It isn't open yet. No artwork is currently available through PlotArt.

For artists

A gallery of your own

Each artist will present a curated collection of original work in their own gallery, with their biography and enquiries that come straight to them. It begins with the Founding 25.

For buyers

Original work, shown properly

When galleries open, you'll be able to discover artists, see work at its true size in a room view, and enquire with the artist directly. PlotArt doesn't sell artwork or take payment.

For homeowners

Live with original art

Through the Living Gallery pilot, homeowners may one day host original work for a while. It's being developed, and nothing is arranged yet.

Where PlotArt is now

  • The Founding 25. Applications are not yet open. Up to 25 artists will be selected from the applications received.
  • Artist galleries. Planned for the selected Founding Artists. No artists have been selected, and no galleries are published yet.
  • Buying art. Not available yet. When galleries open, buyers will enquire with artists directly.
  • The Living Gallery. A pilot in development. No homes are hosting work yet.
  • Art & Space. A board connecting artists with places to show work, in development.

The Living Gallery

Real art. Real homes.

We're developing a pilot in which original work is shown in participating private homes, where it can be seen as it's meant to be lived with, while the artist offers it for sale. Homes would never be public galleries and would never be identified.
