PlotArt · Art for offices

Art for offices and workspaces.

Original paintings and mixed-media works for receptions, meeting rooms, corridors and shared workspaces, bought directly from artists based or working in Ireland. This selection shows the available works of 80 cm or more on the longest side, largest first: sizes that hold a wall seen from across a room.

Buying for a workplace is mostly about scale, light and the people who pass the work every day. A reception wall is seen from a distance and in passing; a meeting-room wall is seen for an hour at a time, from a chair. Every work is shown with the artist’s own dimensions and asking price, and the artist is the seller: enquire directly about the work you have in mind.

Alongside artwork sales, we are developing flexible art hire arrangements for commercial spaces, subject to artwork availability and agreement with the individual artist. See where Art for Hire stands.

Choosing art for a workplace.

  • Measure the wall and the viewing distance. A common guide is a work about two-thirds the width of the furniture or clear wall it hangs above. The art size calculator checks a size in seconds.
  • Check the route in. Measure doors, lifts and stairs, especially for a large canvas or panel that cannot be rolled.
  • Plan the hanging. Ask the artist about weight and fixings, and check the wall. Independent handlers and installers are listed on art services and providers; PlotArt itself does not deliver or install artwork.
  • Mind the environment. Keep works out of direct sun and away from heaters, kitchens and air vents, and ask the artist how the work should be cared for.
  • Agree the paperwork. Ask for an invoice in the business’s name, and confirm any VAT, delivery and installation in writing before you pay.
