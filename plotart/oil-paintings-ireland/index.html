PlotArt · Oil paintings

Original oil paintings for sale in Ireland.

Oil paintings on linen, canvas, panel and paper by artists based or working in Ireland: from expansive abstract canvases and round wooden tondos to landscapes painted on dibond and figures on panel. Every painting is shown whole, with the artist’s own medium line, dimensions and asking price.

Oil paint dries slowly, so a painter can blend, glaze and rework a surface over days or weeks. The result is depth of colour and a range of texture, from thin, translucent layers to heavy, physical brushwork, which is best judged in a close photograph or in person. Ask the artist for detail images before you decide.

Each artist is the seller of their own work: when a painting stays with you, enquire directly with the artist.

Living with an oil painting.

  • Choose the light. Hang it away from direct sun, radiators, fireplaces and damp walls. Even, indirect light shows colour and texture best.
  • Ask about varnish. Ask whether the painting is varnished. To clean it, dust lightly with a soft, dry brush; never use water or cleaning products.
  • Decide on framing. Many contemporary oils are shown unframed with painted edges; others suit a floating frame. Ask the artist which they intend.
  • Allow for curing. A recently finished oil painting can take months to cure fully. Ask when it was completed and how it should be handled.
  • Check the weight. Large canvases and panels can be heavy. Ask about fixings, and see large artworks for works of 100 cm or more.
