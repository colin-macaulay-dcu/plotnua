Why you

The first ten are
invited personally.

PlotArt is a new initiative within PlotNua, the Irish home-discovery platform. It's a place to discover original art, meet the artists behind it and buy directly from them: a gallery where every artist has a home of their own.

It begins with the Founding 25, a curated group of artists based or working on the island of Ireland who will open the PlotArt Virtual Gallery. Most places are filled through open applications, but the first ten I'm inviting personally, because I admire their work and would love it to be among the first people see.

You're one of them. There's no application to fill in. If you'd like to join, just accept below and I'll send you everything you need.

Colin MacAulay

Founder, PlotArt · part of PlotNua

As a Founding Artist

What your place includes.

Your art. Your sales. Your earnings.

  • Your own galleryA dedicated artist gallery with your biography and a page for each work, presented properly, with honest dimensions and your own words. You keep at least 10 works on show.
  • 12 months freeNo membership or listing fees for 12 months, starting from the day your gallery is first published.
  • Zero commissionNo PlotArt commission on enquiries from your founding year, even if the sale completes later. You keep 100% of your artwork sales.
  • Buyers come to youEnquiries go directly to you by email. You agree the sale, payment and delivery with the buyer yourself.
  • No automatic chargesNothing converts to a paid plan. Any future terms would be offered with notice and need your explicit agreement.
  • Founding Artist recognitionYou'll always be one of the artists who opened the PlotArt Virtual Gallery, and your gallery carries the Founding Artist badge while it's active.

Joining

Five simple steps.

Nothing about you or your work is published until you've seen and approved your gallery.

  1. Accept your invitation

    Email hello@plotnua.ie using the button on this page, or from any email app.

  2. Read and accept the terms

    I'll send you the Founding Artist terms. Your place is confirmed when you accept them in writing.

  3. Sign in to PlotArt Studio

    Once you've accepted the terms, I set up your Studio and you'll receive an email called “Your PlotArt Studio is ready” with your sign-in link. You sign in with a one-time code, with no password to remember.

  4. Build your gallery

    Add your biography and at least 10 works, with good photographs, details and prices. Take your time.

  5. Review and publish

    I review your gallery with you, and it opens in the PlotArt Virtual Gallery. Afterwards, you can mark works sold or change what's on show yourself, at any time.
