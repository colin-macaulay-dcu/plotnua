About PlotArt

Original Irish art.
A gallery for every artist.

PlotArt brings artists and art lovers together. Discover original artwork, explore artists' collections and connect directly with the people who create it.

For artists

A gallery of your own

Each artist will present a curated collection of original work in their own gallery, with their biography and enquiries that come straight to them. It begins with the Founding 25.

For buyers

Original work, shown properly

Discover artists, see each work whole with its true dimensions, and enquire with the artist directly. PlotArt doesn't sell artwork or take payment.

For homeowners

Live with original art

Through the Living Gallery pilot, homeowners may one day host original work for a while. It's being developed, and nothing is arranged yet.

From the founder

A Note from Colin

Art has always been at the centre of my life, as a painter and as a lecturer in Visual Arts Education.

In both, I’ve seen how much remarkable work never reaches the people who would love to live with it. PlotArt grew out of PlotNua: while exploring how homes and gardens can become places for creativity, it became clear that original art belonged at the heart of that story, and that artists deserved a better way to be seen.

So PlotArt gives each artist a gallery of their own, with enquiries that go straight to them. It begins with the Founding 25, and with a simple belief: original art belongs in real homes.

Colin MacAulayArtist and founder, PlotArt
