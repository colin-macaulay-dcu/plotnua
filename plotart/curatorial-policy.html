Curatorial policy

An independently curated gallery of contemporary Irish art.

PlotArt brings together emerging, mid-career and established artists based or working on the island of Ireland, whose work shows quality, originality and a considered approach to their practice.

No house style.

We welcome work in every medium, from painting, drawing and printmaking to photography, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, mixed media, digital and interdisciplinary practice, and on any subject. We don’t favour a particular style, subject, medium or way of working. Instead, we look at the strength of each artist’s work and what it would bring to the gallery as a whole.

PlotArt is a growing gallery. Our aim is a collection that is diverse, coherent and rewarding to explore, introducing collectors to exceptional Irish artists and creating real opportunities for those artists to be seen and to sell their work. Our first artists joined through the Founding 25, our introductory programme; the gallery continues to grow through individual applications.

Selection criteria

Five things
we look for.

No single criterion decides an application, and there is no scoring formula. Selection is a matter of careful curatorial judgement across all five.

  • Artistic quality

    A considered approach to technique, materials, composition, expression or ideas. We look at the work itself, not at how it is described.

  • A distinctive voice

    Originality and individuality: a recognisable direction that is your own, whatever your subject or medium.

  • A developed body of work

    A coherent portfolio that shows your approach, your interests and how your work is developing, rather than a single piece.

  • Commitment to your practice

    Sustained engagement with your work. Qualifications, exhibitions and gallery representation are welcome but never required.

  • Contribution to the gallery

    How your work would complement, strengthen or broaden the PlotArt collection.

How applications are considered.

  • Every application is read individually, with its images, statement and any links you provide.
  • We may ask for more, such as further images, a more focused selection of work or a little more about your practice, before we decide.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee selection. We are building a considered collection, and each application is weighed on its own merits.
  • Decisions reflect the gallery’s curatorial direction. A decision not to include an artist is not a judgement on the value of your practice or on you as an artist.
  • We’ll always tell you the outcome, courteously and clearly.
  • Applying costs nothing, and selection is never influenced by payment.

Artificial intelligence.

We welcome original digital work and AI-assisted practice. Artists tell us about any meaningful use of AI in making a work. Images generated entirely from prompts are not automatically eligible as original artist-created work.

How the gallery is arranged.

We set the order of galleries in the Artists’ Gallery by hand, for curatorial reasons. It is never influenced by any fee, payment or commission arrangement.

Our commitment to artists.

PlotArt respects your independence, your creative ownership and your existing professional relationships. You keep your copyright. There is no exclusivity: you remain free to show and sell your work elsewhere, and to work with other galleries. We aim to present your work properly, introduce it to new audiences, and treat every artist, and every application, with the same care and respect.
