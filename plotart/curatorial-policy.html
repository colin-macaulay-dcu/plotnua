Curatorial policy

An independently curated gallery of contemporary Irish art.

PlotArt brings together emerging, mid-career and established artists based or working on the island of Ireland.

Approach

No house style.

We welcome every medium, from painting, drawing and printmaking to photography, sculpture, ceramics, textiles, digital and interdisciplinary practice, and every subject. We look at the strength of each artist’s work and what it would bring to the gallery as a whole.

Our first artists joined through the Founding 25, our introductory programme. The gallery continues to grow through individual applications.

Criteria

Five considerations.

No single criterion decides an application, and there is no scoring formula. Selection is a matter of curatorial judgement across all five.

  • Artistic quality

    A considered approach to technique, materials, composition, expression or ideas. We look at the work itself, not at how it is described.

  • A distinctive voice

    Originality and individuality: a recognisable direction that is your own, whatever your subject or medium.

  • A developed body of work

    A coherent portfolio that shows your approach, your interests and how your work is developing.

  • Commitment to your practice

    Sustained engagement with your work. Qualifications, exhibitions and gallery representation are welcome but never required.

  • Contribution to the gallery

    How your work would complement, strengthen or broaden the PlotArt collection.

Process

How applications are considered.

  • Individually

    Every application is read with its images, statement and any links you provide.

  • With care

    We may ask for further images or a little more about your practice before we decide.

  • Without guarantee

    Submitting an application doesn’t guarantee selection. Decisions reflect the direction of the collection, not a judgement on the value of your practice.

  • Always answered

    We’ll tell you the outcome, whatever it is.

  • Without cost

    Applying costs nothing, and selection is never influenced by payment.

Also

  • Artificial intelligence

    We welcome original digital work and AI-assisted practice. Artists tell us about any meaningful use of AI in making a work. Images generated entirely from prompts are not automatically eligible as original artist-created work.

  • How the gallery is arranged

    We set the order of galleries in the Artists’ Gallery by hand, for curatorial reasons. It is never influenced by any fee, payment or commission arrangement.

  • Independence

    You keep your copyright, and there is no exclusivity: you remain free to show and sell your work elsewhere and to work with other galleries.
