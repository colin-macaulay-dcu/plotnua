PlotArt · Art as a gift

Original art as a gift.

An original artwork is a personal gift: made by hand, chosen for one person and kept for years. This guide covers how to choose art for someone else, at Christmas or at any time of year, and shows small, publicly priced works by artists based or working in Ireland, one from each artist.

See gift-sized works →Small original artworks →

Choosing for someone else.

  • Think about where it will go. A small work fits almost any home: a hallway, a desk, a bedside wall or a shelf. Ask, if you can, about a wall they want to fill.
  • Follow their taste, not yours. Notice the colours in their home and the art they already have. Bright geometric abstraction, layered coastal works and quiet works on paper suit very different people.
  • Set the budget first. Every price shown is the artist’s own published asking price; ask what it includes.

Christmas and other dates.

Each work is sold by the artist, who arranges collection or delivery directly with you. PlotArt makes no delivery promise. If you need a work by a particular date, such as Christmas, ask the artist before you buy and confirm the arrangement in writing. Allow time for framing if the work needs it.

All year round.

Original art marks an occasion that matters: a birthday, a wedding or anniversary, a new home, a retirement or a thank-you. A work by an artist the person already admires, or one connected to a place they love, carries its own story.

Practical points.

  • Ready to hang? Ask whether the work is framed, and how it is packed.
  • Pass on the story. Keep the invoice and the artist’s description of the work to give with it.
  • Something made for them? Some PlotArt artists welcome commissions; allow plenty of time.
