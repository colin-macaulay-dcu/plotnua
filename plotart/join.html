For artists

Join PlotArt.

PlotArt is an independently curated gallery of contemporary Irish art. If you’re based or working on the island of Ireland and your work has a voice of its own, we’d love to see it.

What you receive

A gallery of
your own.

Our first artists joined through the Founding 25, our introductory programme. The Artists’ Gallery now grows through individual applications, and every artist who joins receives their own gallery.

  • Your own artist gallery. Your biography and a page for each work, with large images and true dimensions. Start with one artwork and add more whenever you wish.
  • Collectors who come to you. Enquiries go straight to you, or to your gallery if you’re represented. You agree every sale yourself.
  • Your own Studio. Add works, set your prices and mark work sold in PlotArt Studio, at your own pace.
  • Your independence. No exclusivity: keep showing and selling your work wherever you like. You keep your copyright.

  • No upfront fees. Nothing to apply, nothing to join, and no membership, subscription or listing fees.
  • You’re paid directly. Collectors pay you, and PlotArt invoices its commission after the sale completes.
  • 15% on sales through PlotArt. Commission applies only to sales to collectors PlotArt introduces to you, agreed within 24 months of that introduction. This includes collectors who tell you they found you through PlotArt.
  • Nothing on your own sales. Sales that come to you independently carry no commission.
  • New commissions are yours. A newly commissioned work is agreed directly between you and the collector, and isn’t covered by the commission.
  • No exclusivity. Show and sell your work wherever you like, and keep working with other galleries.
  • The full terms are in the PlotArt Gallery Artist Agreement, which you read and accept in PlotArt Studio before your gallery is first published.

What we look for

Curated,
not catalogued.

We have no house style, and we welcome every medium and subject. Each application is considered against five criteria, with no scoring formula. Read our curatorial policy.

  • Artistic quality

    A considered approach to technique, materials, composition, expression or ideas. We look at the work itself, not at how it is described.

  • A distinctive voice

    Originality and individuality: a recognisable direction that is your own, whatever your subject or medium.

  • A developed body of work

    A coherent portfolio that shows your approach, your interests and how your work is developing, rather than a single piece.

  • Commitment to your practice

    Sustained engagement with your work. Qualifications, exhibitions and gallery representation are welcome but never required.

  • Contribution to the gallery

    How your work would complement, strengthen or broaden the PlotArt collection.

How it works

From application
to gallery.

Applying, accepting the agreement and publishing your gallery are three separate steps, and nothing is published without your approval.

  1. You apply

    Tell us about yourself and share three to five images of your work, or a link to your portfolio.

  2. We review

    We confirm we’ve received your application, then read it individually against our criteria. We may ask to see a little more.

  3. We tell you the outcome

    By email, courteously and clearly, whatever the decision.

  4. Your gallery opens

    If you’re selected, you receive an invitation to PlotArt Studio. Before you first submit your gallery, you read and accept the Gallery Artist Agreement there. Your gallery goes live once you’ve submitted it and we’ve reviewed it.
