For artists

An invitation to join PlotArt.

Applications are open.

A curated space for contemporary Irish art. We welcome applications from artists based or working on the island of Ireland.

ApplyCuratorial policy

The gallery

A gallery of your own.

  • Your work, shown properly

    A biography and a page for each work, shown whole and at its true proportions.

  • Introductions to collectors

    Enquiries come to you, or to the gallery that represents you. Every sale is yours to agree.

  • Your own Studio

    Add work, set prices and update availability at your own pace.

Selection

How we select.

Every application is read individually. We have no house style and welcome every medium and subject. Applying doesn’t guarantee a place. Read the curatorial policy.

  • Artistic quality

    A considered approach to materials, technique and ideas.

  • A distinctive voice

    A recognisable direction that is your own.

  • A developed body of work

    A coherent portfolio rather than a single piece.

  • Commitment to your practice

    Sustained engagement. Qualifications and representation are welcome, never required.

  • Contribution to the gallery

    What your work would bring to the collection.

Applying

From application to gallery.

  1. Apply

    A short biography and three to five images of your work, or a link to your portfolio.

  2. Review

    We read every application and may ask to see a little more.

  3. Decision

    We let you know by email, whatever the outcome.

  4. Your gallery

    If you’re selected, you’re invited to PlotArt Studio, where you read and accept the Gallery Artist Agreement before your gallery is first published. Nothing goes live without your approval.

Terms

The practical details

Fees
There are no fees to apply, to join or to remain.
Commission
PlotArt’s commission is 15% of the price of an original work or signed limited-edition print sold to a collector PlotArt introduced to you, once the sale is complete. It doesn’t apply to your independent sales.
Introductions
A collector is introduced by PlotArt when they reach you through an enquiry carrying a PlotArt reference, or tell you before the sale is agreed that they found you through PlotArt. Collectors you already knew, or who found you another way, are independent.
Duration
An introduction counts for 24 months. After that, only the sale of a particular work you were already discussing in writing within those 24 months carries commission.
Commissioned work
Newly commissioned work is agreed between you and the collector and isn’t subject to the commission.
Payment
Collectors pay you directly. PlotArt invoices its commission after a completed sale, on the price of the work including any frame, and excluding delivery, insurance, installation and any VAT you charge.
Independence
There is no exclusivity, and you keep your copyright.
Agreement
The full terms are in the PlotArt Gallery Artist Agreement, which you read and accept in PlotArt Studio before your gallery is first published. Founding Artists keep their own agreement; artists joining now become Gallery Artists.
