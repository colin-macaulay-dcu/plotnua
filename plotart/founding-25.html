The PlotArt Founding 25

The Founding 25.

The Founding 25 programme has concluded.

The Founding 25 were artists based or working on the island of Ireland who opened the PlotArt Artists’ Gallery in 2026, each with their own dedicated artist gallery and biography page. This page is kept as a record of the programme.

Founding Artists’ terms, founding-year entitlements and the Founding Artist badge continue as agreed. For current information for artists, see For Artists.

The founding offer

25 artists.25 galleries.100% free for the first year.

Their art. Their sales. Their earnings.

  • Founding Artists were offered no membership, subscription or listing fees, ever.
  • They keep all of their artwork sales — no PlotArt commission on enquiries from their founding year, even if the sale completes later.
  • Nothing changes automatically after their founding year.

Founding places were awarded through a curated selection, not on a first-come, first-served basis.

  • A large artist card in the Artists’ Gallery, led by their work.
  • Space for at least 10 works at a time.
  • Every work shown whole, at its true proportions, with its own page.
  • Enquiries directed to the artist, or to their gallery if represented.
  • Their general location shown only if they chose.

The programme

25 artists.
A new home for Irish art.

PlotArt selected up to 25 Founding Artists from the applications received: the artists whose work opened the gallery, and whose feedback helped shape what it became. Every artist accepted into the Artists’ Gallery, then and since, receives their own gallery and biography page; the Founding 25 were the first, with the founding offer below.

  1. No fees, ever

    No membership, subscription or listing fees, then or later. Each Founding Artist’s first 12 months were commission-free too.

  2. Zero PlotArt commission

    Founding Artists keep all of their artwork sales: no PlotArt commission during their founding year, including on sales from founding-year enquiries that complete after it.

  3. Their own artist gallery

    A dedicated gallery and biography page, led by their work.

  4. Space for at least 10 works

    Each started with one artwork, with guaranteed space for at least 10 works at a time.

  5. Permanent Founding Artist recognition

    Founding Artists will always be the artists who opened PlotArt. Each gallery carries the Founding Artist badge while it's active.

  6. Selected promotional opportunities

    Selected launch features and promotions could be offered. They weren't guaranteed.

The founding terms, as offered

Free means free.

A record of the terms as offered in 2026. Founding Artists’ terms, founding-year entitlements and the Founding Artist badge continue as agreed.

  • PlotArt selected up to 25 Founding Artists from the applications received. Selection was based on the quality, originality and diversity of the proposed founding collection.
  • A place was confirmed only when a selected artist accepted the programme terms.
  • No fees, ever. And no commission in the founding year.
  • A Founding Artist’s founding year begins on the day their gallery is first published.
  • Enquiries that reach a Founding Artist through PlotArt during their founding year stay commission-free, even if the sale completes after the year ends.
  • Their art, their sales, their earnings: the artist agrees the sale with the buyer and keeps what they earn, and keeps ownership of the work until it's sold.
  • The artist keeps the copyright in their work.
  • The Founding Artist designation is permanent; the badge shows while the artist’s gallery is active.
  • Nothing changes automatically. There are never membership, subscription or listing fees, and no commission applies unless a Founding Artist chooses to accept new terms. Enquiries from the founding year stay commission-free for good.
  • PlotArt artist galleries carry no membership, subscription or listing fees, ever. After the founding year, nothing changes automatically: PlotArt intends to work only on commission from sales it introduces, and that would apply only if the artist chooses to accept new terms.
  • PlotArt could not guarantee sales, homepage features or home placements. Homepage artist features rotate fairly among participating artists.
  • Each selected artist received the full Founding Artist terms before anything was agreed or published.
  • The Founding 25 is the PlotArt Artists’ Gallery. The Living Gallery is a separate, proposed programme. Taking part in it is optional, and it is never a condition of being a Founding Artist.

How PlotArt works

From studio to someone's wall.

Each work is presented whole, with its true dimensions, and enquiries go directly to the artist.

  1. Work is presented properly

    An artist gallery of their own, and a page for each work: large images, honest dimensions and the artist’s own words about it.

  2. Buyers enquire

    Enquiries go to the artist, or to their gallery if they’re represented. The artist stays in control of every sale.

  3. Permanent Founding Artist recognition

    Founding Artists will always be the artists who opened the PlotArt Artists’ Gallery, and each gallery carries the Founding Artist badge while it's active.
