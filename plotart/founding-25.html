The PlotArt Founding 25

The Founding 25.

The Founding 25 are artists based or working on the island of Ireland who are helping to shape a new way of discovering original art. They open the PlotArt Artists’ Gallery, each with their own dedicated artist gallery and biography page. Start with one artwork and add more whenever you wish, with space for at least 10 at a time.

The programme

25 artists.
A new home for Irish art.

PlotArt will select up to 25 Founding Artists from the applications received: the artists whose work opens the gallery, and whose feedback shapes what it becomes. Every artist accepted into the Artists’ Gallery, then and later, receives their own gallery and biography page; the Founding 25 are the first, with the founding offer below.

  1. No fees, ever

    No membership, subscription or listing fees, now or later. Your first 12 months are commission-free too.

  2. Zero PlotArt commission

    Keep 100% of your artwork sales: no PlotArt commission during your founding year, including on sales from founding-year enquiries that complete after it.

  3. Your own artist gallery

    A dedicated gallery and biography page, led by your work.

  4. Space for at least 10 works

    Start with one artwork. Add more whenever you wish: you're guaranteed space for at least 10 works at a time.

  5. Permanent Founding Artist recognition

    You'll always be one of the artists who opened PlotArt. Your gallery carries the Founding Artist badge while it's active.

  6. Selected promotional opportunities

    Selected launch features and promotions may be offered. They aren't guaranteed.

The terms, plainly

Free means free.

  • PlotArt will select up to 25 Founding Artists from the applications received. Selection is based on the quality, originality and diversity of the proposed founding collection.
  • A place is confirmed only when a selected artist accepts the programme terms.
  • No fees, ever. And no commission in your founding year.
  • Your founding year begins on the day your gallery is first published.
  • Enquiries that reach you through PlotArt during your founding year stay commission-free, even if the sale completes after the year ends.
  • Your art, your sales, your earnings: you agree the sale with the buyer and keep what you earn. You keep ownership of your work until it's sold.
  • You keep the copyright in your work.
  • Your Founding Artist designation is permanent; your badge shows while your gallery is active.
  • Nothing changes automatically. There are never membership, subscription or listing fees, and no commission applies unless you choose to accept new terms. Enquiries from your founding year stay commission-free for good.
  • PlotArt artist galleries carry no membership, subscription or listing fees, ever. After your founding year, nothing changes automatically: PlotArt intends to work only on commission from sales it introduces, and that would apply only if you choose to accept new terms.
  • We can't guarantee sales, homepage features or home placements. Homepage artist features rotate fairly among participating artists.
  • You'll receive the full Founding Artist terms before anything is agreed or published.
  • The Founding 25 is the PlotArt Artists’ Gallery. The Living Gallery is a separate, proposed programme. Taking part in it is optional, and it is never a condition of being a Founding Artist.

How PlotArt works

From studio to someone's wall.

Each work is presented whole, with its true dimensions, and enquiries go directly to you.

  1. Your work is presented properly

    Your own artist gallery, and a page for each of your works: large images, honest dimensions and your own words about it.

  2. Buyers enquire

    Enquiries come to you, or to your gallery if you're represented. You stay in control of every sale.

  3. Permanent Founding Artist recognition

    You'll always be one of the artists who opened the PlotArt Artists’ Gallery, and your gallery carries the Founding Artist badge while it's active.
