The programme

25 artists.
A new home for Irish art.

PlotArt will select up to 25 Founding Artists from the applications received: the artists whose work opens the gallery, and whose feedback shapes what it becomes. Every artist accepted into the Virtual Gallery, then and later, receives their own gallery and biography page; the Founding 25 are the first, with the founding offer below.

  1. Twelve months' free gallery membership

    No membership or listing fees during your founding year.

  2. Zero PlotArt commission

    Keep 100% of your artwork sales: no PlotArt commission during your founding year, including on sales from founding-year enquiries that complete after it.

  3. Your own artist gallery

    A dedicated gallery and biography page, led by your work.

  4. 10 artwork listings at a time

    A curated collection of at least 10 of your works at a time, chosen with you.

  5. Permanent Founding Artist recognition

    You'll always be one of the artists who opened PlotArt. Your gallery carries the Founding Artist badge while it's active.

  6. Selected promotional opportunities

    Selected launch features and promotions may be offered. They aren't guaranteed.

The terms, plainly

Free means free.

  • PlotArt will select up to 25 Founding Artists from the applications received. Selection is based on the quality, originality and diversity of the proposed founding collection.
  • A place is confirmed only when a selected artist accepts the programme terms.
  • 100% free for your founding year: no membership fees, no listing fees and no PlotArt sales commission.
  • Your founding year begins on the day your gallery is first published.
  • Enquiries that reach you through PlotArt during your founding year stay commission-free, even if the sale completes after the year ends.
  • Your art, your sales, your earnings: you agree the sale with the buyer and keep what you earn. You keep ownership of your work until it's sold.
  • You keep the copyright in your work.
  • Your Founding Artist designation is permanent; your badge shows while your gallery is active.
  • When the year ends, nothing changes automatically: there's no automatic paid subscription and no automatic commission. No charge ever applies unless you explicitly accept new terms.
  • Free membership after the founding year is at PlotArt's discretion. We may continue it, but aren't obliged to do so indefinitely, and we'll give you at least 60 days' notice before it ends.
  • We can't guarantee sales, homepage features or home placements. Homepage artist features rotate fairly among participating artists.
  • You'll receive the full Founding Artist terms before anything is agreed or published.
  • The Founding 25 is the PlotArt Virtual Gallery. The Living Gallery is a separate, proposed programme. Taking part in it is optional, and it is never a condition of being a Founding Artist.

How PlotArt works

From studio to someone's wall.

Each work will be presented as it would hang, shown at true size in a room view, with enquiries going directly to you.

  1. Your work is presented properly

    Your own artist gallery, and a page for each of your works (at least 10 at a time): large images, honest dimensions, a room view for scale and your own words about it.

  2. Buyers enquire

    Enquiries come to you, or to your gallery if you're represented. You stay in control of every sale.

  3. Permanent Founding Artist recognition

    You'll always be one of the artists who opened the PlotArt Virtual Gallery, and your gallery carries the Founding Artist badge while it's active.

Apply to become a Founding Artist.

Tell us about you and your work. It takes about ten minutes, and all you need is a link to where we can see your work.

What happens next

  1. We read every application ourselves.
  2. We email you within two working days to confirm we've received it, with your application reference.
  3. If you're selected, we send you the Founding Artist terms. Your place is confirmed only when you've accepted them.
  4. Nothing about you is published until you've seen and approved your gallery.

There are up to 25 founding places, and applying doesn't guarantee one. You don't need to send images of your work now.
