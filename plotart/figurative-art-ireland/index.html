Figurative art.

Contemporary figurative painting and mixed media by artists based or working in Ireland: figures, faces and heads that sit between the recognisable and the abstract. Some works hold a single figure or a face in profile; others set two or three figures in a charged, invented space.

This is a curated selection. PlotArt chooses each work from the artist’s own description and image text: a work is here when the artist presents figures, faces or people in it. The page changes as works are sold or published. PlotArt’s Being gallery, a curated home for figurative work, is in preparation.

Looking at figurative work.

Contemporary figurative art rarely aims at a likeness. Gestural marks, fragments of handwritten text and strong colour carry mood and psychology; a figure may stand for a feeling, a relationship or an idea. Read the artist’s description on each artwork page: several works here come with the artist’s own account of what the figures stand for.

A figure or face of your own?

The works here are not commissioned portraits. If you have a particular person, figure or subject in mind, the commissions page shows the artists who welcome commissions, and how to approach one.

Before you choose.

  • Measure the wall. Faces and figures hold attention: think about where the work will be seen from, and how often.
  • Ask about framing. Works on paper need glazing; canvases and panels may hang as they are.
  • Get it in writing. Confirm the work, the total price and the arrangements with the artist before you pay.
