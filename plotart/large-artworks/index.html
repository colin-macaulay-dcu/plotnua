Before you choose a large work.

Measure the wall and the room. Check the work’s stated dimensions against the wall you have in mind, and the distance it will usually be seen from. The free PlotArt art size calculator compares a work’s size with your wall or furniture.

Check the way in. Measure doors, stairs and lifts along the route, especially for a large canvas or panel that can’t be rolled.

Ask about weight and hanging. Ask the artist whether the work is framed, how much it weighs and what fixings it needs, and make sure the wall can take it.

Agree delivery with the artist. Collection, delivery, any installation and the total price are arranged directly between you and the artist. Ask for them in writing before you pay.

For a workplace or commercial interior, consider light, heat and where people will see the work, and ask the artist how it should be cared for. Art for business has more on buying for a workplace.
