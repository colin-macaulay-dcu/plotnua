The collection

Finished before it leaves the factory.

What the price covers

NOOK’s starting prices are for the finished unit at the factory. They are not a quote for your garden: taxes (VAT), delivery, foundations (screw piles, an existing level concrete base, or concrete pads) and installation are extra, and NOOK quotes them for your specific address. NOOK guarantees residential units for 3 years, plus each component’s own manufacturer warranty.

Planning in Ireland

A garden room of up to 30 m² can be built without planning permission in Ireland if it meets every condition, including that nobody lives or sleeps in it. NOOK designs a bedroom-with-bathroom layout, but using any garden building as somewhere to live or sleep needs planning permission. Check your plans with your local authority before you order.
