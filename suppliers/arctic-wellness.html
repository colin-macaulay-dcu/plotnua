For Irish homeowners

Worth checking first.

General points, not an assessment of your property.

Planning

A garden sauna is usually treated as a garden structure, and size, height and position limits apply. Arctic Wellness says it checks whether planning permission applies for each build; confirm this for your own garden.

Crane access

Prebuilt saunas arrive in one piece and are lifted in by crane, so there needs to be a route for it. A custom build is made on site instead.

Power

A sauna heater needs its own electrical supply. Arctic Wellness arranges the electrics as part of the project.

Cold water

Cold immersion isn't for everyone. If you have a heart or blood-pressure condition, check with your doctor first.
