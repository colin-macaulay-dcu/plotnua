The collection

Built, finished and lived in.

Gardenhouse24 is run by ABC Gardenhouse IRL Ltd, with a showroom in Limerick. Its buildings are solid log construction in Nordic spruce. Each one starts as a kit, and the foundation, roof covering, insulation, electrics and installation are chosen and priced separately. Before you budget

What the listed price covers

A listed price is the starting point, not the total. These are the costs the supplier itemises separately.

Included

The building kit: walls, floor and roof boards, doors and windows, as each product lists. The listed price is for the kit.

Delivery

Free kerbside delivery to mainland Ireland on orders over €1,000. The lorry needs a space about 10 m long and 4.5 m wide to unload; hard-to-reach sites need specialist delivery at extra cost.

Installation

Optional, and priced per product: €1,430 to €3,030 for the products shown here.

Foundation

Not included. The base must be level and able to carry the building. The supplier sells foundation options (on the Q-BIC 44 A page, for example, from €840 for a self-assembly base to €2,835 for a concrete base).

Roof covering

Not included as standard on most flat-roofed models (felt or EPDM is extra). The LEO barrel sauna includes bitumen shingles.

Insulation and electrics

Extras. On the Q-BIC 44 A page, floor and roof insulation starts at €1,400 and the electrical package is €3,000. The saunas' electric heaters are sold only with the electrical service package.

Prices were checked on 4 November 2026. The supplier is advertising a November promotion, so the price you are quoted may differ. Confirm the current price and what it includes with the supplier.

For Irish homeowners

Worth checking first.

General points, not an assessment of your property.

Planning

Many garden buildings in Ireland can be built without planning permission if they meet the conditions for exempted development, which cover size, height, position and use. A building that is exempt cannot be lived in. Check your plans with your local authority before you buy, especially for the largest cabins: the KAARLO 44's base, terrace included, is about 37 m².

Year-round use

These are solid single-skin log walls. If you want to use a room through the winter, budget for the insulation packages and a 70 mm wall option where one is offered.

Lead time

8 to 12 weeks for the products shown, depending on the model, as listed on each product page.

Warranty

An anti-rot warranty of up to 5 years, depending on the product (some product pages list 10 years). It depends on the timber being treated and maintained as the supplier instructs.
