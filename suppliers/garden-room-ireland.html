PlotNua’s guide to garden room prices in Ireland shows the published ranges for installed rooms and kits across the suppliers it has researched.

Planning

Whether planning permission is needed depends on the room's size, where it sits and how it will be used. Ireland's exemption for garden buildings does not extend to use as a separate home, so an annex with a bedroom, kitchen and shower needs particular care. Check your plans with your local authority before you commit.
