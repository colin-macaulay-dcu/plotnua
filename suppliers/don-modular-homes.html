The whole cost

Ask for a written quote that includes the base, delivery, crane, installation, connections and VAT, so you can compare like with like.

Planning

A garden room that is not lived in can be exempt from planning permission within set limits. A home in the garden is different: depending on its size, it may need permission or a notification under the newer rules for detached units, and every condition applies. Don Modular Homes publishes its own guidance; treat it as the supplier's view and check your plans with your local authority.
