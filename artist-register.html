PlotArt · in development

Your art. Somewhere unexpected.

What if the perfect place for your artwork wasn’t a gallery?

PlotArt is exploring whether homes and gardens in Ireland could become new places for artists to display, show, install and store their work — and perhaps help it find a buyer.

If that interests you, tell us. Registering is free, private and doesn’t commit you to anything.

Register your interest ↓
The Idea

Homeowners have space. Artists need it.

Through A Home for Art, PlotNua is hearing from Irish homeowners who might be willing to offer a wall, a room, a garden or a dry place to keep finished work. Some would simply like to live with original art. Some would open a space to visitors, by arrangement.

Before anything is built, we want to hear from artists: whether this would be useful to you, what kind of opportunity would matter, and what your work needs.

Before You Register

What registering means, and what it doesn’t.

  • It’s free, and it doesn’t commit you to anything.
  • It’s private. Your details are not published, and there are no public artist profiles.
  • It’s early. PlotArt is in development, and no matching service is being launched at this stage.
  • Registering doesn’t mean you’ll be offered a space, an exhibition, a sale, a commission or an introduction. PlotNua is simply finding out whether artists would be interested.
  • If the idea develops, we’ll email you first, and nothing will be arranged without your agreement.

Before you register — what we keep, and what we never ask for

What we keep. The name you work under, your email address, where you’re based, where you would consider opportunities, what you make and which opportunities interest you — and, if you choose to tell us, the space your work needs, a website or portfolio link, and a short statement. We also keep a record that you gave your consent, which version of our privacy notice applied, and when you registered.

What we never ask for. Your home address, your phone number, your date of birth, photographs, prices or details of your sales.

Why we keep it. So that we can contact you about the development of PlotArt and about potential opportunities to connect with homeowners — for example to ask what would be useful to you, or to let you know if the idea develops.

Your details stay private. Registration details are accessible only to authorised people managing the register and to service providers where necessary to operate it. They are not shared with homeowners or made publicly available. There is no public profile, listing or directory, and nothing will be arranged without your agreement. We never sell your information or pass it to anyone for advertising.

Leaving. You can withdraw your consent at any time. Email hello@plotnua.ie and we will delete your record. There is nothing to cancel and no notice period.

How long we keep it. A maximum of 12 months, and less if the pilot no longer needs it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

After deletion. We delete everything the register holds about you, including the record of your consent. Our database provider keeps deleted records in its trash for 7 days and its own recovery copies for up to 1 year; we never restore deleted records from them. We keep only an anonymous log entry with the dates and the reason, which contains nothing that identifies you.

The full details are in our privacy notice.
