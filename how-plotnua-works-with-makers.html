How PlotNua works with makers

Research first. Relationships second.

PlotNua researches products and makers available to Irish homeowners. That research is PlotNua Atlas, and it is much wider than the makers you see featured on our pages.

Featured makers and wider research

Why some makers have photographs.

We only show photographs we have permission to use. Featured makers are the ones who have given PlotNua that permission, so their products appear with images in our galleries.

Many more makers appear as text. A product without a photograph is not a lesser product: it simply belongs to a maker who hasn’t given us image permission. In a shortlist or a comparison, it is considered on exactly the same terms.

Featured maker
The maker has given PlotNua permission to show its photographs. Being featured is not a ranking, an endorsement or a paid placement.
Researched by PlotNua
Atlas holds the product from the maker’s own published information. There may be no relationship between PlotNua and the maker at all.
PlotNua may earn a commission
An agreed referral arrangement exists for that offer. It is shown next to the offer, and it does not move the product up any list.

How PlotNua is funded

Free for homeowners. Funded by makers.

PlotNua is free for homeowners. You never pay us to discover, research, compare or get help choosing.

When a homeowner buys from a maker, some makers may pay PlotNua a referral fee or a commission under an agreed arrangement. You buy directly from the maker: PlotNua does not take your payment or sell you the product.

Where an arrangement applies, we say so next to the offer, including any discount a maker offers PlotNua homeowners.

What never changes

Commercial arrangements never decide what we show you.

  • Which products appear. A shortlist is built from what Atlas knows about each product and your property, not from who has an arrangement with us.
  • Whether a product suits your property. Suitability comes from the evidence, never from a commission.
  • The order you see. No maker can pay to be ranked higher. In our maker directories, the first makers appear in a fresh random order on every visit.

What we know, and what we don’t

We never fill a gap with a guess.

Each fact in Atlas carries its own evidence state: verified, partially verified, or not yet known. Prices are described just as carefully.

  • Price not published: Atlas holds no price evidence for that product.
  • Price not confirmed: Atlas holds price evidence it cannot yet rely on.
  • Priced on enquiry: the maker gives prices by quote.

If something matters to your decision and we don’t know it, we tell you, and suggest the question to ask the maker.
