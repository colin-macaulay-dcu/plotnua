# PlotArt design and communication standard

Approved by the founder on 27 November 2026. It applies to every new or redesigned PlotArt page, explanatory and commercial pages first. Rules marked ✓ are measurable and may be checked by the launch guard.

1. **Headlines:** short and declarative, 2–6 words, in the existing serif; a full stop for statements ("Two ways to hire.").
2. **Supporting text** ✓: hero sub-line up to 30 words; section introduction up to 25 words; visible paragraphs up to 45 words. Anything longer goes into an expandable section or a linked page.
3. **One primary action per section** ✓: a filled ivory button, plus at most one quiet text link.
4. **Numbers only when meaningful:** large figures (4%, 6 months, €80) where the number is the message; never decorative counts.
5. **Numbered steps only for real sequences** ✓: 01, 02, 03…; no numbering on unordered lists.
6. **Detail on demand:** terms, conditions and edge cases in expandable sections or secondary pages; the visible page states the offer, the price, the process and the next step.
7. **Commercial clarity:** on any page that sells or rents, the price model and the action appear within the first two screens on desktop ✓.
8. **Purposeful space** ✓: no empty vertical gap over 160 px on desktop (120 px on mobile) between content blocks; spacing separates sections, not sentences.
9. **No filler:** no repeated headings, no restated introductions, no decorative icons, no empty-state blocks when nothing is available (hide the section instead).
10. **Identity unchanged:** anthracite, warm ivory, Instrument Serif and Hanken Grotesk, artwork-led, square frames, uncropped art, existing image permission and disclosure rules.
11. **Preserve what works:** artist pages, artwork pages and the gallery grids are the reference. Do not redesign them.

Shared styles for the standard live in `worker/plotart-studio/public-hire/art-for-hire.css` (`afx-` classes); reuse them on other pages rather than inventing new components.

Commercial wording follows the approved business rental model PA-BHR-2026-11 v1.1: PlotArt selects the work and coordinates the placement; each artist approves; the hire agreement is between the business and the artist, and PlotArt's fee is a separate agreement. General hire terms (PA-AFH) are not active until separately approved.
