The rest of the budget

The office price is only part of the project.

Both product pages exclude base pads, installation, craneage and haulage. The maker gives $site_works excluding VAT as a typical additional allowance, dependent on site complexity. Connection to the house’s electricity supply is separately priced.

PlotNua’s reading: the maker’s description of a finished interior does not make the headline price an all-in garden project. Get a written quotation for your address before deciding whether the total fits your budget.

Send photographs of the access route as well as the proposed position. Ask whether the quotation includes every visit, lifting operation and connection needed to leave the office ready for you to use. Keep the agreed drawing and specification with the quote.

Before you choose

Start with a working day in the room.

  • Your desk and storage. Mark out the furniture on a plan. Would the fitted Signature layout work for your equipment, or would you prefer your own furniture?
  • Comfort. Ask for the floor, wall, roof and glazing specifications, ventilation provision, heating controls and running-cost information for the agreed model.
  • Connections. Ask who arranges and certifies the electrical supply, how internet will reach the room and which costs sit outside the quote.
  • Availability. Confirm delivery to your county, stock or production timing, the site-survey process and the payment schedule directly with Concept Modular.
  • Aftercare. Request the written warranty and its exclusions. Ask which parts of the finished office it covers and who handles a claim.

A compact size or the name “office” does not establish planning compliance for your property. Use PlotNua’s garden-room planning guide to understand the conditions, then check the proposed building and its intended use.
