These are starting prices, not quotations for your address. Model numbers are names: do not read “Shomera 12” or “Shomera 25” as verified usable floor areas. Ask for drawings that distinguish internal space, external dimensions and covered outdoor areas.

Classic or Contemporary?

Classic provides the timber-frame studio format. Contemporary adds a more pronounced overhang and recessed outdoor space. Compare the room you can furnish and the covered space separately; a desk, sofa or exercise equipment needs usable space inside the walls.

For either range, ask for the exact cladding, glazing, insulation and ventilation specification for your chosen model. Published descriptions are a useful starting point, but they do not establish measured performance for a particular finished building.

Installed does not settle every finishing cost

Shomera describes managing the work from design through construction and installation. Its main garden-studio page says interiors are left ready for decoration and external landscaping remains the customer’s responsibility. Agree the painting, paths, planting, reinstatement and utility connections in the written specification.

The maker describes concrete-pad foundations and galvanised steel support beams. Ask how the quotation deals with your ground conditions, levels and access route rather than assuming a standard foundation detail covers every garden.

Questions to put in the quotation

  • VAT and total price: Is VAT included, at what rate, and what is the total for the agreed specification?
  • Scope: Which groundworks, delivery, installation, electrical connections and finishes are included? Who handles any excluded work?
  • Drawings: How much usable internal space remains after the wall build-up, and where do overhangs and projections sit?
  • Everyday use: What heating, ventilation, sockets and internet connection suit your office, studio or gym?
  • Programme and aftercare: What dates, payment stages, maintenance requirements and written warranty terms apply to your order?

Check planning for the intended use

A suggested use, model name or supplier statement does not establish a planning exemption for your property. Use PlotNua’s garden-room planning guidance and check the proposed building and its use against the full conditions. If somebody will sleep there, follow the separate garden-home guidance.
