Here today

Panels turn daylight into electricity the house uses as it is made.

A balcony railingmaybe the next one.
Being worked on in Ireland

In Germany, small plug-in panels hang from balconies. Ireland has not yet published a dedicated regulatory pathway for these systems. ESB Networks is involved in developing an approach, but homeowners should not assume that plug-in installation is currently permitted without following the applicable connection and safety requirements.

4Host computing

A computer that heats your water.

The surprise on this page.

Your hot water cylinderpart of a data centre.
Operating elsewhere, not in Ireland

In Britain there are occupied homes with a computer bolted to the hot water cylinder. A business pays for the electricity; the work it does becomes the household's hot water.

Your towna place where computing is spread out, not stacked up.
Large sites in Ireland, not homes

In Ireland, Tandem Compute is developing distributed AI infrastructure at larger commercial and community sites, not homes.

None of this is available to an Irish homeowner today. What would a house be asked? The Compute Readiness check: information, not an application.
