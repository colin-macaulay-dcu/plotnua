What we keep, and what we never ask for

What we keep. Your first name, your email address, your county and the area you chose, how much space you’re looking for, roughly when, and, if you tell us, your town or general area, how far you’d travel and anything you add in the note. Your answers to the Grower Check are not sent to us; they fill in this form, where you can see and change them. We also keep the administrative records needed to manage your registration, your consent and its status.

What we never ask for. Your address, your Eircode, your phone number, your surname, your age, or anything about your health. We don’t collect them, so we can’t hold them or lose them.

Why we keep it. So that we can tell you if someone near you has growing space they’d consider sharing.

Joining the register does not share your details with anyone. No other person sees your record. There is no public listing, no public map, no profile page, and no directory. Your record is private to PlotNua.

If we think there’s a possible fit, we’ll email you and ask. We’ll describe the homeowner in general terms only. No name, no email, no contact details.

Nothing is shared until you say yes. Your email address only reaches another person after you have said yes to that specific introduction. If you don’t reply, nothing happens.

What “registered” means. Only that you asked PlotNua to tell you about potential growing space. Nobody has checked or approved anything.

We never sell your information, and we never pass it to anyone for advertising.

Leaving. Email hello@plotnua.ie and we will delete your record. There’s nothing to cancel and no notice period.

How long we keep it. A maximum of 24 months. You can ask us to delete it at any time, and we may delete it sooner if it is no longer needed.

One thing we keep after deletion. A separate note recording that you gave permission and that we honoured your request, holding a one-way digital fingerprint made from your email address, not the address itself.

The full details are in our privacy notice.
