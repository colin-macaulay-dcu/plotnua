Free for homeowners

You never pay PlotNua.

PlotNua is free for homeowners: discovering, researching, comparing and getting help choosing all cost you nothing.

Some makers may pay PlotNua a referral fee or a commission when a homeowner buys from them. Where that applies, we say so next to the offer. It never changes which products we show you, whether we think they suit your property, or the order we show them in.
