Garden offices in Ireland

One garden. Different ways to work.

Start with the space you need, rather than a particular product. A garden office might be a single desk away from household distractions, or a more flexible room for work and creative projects.

A compact workspace

A desk, a chair, reliable connectivity and enough room to think. Consider light, ventilation, power and the space needed to move comfortably.

A creative studio

For drawing, designing or making. Think about storage, daylight, surfaces and the equipment your work requires.

A shared office

Two people working from home? Check desk positions, acoustic separation, heating and how the room will be used throughout the day.

A flexible garden room

Work on weekdays, other uses at weekends. Confirm that the design and proposed use suit your property and circumstances.

Before you decide

The practical questions matter.

Planning and permitted use

Do not assume a garden office is automatically exempt from planning permission. Size, location, existing development and proposed use all matter.

Year-round comfort

Ask about insulation, glazing, heating, ventilation and how the building performs in Irish weather.

Site access and installation

Confirm foundations, drainage, delivery access, assembly, electricity and broadband before comparing quotations.

What the quote includes

Compare like with like: VAT, base, transport, installation, finishes and electrical connections may be priced separately.

This is general information, not a planning determination or building specification. Check current requirements with your local planning authority and obtain a site-specific written quotation.
