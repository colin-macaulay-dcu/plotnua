A Garden Office That Works for You

A garden office could give your working day a place of its own. Imagine stepping out of the house and into a quiet room at the end of the garden.

Garden offices in Ireland

One garden. Different ways to work.

Start with the space you need, rather than a particular product. A garden office might be a single desk away from household distractions, or a more flexible room for work and creative projects.

A compact workspace

A desk, a chair, reliable connectivity and enough room to think. Consider light, ventilation, power and the space needed to move comfortably.

A creative studio

For drawing, designing or making. Think about storage, daylight, surfaces and the equipment your work requires.

A shared office

Two people working from home? Check desk positions, acoustic separation, heating and how the room will be used throughout the day.

A flexible garden room

Work on weekdays, other uses at weekends. Confirm that the design and proposed use suit your property and circumstances.

Before you decide

The practical questions matter.

Planning and permitted use

Do not assume a garden office is automatically exempt from planning permission. Size, location, existing development and proposed use all matter.

Year-round comfort

Ask about insulation, glazing, heating, ventilation and how the building performs in Irish weather.

Site access and installation

Confirm foundations, drainage, delivery access, assembly, electricity and broadband before comparing quotations.

What the quote includes

Compare like with like: VAT, base, transport, installation, finishes and electrical connections may be priced separately.

This is general information, not a planning determination or building specification. Check current requirements with your local planning authority and obtain a site-specific written quotation.

Frequently asked questions

Questions to ask before choosing a garden office in Ireland.

Do I need planning permission for a garden office in Ireland?

Do not assume that a garden building can be used as a home office without permission. Exemptions for domestic garden structures have conditions, and business or work-related use can change the assessment. Check the exact proposal with your local planning authority before committing.

Can a garden office be used all year?

Potentially, but the specification matters. Compare insulation, glazing, heating, ventilation and moisture management, and ask the maker for written details of year-round performance.

What costs should I include beyond the garden office price?

Check whether the quotation covers the base, site preparation, delivery, lifting equipment, installation, VAT, electricity, broadband and internal finishes. See PlotNua's garden-room price guide for a wider comparison.

How small can a garden office be?

Start with your desk, chair, storage and movement space rather than a headline floor area. Measure the working layout and ask for internal dimensions, not only external measurements.

These answers are general guidance, not legal or planning advice. Confirm current requirements and the intended use for your own property.
