Privacy

Privacy matters. So does clarity.

PlotNua is built on trust. Whether you're simply exploring ideas or getting in touch with us, we believe you should always understand what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

This Privacy Policy explains how PlotNua handles personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

1. Who We Are

PlotNua is an independent website operated by Colin MacAulay (registered as Colin Macaulay), who is the data controller for the personal information processed through this website. PlotNua explores new possibilities for residential property in Ireland and helps homeowners discover ideas, information and, where appropriate, independent suppliers.

PlotNua is a business name registered with the Companies Registration Office (Business Name Registration No. 794392) by Colin MacAulay, who runs it as an individual; it is not a company. PlotArt is an initiative of PlotNua. Our full business details are on our Legal information page.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: hello@plotnua.ie

2. Information We Collect

PlotNua currently collects only a limited amount of personal information. When you get in touch, this may include:

  • your name;
  • your email address; and
  • the message you submit through the Contact page.

If you use Your Plot, you can enter an Eircode so that we can find your property and show you the parts of Your Plot that depend on knowing where it is. We do not keep your Eircode, and it is not stored alongside the options you save to My Plot.

If you choose to join the Garden Owner Register, we also collect the information described in Section 7.

If you choose to join the Grower Register, we also collect the information described in Section 7.

If you choose to join the Art Space Register, we also collect the information described in Section 8.

If you choose to join the Artist Register, we also collect the information described in Section 8.

If you apply to the PlotArt Founding 25, we also collect the information described in Section 8.

We do not ask for unnecessary personal information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • respond to your enquiry;
  • communicate with you about your enquiry;
  • find your property when you use Your Plot, and provide the features that depend on its location;
  • show public planning information recorded near a property, where any is found;
  • operate and improve the PlotNua website; and
  • understand how visitors use the website through analytics.

We do not sell personal information.

4. Website Analytics

PlotNua uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the website. Google Analytics may collect information such as:

  • pages visited;
  • approximate location;
  • device type;
  • browser type; and
  • time spent on the website.

This analytics data may involve identifiers such as cookies or similar technologies, and should not be considered fully anonymous. We use it to understand website performance and improve the visitor experience.

Google Analytics will only be activated after a visitor has made an appropriate choice through the website's cookie consent controls, where consent is required. Visitors can withdraw or change their choice through those controls and can also manage cookies through their browser settings.

5. Contact Forms

WhatsApp enquiries

You can also choose to contact PlotNua through WhatsApp Business on +353 83 403 6695. The website link opens WhatsApp; it does not send a message automatically or include your property information. When you send us a message, we receive its contents, any attachments you choose to send, and the contact or profile information WhatsApp makes available to us, which may include your phone number and profile name.

We use this information to respond to your enquiry and handle related follow-up, relying on our legitimate interests in answering enquiries or, where applicable, taking steps at your request before entering into an arrangement. Messaging us does not join you to a register or opt you into marketing. WhatsApp enquiries follow the contact-enquiry retention arrangements in Section 12. You can ask about or request deletion of your information by messaging us or emailing hello@plotnua.ie.

WhatsApp also processes information under its own Privacy Policy. Email and the website contact form remain available if you prefer not to use WhatsApp.

Website forms

When a visitor sends a message through a form on this website — the Contact page, the question form in Your Plot, or the supplier enquiry described in Section 6 — the submission is processed using Formspree before being delivered to hello@plotnua.ie. The information submitted is used to review and respond to the enquiry. Only what is entered in that form is sent: an Eircode and saved My Plot options are not.

Formspree operates under its own privacy terms. Where personal information is transferred outside the European Economic Area, Formspree states that it relies on appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable. Visitors should refer to Formspree's own Privacy Policy for further information about how it processes personal information.

6. Supplier Enquiries

Your Plot can help you send an enquiry to a supplier you have chosen yourself. This only happens if you ask for it. You choose the supplier and the product, you can read and edit the whole message before it is sent, and nothing is sent until you tick the consent box and press send.

What is sent:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • the town or county you enter;
  • the message as you have written it; and
  • the name of the supplier and the product you selected.

What is not sent:

  • your Eircode;
  • your full address;
  • your property's coordinates;
  • the 3D view of your property;
  • the options you have saved to My Plot;
  • anything you compared that supplier against; and
  • any PlotNua ranking or score.

None of these leave your browser with the enquiry.

The enquiry is delivered to PlotNua. Depending on the supplier, it is handled either by Formspree or by PlotNua's own enquiry service, which is hosted on infrastructure provided by Cloudflare and records the enquiry in PlotNua's own records so that we can pass it on, answer you about it, and tell whether it reached the supplier.

What is recorded is the same information listed above — your name, your email address, the town or county you entered, your message, and the supplier and product you chose — together with the date, a reference for the enquiry, and the wording of the consent you agreed to. Nothing from the “what is not sent” list is recorded.

PlotNua then passes the enquiry to the supplier you chose. A person at PlotNua does this; it is not sent automatically. From that point the supplier is independent of PlotNua and may reply to you directly using the name and email address you gave. How that supplier handles your details is governed by its own privacy policy, not by this one, and any conversation you have with it afterwards is between you and them.

While the supplier enquiry service is being piloted, we may contact you afterwards to ask whether the supplier replied and how the enquiry went. You do not have to respond, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Your name, email address and location are not saved to My Plot and are not stored in your browser.

7. The Garden Owner Register and the Grower Register

PlotNua keeps two registers for The Borrowed Garden pilot: the Garden Owner Register, for homeowners who might share part of their garden, and the Grower Register, for people who would like somewhere to grow. They are separate lists with separate forms and separate consent. Joining one does not add you to the other, and nobody on one register can see the other.

The Garden Owner Register (homeowners)

The Borrowed Garden Property Check is private: answering its questions does not send PlotNua your details. After seeing your result, you can choose to join the Garden Owner Register. It is a list, kept privately by PlotNua, of homeowners who might be willing to share part of their garden with someone nearby who would like somewhere to grow. PlotNua is exploring this as a small pilot. Joining is voluntary. Matching is not live, and joining the register does not arrange an introduction.

What we collect when you join:

  • your first name;
  • your email address;
  • the area you choose from a short list;
  • what you tell us about the space: roughly how big it is, whether there is water, and roughly when;
  • anything you choose to add in the note;
  • whether you own the home, rent it or are buying it, carried over from your Property Check answer. We keep this one answer because, if you rent, the owner would have to agree before any introduction could happen; and
  • a record that you gave your consent, which version of this notice applied, and when you joined.

We do not collect your address, your Eircode, your phone number, your surname, your age, photographs of your garden, or anything about your health. Your other Property Check answers and your Property Check result are not sent to the register.

We use this information so that we can tell you if someone nearby is looking for growing space. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you join. You can withdraw it at any time.

Your record is private to PlotNua. It is not published, and there is no public listing, map, profile or directory. Records are reviewed by a person at PlotNua. Your email address would only be shared with another person if you said yes to one specific introduction, and introductions are not yet available.

The form sends your details to a PlotNua service hosted on infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, which records them in the register. The register is stored in Airtable, a database service. Cloudflare and Airtable are both based in the United States, so your information may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Cloudflare states that it relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for such transfers. Airtable's Data Processing Addendum states that the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply to personal data transferred from the European Economic Area. Visitors should refer to Cloudflare's and Airtable's own privacy policies for more about how they process personal information.

We keep your record for a maximum of 24 months. You can ask us to delete it at any time, and we may delete it sooner if it is no longer needed.

To leave the register or ask us to delete your record, email hello@plotnua.ie. There is nothing to cancel and no notice period. When we delete your record we keep a separate note that you gave permission and that we honoured your request. That note holds a one-way digital fingerprint made from your email address, not the address itself, so that we can show we did what you asked.

The Grower Register (growers)

The Grower Register is a list, kept privately by PlotNua, of people who would like somewhere to grow and who might be introduced to a homeowner nearby. PlotNua is exploring this as a small pilot. Joining is voluntary and free. Matching is not live, and joining the register does not arrange an introduction or mean that a garden is available.

The Borrowed Garden Grower Check is private: answering its questions does not send PlotNua your details. If you choose to join the register, your answers fill in the form, where you can see and change them before you submit.

What we collect when you join:

  • your first name;
  • your email address;
  • your county and the area you choose from a short list;
  • how much space you are looking for and roughly when;
  • if you choose to tell us, your town or general area and how far you would travel;
  • anything you choose to add in the note; and
  • a record that you gave your consent, which version of this notice applied, and when you joined.

We do not collect your address, your Eircode, your phone number, your surname, your age, or anything about your health.

We use this information so that we can tell you if someone near you has growing space they would consider sharing. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you join. You can withdraw it at any time.

Your record is private to PlotNua. It is not published, and there is no public listing, map, profile or directory. Records are reviewed by a person at PlotNua. If we think there might be a fit, we will email you first and describe the homeowner in general terms only. Your email address would only be shared with another person if you said yes to one specific introduction, and introductions are not yet available.

Grower Register records are kept in the same database as the Garden Owner Register, in a separate table, and are sent and stored by the same service providers, in the same way, as described above.

We keep your record for a maximum of 24 months. You can ask us to delete it at any time, and we may delete it sooner if it is no longer needed.

To leave the register or ask us to delete your record, email hello@plotnua.ie. There is nothing to cancel and no notice period. When we delete your record we keep a separate note that you gave permission and that we honoured your request. That note holds a one-way digital fingerprint made from your email address, not the address itself, so that we can show we did what you asked.

8. The Art Space Register and the Artist Register

PlotNua keeps two separate registers for the A Home for Art idea: the Art Space Register, for homeowners who might offer space for artists' finished work, and the Artist Register, for artists who might be interested in that kind of space. They are separate lists with separate forms and separate consent. Joining one does not add you to the other, and neither register is shared with the people on the other.

The Art Space Register (homeowners)

The Open Your Home to Art Property Check is private: answering its questions does not send PlotNua your details. After seeing your result, you can choose to join the Art Space Register. It is a list, kept privately by PlotNua, of homeowners in Ireland who might be willing to offer space for artists' finished work: storing it, displaying it, opening a space to visitors by arrangement, or helping it find a buyer. Joining is voluntary. PlotNua is not introducing homeowners to artists, there is no matching or booking service, no payment is offered, and joining the register does not arrange anything.

This register is separate from the Garden Owner Register described in Section 7. It is kept in a separate database, and your details are not shared between the two.

What we collect when you join:

  • your first name;
  • your email address;
  • your county;
  • which of storing, displaying, opening to visitors or helping to sell you would consider;
  • if you choose to tell us: the kind of space, whether money is part of it for you, and anything you add in the note; and
  • a record that you gave your consent, which version of this notice applied, and when you joined.

We do not collect your address, your Eircode, your town, your phone number, your surname, your age, photographs of your home, or anything about the value of what is in it. The form starts with three of your Property Check answers filled in, and only what is in the form when you submit it is sent. Your other Property Check answers and your Property Check result are not sent to the register.

We use this information to contact you about the Open Your Home to Art pilot, for example to ask a few questions about what you would consider, or to let you know if the pilot develops. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you join. You can withdraw it at any time.

Your record is private to PlotNua. It is not published, and there is no public listing, map, profile or directory. Records are reviewed by a person at PlotNua. Registration details are accessible only to authorised people managing the register and to service providers where necessary to operate it. They are not shared with artists or made publicly available. Your email address would only be shared with another person if you agreed to a specific arrangement, and no such arrangements are available.

The form sends your details to a PlotNua service hosted on infrastructure provided by Cloudflare, which records them in the register. The register is stored in Airtable, a database service. Cloudflare and Airtable are both based in the United States, so your information may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Cloudflare states that it relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and on its certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for such transfers. Airtable's Data Processing Addendum states that the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply to personal data transferred from the European Economic Area. Visitors should refer to Cloudflare's and Airtable's own privacy policies for more about how they process personal information.

The services that carry the form, such as Cloudflare, process technical information such as your IP address so that your request can be delivered securely. PlotNua does not record it in the register.

We keep your record for no longer than 12 months, and we delete it sooner if the pilot ends or no longer needs it. You can ask us to delete it at any time. This is how long PlotNua keeps your record; the recovery copies kept by our database provider are described below.

To leave the register or ask us to delete your record, email hello@plotnua.ie. There is nothing to cancel and no notice period. When we delete your record, we delete everything the register holds about you, including the record of your consent. A deleted record can be restored from our database provider's trash for 7 days. Separately, the provider keeps its own automatic recovery copies of the database for up to 1 year, and these may still contain a deleted record until they expire. Those copies are managed by the provider, not used by PlotNua, and we never restore deleted registration records from them. We keep only an anonymous entry in our deletion log, recording when a deletion was requested or became due, when it was completed and why, so that we can show deletions are carried out. That entry contains nothing that identifies you.

The Artist Register (artists)

The Artist Register is a list, kept privately by PlotNua, of artists who might be interested in displaying, showing, installing or storing their work in homes and gardens in Ireland, or in reaching potential buyers through a home setting. Artists can join through PlotArt's Artist Register page while registrations are open. Joining is voluntary and free, and it does not commit you to anything.

PlotArt is in development. There is no matching service at present. PlotNua is not introducing artists to homeowners, there is no exhibition, booking or sales service, no payment is offered, and registering does not guarantee an opportunity of any kind.

What we collect when you join:

  • the name you work under;
  • your email address;
  • the county where you are based, or that you are based outside Ireland;
  • where you would consider opportunities and, if you choose to tell us, which counties or regions;
  • what you make and which opportunities interest you;
  • if you choose to tell us: the space your work needs, a link to your website or portfolio, and a short statement about your work; and
  • a record that you gave your consent, which version of this notice applied, and when you joined.

We do not collect your home address, your phone number, your date of birth, photographs of your work, prices or details of your sales.

We use this information to contact you about the development of PlotArt and about potential opportunities to connect with homeowners, for example to ask what would be useful to you or to let you know if the idea develops. We rely on your consent, which you give by ticking the box when you join.

Your registration is stored privately. It is not published: there is no public artist profile, listing, map or directory. Registration details are accessible only to authorised people managing the register and to service providers where necessary to operate it. Your details are not shared with homeowners, automatically or otherwise. If a possible opportunity ever arose, we would contact you first, and your details would only be shared with a homeowner if you agreed to that specific arrangement. No such arrangements are available at present.

Artist registrations are kept in the same database as the Art Space Register but in a separate table, and are sent and stored by the same service providers, in the same way, as described above for the Art Space Register.

We keep your registration for no longer than 12 months, and we delete it sooner if it is no longer needed.

You can withdraw your consent, or ask us to delete your registration, at any time by emailing hello@plotnua.ie. There is nothing to cancel and no notice period. When we delete your registration, we delete everything the Artist Register holds about you, including the record of your consent, and the same arrangements for our database provider's trash and recovery copies, and for our anonymous deletion log, apply as described above for the Art Space Register.

PlotArt Founding 25 applications (artists)

PlotArt, an initiative of PlotNua, is inviting artists based or working on the island of Ireland to apply to become one of up to 25 Founding Artists, the first artists in the PlotArt Virtual Gallery. Applications are reviewed individually, and places are awarded through a curated selection, not on a first-come, first-served basis. Applying is separate from the Artist Register: it does not add you to the Artist Register, and being on the Artist Register does not count as an application.

What we collect when you apply:

  • the name you work under, your email address and your county;
  • your primary medium and a short biography you write, and images of your work that you upload (up to five), a link to your portfolio, or both;
  • if you choose to give them, your website, an Instagram handle or professional profile link, and anything else you tell us;
  • your confirmations that you are 18 or over and based or working on the island of Ireland, that the work is your own or that you are authorised to represent the artist, and that you have read this notice; and
  • which version of that wording and of this notice applied, and when you applied.

The details marked as required are needed for us to consider your application. If you don't provide them, we can't review it. The optional details are up to you.

We do not ask for your home address, your phone number or your date of birth when you apply. Please don't include health or other sensitive personal details in your biography.

If you upload images of your work, your browser first makes a smaller copy of each one, which also removes hidden file information such as camera and location data. The images are stored privately in the European Union by Cloudflare, separately from anything published in PlotArt galleries. We use them only to consider your application: they are never published, and you can ask us to delete them at any time. Images you remove before you apply, or that are not sent with an application, are deleted automatically within a day. We keep the images sent with an application for as long as we keep the application, as described below, and delete them with it.

We use this information to review your application and to contact you about it. We do this because you have asked us to consider your application, as a step before any agreement is made. We keep a record of each decision and of the wording you confirmed because we have a legitimate interest in running a fair and accountable selection, and we use simple technical checks, such as a hidden form field and limits on repeated submissions, to keep the form free of spam, which is also in our legitimate interests. These checks do not use cookies. Every application is read and decided by a person. We do not use automated decision-making or profiling to select artists.

Applying is only a request to be considered. It does not make you a member of PlotArt or give you a place. If you are selected, accepting a Founding Artist place happens separately, under the Founding Artist terms, and you have 30 days to accept an offer. Nothing about you or your work is published unless and until you approve your artist gallery. Applying does not give us permission to publish anything.

Your application is stored privately and is not published. It is accessible only to the person at PlotNua who reviews applications, and to our service providers where necessary to operate the form. Applications are kept in the same database as the Artist Register but in a separate table, and are sent and stored by the same service providers, in the same way, as described above for the Art Space Register. We reply to you by email from hello@plotnua.ie, which runs on Google Workspace. Google may process this information outside the European Economic Area; where it does, it relies on the safeguards in its data processing terms, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep your application: if it is not selected, is withdrawn, or a place offered is not taken up, for 12 months after that decision; if it has not been decided, for no more than 12 months after you applied. If you become a Founding Artist, we keep your application for as long as you take part and for 12 months afterwards, and any record we are required by law to keep is kept only for as long as the law requires.

If you agree to join our waiting list, we keep your application on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by emailing hello@plotnua.ie. We will ask you again before keeping it on the waiting list for longer than 12 months.

If you become a Founding Artist, we will also use your contact details to manage your Founding Artist agreement and to prepare your gallery with you. Anything published about you or your work is published only with your approval, under the separate gallery terms, and this notice will be updated before any artist gallery is published.

You can withdraw your application, correct it, or ask us to delete it at any time by emailing hello@plotnua.ie. When we delete an application, we delete everything we hold about it, including the record of the wording you confirmed, and the same arrangements for our database provider's trash and recovery copies, and for our anonymous deletion log, apply as described above for the Art Space Register. You also have the other rights described in Section 14, including the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interests.

PlotArt artist galleries and PlotArt Studio (artists)

If you become a PlotArt artist, you manage your gallery through PlotArt Studio, a private account you sign in to with a one-time code sent to your email address.

What we hold for your gallery:

  • your name and the email address you sign in with, and a record of your sign-ins, which our sign-in provider keeps for security;
  • your biography and statement, your portrait if you add one, your county if you choose to show it, and links to your website or Instagram if you add them;
  • your artworks' details and photographs, who took each photograph, and your declaration of any meaningful use of AI in making a work; and
  • the version and date of the gallery licence you accepted, and a record of the changes you and we make to your gallery and of our review decisions.

We use this information to run your gallery under your artist agreement. We show your county and your portrait only if you choose to, on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in PlotArt Studio or by emailing hello@plotnua.ie. We keep sign-in and change records, and a record of what was published and when, because we have a legitimate interest in keeping accounts and the gallery secure and accountable.

Only what you approve is published, as it appears in your gallery. Before a photograph is published, hidden file information, including camera and location data, is removed. Your email address is never published.

PlotArt Studio runs on infrastructure provided by Cloudflare. Your photographs are stored in the European Union. Published gallery pages are served from the same hosting as the rest of this website.

We keep your account, your gallery records and your original photographs while you take part and for 12 months afterwards. Photographs you remove are deleted within 30 days. We keep a record of what was published and when, without images, for the records of the founding programme and of enquiries.

Enquiries to PlotArt artists (buyers)

If you send an enquiry about an artwork or an artist through a PlotArt gallery, we collect your name, your email address and your message, together with the artwork and the time you sent it.

We pass your name, email address and message to the artist so that they can reply to you directly, and we send you a short confirmation. We do this to deal with your request, as a step you have asked us to take, and because we have a legitimate interest in keeping a record of the enquiries PlotArt introduces to artists. The artist then handles your enquiry and any sale directly with you; PlotArt does not take payment or arrange delivery.

A simple hidden form field and limits on repeated enquiries keep the form free of spam. They do not use cookies, and we do not store your IP address.

We keep the record of each enquiry for 24 months and then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner by emailing hello@plotnua.ie; the artist you contacted keeps any email you exchange with them under their own responsibility.
