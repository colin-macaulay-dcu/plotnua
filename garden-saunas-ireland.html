Garden Saunas in Ireland

How garden saunas are sold

Three ways to buy a garden sauna.

The route decides what the price covers and who does the work in your garden. Some makers offer more than one.

As a kit

Delivered flat-packed to assemble yourself or with paid help. Gardenhouse24, OGNYX, Pineca, Polaris and Summerhouse24 sell saunas this way. On the models checked, Gardenhouse24, Pineca, Polaris and Summerhouse24 list the heater as an extra, so check it is in any price you compare.

Made to order

Built for you and delivered ready to place. Irish Sauna Company makes each model to order in Connemara, Co. Galway, and delivers it assembled. Arctic Wellness makes its prebuilt models to order in Dublin. The Mobile Pods configures saunas to order and prices each one on enquiry.

Supplied and installed

The maker puts it in place. Arctic Wellness supplies and installs its saunas with its own team. Polaris offers assembly by its team, and Gardenhouse24 and Summerhouse24 offer paid installation on the models checked. Irish Sauna Company and Polaris say the electrical connection is made by your own electrician.

Built around your space

Bespoke and custom-built garden saunas.

If a standard model doesn’t suit the space, three makers in this guide work to order.

Arctic Wellness

Designs custom saunas around a house or garden and builds them in place with its own team, or adapts one of its existing designs. Custom builds are quoted per project.

Irish Sauna Company

Handcrafts each of its garden saunas to order in Connemara, Co. Galway, with Harvia electric heaters, and delivers them assembled. Its models carry published prices.

The Mobile Pods

Configures saunas to order — electric or wood-burning stove, lighting, finishes — and also builds to custom designs. Each is priced on enquiry.

Before you choose a model

What to settle first.

The heater

Electric or wood-fired changes what your garden needs: an electric heater has to be connected by a Registered Electrical Contractor, and a wood-fired stove needs a flue. Check the heater is in the price — on the models checked it is included by Irish Sauna Company and an extra elsewhere.

The base

A sauna needs a level, solid, well-drained base. Most makers price foundations separately or leave them to you; Arctic Wellness says its team prepares the base before it installs.

Getting it in

Larger saunas come by lorry, often with a crane to lift them. Check gates, overhead cables and where it can unload. Irish Sauna Company, Polaris and Summerhouse24 each set out their access requirements.

Outside and inside sizes

Compare the external size with your garden, and the bench length with the people who will use it. Makers measure differently: some give a footprint, others a diameter and a length.

Planning

Garden saunas and planning permission.

A sauna cabin is a structure you put up, so the rules on development apply. It can only be built without planning permission if it fits Class 3 of the exempted-development rules for houses, which covers a “greenhouse, garage, store, shed or other similar structure”, and meets every one of its conditions:

  • nothing in front of the front wall of the house;
  • no more than 30 m² in total, counted together with every other such structure already in the garden (the limit was 25 m² until 27 July 2026);
  • at least 25 m² of private open space left to the rear or side of the house;
  • no higher than 4 m with a tiled or slated pitched roof, or 3 m otherwise;
  • external finishes matching the house if it stands to the side;
  • not used for human habitation, or for any purpose other than one incidental to the enjoyment of the house.

Saunas are not named in Class 3. Whether a sauna cabin counts as an “other similar structure” is not settled: PlotNua found no Irish decision on the point. The exemption also does not apply in the circumstances listed in article 9 of the 2001 Regulations — for example, where the work would breach a condition of an existing planning permission. For a formal determination on whether your sauna would be exempt, ask your local planning authority (your city or county council) for a declaration on exempted development.

Reading the prices

What a published price covers.

Product, not project

These are the makers’ prices for the sauna, not a finished-project cost. The base, electrical connection, delivery and installation may be extra; the comparison shows what each maker says about them.

VAT

Irish Sauna Company and Pineca state that their prices include VAT. Arctic Wellness, Gardenhouse24, OGNYX and Polaris do not say, and Summerhouse24’s product page did not show it when checked.

Prices that change

Irish Sauna Company’s prices here are introductory and rise on 30 November 2026. A time-limited promotion on one Pineca model was not used. Confirm the current price with the maker.

No average, no ranking

PlotNua does not average these prices or rank the makers. Sizes, heaters and what is included differ too much for one figure to mean anything.
