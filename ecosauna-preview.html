04 Before anything goes live

Three things we’d like to check with you.

01

Prices and VAT

Your configurator starts the Barrel Sauna at €3,300, the Hot Tub at €2,300 and the Plunge Pool at €1,100. Some of your Ireland pages give €2,800, €3,100 and €1,950. Which should a homeowner see, and do your prices include VAT?

02

Delivery and timing

Your configurator prices delivery by location and confirms it on contact, and your county pages say a custom order usually arrives within 4 to 6 weeks. Is there a typical delivery range we could show, and does that timescale hold across the range?

03

Installation and cover

Installation is a priced option on the Barrel and Cube Saunas. Is it also offered for the Hot Tub, Plunge Pool and Phytosauna? And what would you like a homeowner to know about the warranty on each?
