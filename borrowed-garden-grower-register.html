The Borrowed Garden · Grower Register

Somewhere to grow, closer than you think.

Would you like a little ground to grow on?

Some homeowners have a corner of garden they don’t use. PlotNua is exploring a small pilot that would introduce them to people nearby who’d like somewhere to grow.

If that’s you, whether you’re a complete beginner or have grown for years, tell us. It’s free, private and doesn’t commit you to anything.

How It Would Work

Two registers, one careful introduction.

  1. Homeowners with a spare corner tell PlotNua on the Garden Owner Register.
  2. Growers tell PlotNua what they’re looking for, here.
  3. If we think there might be a fit, we email each of you separately and ask. We describe the other person only in general terms: an area, how much space, roughly when.
  4. Only if you both say yes do we share first names and email addresses. You meet at the garden, in daylight, and decide together.
Where Things Stand

Honest about where we are.

  • No garden is available through PlotNua today. Matching isn’t live yet. Joining the register doesn’t arrange anything.
  • We’re starting small, in Raheny, Killester, Donnycarney and Artane. If you live elsewhere you’re welcome to join, but we aren’t looking for anyone to introduce outside those areas yet.
  • We can’t promise anything. We may never find a garden near you.
  • You stay in control. Nothing is shared with anybody until you say yes to one specific introduction.

Hoping to grow sooner? Community gardens, garden-sharing services and council allotments may have space now. See who’s already helping people find ground.

Before you join — what we keep, and what we never ask for

What we keep. Your first name, your email address, the area you chose, how far you’d travel, how much space you’re looking for, roughly when, and anything you choose to tell us in the notes box. We also keep the administrative records needed to manage your registration, your consent and its status.

What we never ask for. Your address, your Eircode, your phone number, your surname, your age, or anything about your health. Your answers to the six quick questions above are not sent.

Why we keep it. So that we can tell you if someone near you has growing space they’d consider sharing.

Joining doesn’t share your details with anyone. No other person sees your record. There is no public listing, no map, no profile page and no directory.

If we think there’s a possible fit, we’ll email you and ask. We’ll describe the homeowner in general terms only. Your email address only reaches another person after you have said yes to that specific introduction. If you don’t reply, nothing happens.

What “registered” means. Only that you asked PlotNua to tell you about potential growing space. It doesn’t mean anyone has checked or approved anything.

Leaving. Email hello@plotnua.ie and we will delete your record. There’s nothing to cancel and no notice period.

How long we keep it. A maximum of 24 months. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

One thing we keep after deletion. A separate note that you gave permission and that we honoured your request, holding a one-way digital fingerprint made from your email address, not the address itself.

The full details are in our privacy notice.

This is a register, not a match.

If an Introduction Ever Happens

PlotNua introduces people. That is all it does.

PlotNua doesn’t verify anybody’s identity, hasn’t Garda vetted anyone, hasn’t checked references and doesn’t provide insurance. PlotNua doesn’t handle money or take a fee.

Meeting for the first time
  • Meet at the garden, in daylight, before anything is agreed.
  • Tell somebody else where you’re going and when you expect to be back. Bring somebody with you if you’d prefer.
  • Agree how you’ll get in, which tools you’ll use and where they’re kept. A key isn’t needed for this to work.
  • Either of you can stop at any time, for any reason or none.
  • If something feels wrong, email hello@plotnua.ie. If you are in danger, contact An Garda Síochána on 999 or 112.
