# PlotArt Payment Pilot Addendum — PA-PAY-2026-11 (DRAFT r7, 17 November 2026)

**Status: unissued draft for founder and professional review.** It has not been accepted by any artist and is not installed in PlotArt Studio. It may be offered to artists only after it has been approved, issued as fingerprinted HTML, and installed by an approved migration. Bracketed text marks drafting alternatives or details to be completed before issue. Review notes, founder decisions and evidence are kept in `PA-PAY-PA-BPT-r7-REVIEW-NOTES.md`, not in this text.

## 0. How this addendum relates to the artist's agreement

0.1 This addendum is optional. It applies only to orders paid through the PlotArt payment pilot. It sits beside the artist's PlotArt agreement (PA-FAA-2026-11 Founding Artist Agreement or PA-GAA-2026-11 Gallery Artist Agreement) and the Gallery Publication and Image Licence PA-GPIL-2026-11.

0.2 **Nothing in this addendum reduces any right the artist has under their agreement.** In particular:
  (a) the protections of a Founding Artist are unaffected. Every Founding Enquiry stays commission-free permanently, and commission can arise only under separate commission terms that the artist has expressly accepted (PA-FAA cl. 3, 5 and 6). This addendum is not such terms. Orders that carry no PlotArt commission, including every Founding Enquiry, are not taken through the pilot (clause 1.3), so no pilot charge or fee of any kind applies to them;
  (b) for a Gallery Artist, the commission rate, Commission Base and refund rules remain those of PA-GAA cl. 5 and 9, including that the artist keeps at least 85% of the Commission Base. On a pilot order, the artist's combined deduction for PlotArt commission and Stripe's payment processing is never more than that commission (clause 3.3);
  (c) if any part of this addendum could be read as reducing such a right, the artist's agreement prevails on that point.

0.3 This addendum adds pilot conditions that apply only to pilot orders. These cover the quote process, transit insurance, how payment is taken, and how commission on a pilot order is collected and returned. By accepting this addendum, the artist agrees to them for those orders only.

## 1. What the pilot is

1.1 For a sale arising from an enquiry PlotArt has introduced, the artist may let the buyer pay by card through a Stripe-hosted checkout. This happens after the artist and PlotArt have agreed the artwork, its price, its delivery and its return terms in a quote.

1.2 Taking part is optional. Direct enquiries and direct sales continue as before. The artist can leave the pilot at any time in PlotArt Studio. Unpaid quotes are then cancelled, and orders already paid are completed under this addendum.

1.3 The pilot covers one existing original artwork by one artist per order, in euro, where the sale carries PlotArt commission under the artist's agreement. It does not cover:
  (a) sales that carry no PlotArt commission, including every sale arising from a Founding Enquiry. These continue as direct sales between the artist and the buyer;
  (b) prints, commissions of new work, hire, multi-artwork orders, discounts, gift cards or instalments.

1.4 During the pilot, PlotArt quotes artworks priced up to EUR 9,999.99. It will not quote a purchase that would bring a buyer's apparently linked purchases through the pilot to EUR 10,000 or more in twelve months. Linked purchases are those by the same buyer, or by anyone PlotArt knows to be acting with them. These are limits on the scope of the pilot, not a statement about anyone's legal obligations.

## 2. The artist is the seller

2.1 The artist is the seller of the artwork and contracts directly with the buyer. PlotArt introduces the buyer and coordinates the sale through its platform. It is not the seller and does not take ownership or possession of the artwork.

2.2 **Seller information.** Before a quote is issued, the artist gives PlotArt, and keeps up to date, the information a buyer must have before paying:
  (a) legal name and any trading name;
  (b) the geographical address at which the artist is established, and any different address for returns and complaints;
  (c) an email address and a telephone number at which the buyer can contact the artist;
  (d) whether the artist sells as a trader (in the course of their business, craft or profession);
  (e) VAT registration and number, if registered.
The artist enters this information in PlotArt Studio and confirms that it is accurate. A quote cannot be approved or issued without it. PlotArt shows it on the buyer's quote and in the buyer's purchase confirmation. Each issued quote keeps its own copy; if the artist changes the information after approving a quote, that quote must be approved again before it is issued.
[Pilot eligibility, founder decision F2: the pilot is open only to artists who declare that they sell as traders. Alternative: also allow artists who declare they do not, with PA-BPT 1.2 telling buyers that consumer cancellation rights do not apply.]

2.3 Payment is made to the artist's own Stripe account, which the artist opens through Stripe's hosted onboarding. Stripe's own terms, including the Stripe Connected Account Agreement, apply between the artist and Stripe. Identity documents and bank details are given by the artist directly to Stripe, not to PlotArt. What PlotArt can see is described in clause 9.

2.4 The artist authorises PlotArt, for each order the artist has approved:
  (a) to create a Stripe-hosted checkout on the artist's Stripe account, for exactly the approved amounts;
  (b) to receive PlotArt's commission, if any, from that payment as a Stripe application fee;
  (c) to issue refunds from that payment on the artist's instruction or with the artist's agreement (clause 7).

2.5 The artist authorises PlotArt to receive, on the artist's behalf, a buyer's cancellation notice, complaint or damage report sent to hello@plotnua.ie. PlotArt passes it on to the artist promptly, and the artist deals with it.

## 3. Commission, processing fees and tax

3.1 **PlotArt commission** is exactly what the artist's agreement provides:
  (a) none on a sale arising from a Founding Enquiry (PA-FAA cl. 5);
  (b) for a Founding Artist, none unless separate commission terms that the artist has expressly accepted were in effect when the enquiry was introduced (PA-FAA cl. 6);
  (c) for a Gallery Artist, the rate and Commission Base in PA-GAA cl. 5.

Commission is never charged on delivery, packing, transport, insurance, installation or import costs.

3.2 **How commission is collected on a pilot order.** Instead of invoicing under PA-GAA cl. 8, PlotArt receives its commission from the buyer's payment as a Stripe application fee, for the amount shown on the quote that the artist approved. Commission is recorded at its full amount; the processing reimbursement in clause 3.3 is recorded separately and does not reduce the commission. PlotArt gives the artist a statement for each pilot order (clause 3.8) [and, where the law requires, an invoice for the commission]. Commission on sales outside the pilot continues to be invoiced as before.

3.3 **Stripe processing fees: PlotArt reimburses them.** Stripe charges a processing fee on each card payment, on the whole amount the buyer pays, including delivery. Stripe deducts it, and any VAT Stripe charges on it, from the balance of the artist's Stripe account. On every pilot order:
  (a) PlotArt reimburses the artist the actual processing fee Stripe charged on that payment, and the VAT Stripe charged on that fee, if any;
  (b) the amounts are those Stripe records for the payment, not estimates. PlotArt reimburses them after Stripe has recorded them, normally within minutes of the payment, and in any case within [7] days [provisional period, awaiting founder approval];
  (c) the reimbursement is paid into the artist's Stripe balance by returning part of PlotArt's application fee;
  (d) as a result, the artist's combined deduction for PlotArt commission and Stripe's processing of the payment is no more than the commission.

3.4 **What the reimbursement does not cover.** It covers Stripe's processing fee on the buyer's payment and the VAT Stripe charges on that fee, and nothing else. In particular, it does not cover:
  (a) Stripe's dispute fees (clause 7.8);
  (b) fees for optional Stripe services or features the artist uses on their account, and currency-conversion or payout fees;
  (c) any VAT the artist accounts for themselves under the reverse charge (clause 3.6(b));
  (d) a negative Stripe balance, or anything Stripe recovers from the artist under its own terms.
Where Stripe charges such a fee on the same payment, the artist's statement shows it separately as not reimbursed.

3.5 **If the commission is not enough.** The reimbursement and any commission returned on a refund (clause 7.7) are both paid out of the application fee for that order, so together they cannot exceed it. Where what PlotArt owes the artist under clause 3.3 or 7.7 is more than what is left of the application fee, PlotArt pays the difference to the artist directly, by bank transfer, within [30] days of it arising [provisional period, awaiting founder approval], and records the payment. To make that payment, PlotArt asks the artist for their bank details by telephone, never by email, and enters them only in its business online banking. They are not recorded in PlotArt Studio, the payment pilot, PlotArt's application logs or its ordinary correspondence. PlotArt's bank keeps them as part of the payee entry, unless PlotArt deletes it, and in its record of the transfer, which forms part of PlotArt's accounting records (clause 9.8). This can happen, for example, when the buyer is refunded in full (example 3 in clause 3.7), or when a small artwork has a large delivery charge and is paid with an international card. The artist's statement shows any amount PlotArt still owes.

3.6 **VAT on Stripe's fees** is a matter between Stripe and the artist, and depends on the artist:
  (a) for Stripe account holders based in Ireland, Stripe states that VAT applies to all its fees at the standard rate, added on top of the fees, and Stripe issues a monthly tax invoice. PlotArt reimburses the VAT Stripe charges on the processing fee for a pilot payment;
  (b) for Stripe account holders elsewhere in Europe, Stripe states that it supplies its services from Ireland without charging VAT on its fees, and the account holder may need to account for VAT under the reverse charge. Stripe charges no VAT on the fee, so there is no VAT for PlotArt to reimburse;
  (c) whether any VAT on Stripe's fees is a final cost depends on the artist's own VAT position. PlotArt does not give tax advice.

3.7 **Examples** (illustration only). A Gallery Artist on 15% commission sells a EUR 1,000 artwork, plus EUR 75 delivery and insurance; the buyer pays EUR 1,075. The fees use Stripe's published standard pricing for EEA businesses at the time of drafting (standard EEA cards 1.5% + €0.25; premium EEA cards 1.9% + €0.25; UK cards 2.5% + €0.25; international cards 3.25% + €0.25), which will be re-checked when this addendum is issued. VAT on Stripe's fee is shown for a Stripe account based in Ireland (clause 3.6).

Example 1: the sale completes, paid with a standard EEA card.

| | |
|---|---|
| Buyer pays | €1,075.00 |
| of which artwork / delivery | €1,000.00 / €75.00 |
| PlotArt commission (15% of the artwork only) | − €150.00 |
| Stripe processing fee on €1,075 | − €16.38 |
| VAT charged by Stripe on that fee | − €3.77 |
| Reimbursed by PlotArt (fee and VAT) | + €20.15 |
| **Artist's net proceeds** | **€925.00** |
| Artist's combined deduction for commission and processing | €150.00, the commission |

With other cards, the fee and VAT reimbursed would be (fee + VAT): premium EEA €20.68 + €4.76; UK €27.13 + €6.24; international €35.19 + €8.09. The artist's net proceeds stay €925.00. For a Stripe account outside Ireland, Stripe charges no VAT on the fee, so PlotArt reimburses €16.38 and the net proceeds are again €925.00.

Example 2: as example 1, then the artist agrees with the buyer to refund €500 of the price. The buyer keeps the artwork (one original); this is a partial refund of its price, not a return of part of it.

| | |
|---|---|
| Net proceeds before the refund | €925.00 |
| Refunded to the buyer | − €500.00 |
| Commission returned by PlotArt (15% of €500) | + €75.00 |
| **Artist's net proceeds** | **€500.00** |
| Combined deduction for commission and processing | €75.00, the commission on the artwork the buyer kept |

Example 3: as example 1, then the buyer cancels and is refunded €1,075 in full.

| | |
|---|---|
| Net proceeds before the refund | €925.00 |
| Refunded to the buyer | − €1,075.00 |
| Commission returned through Stripe: €150.00 less the €20.15 already returned as the reimbursement | + €129.85 |
| Paid by PlotArt directly (clause 3.5) | + €20.15 |
| **Artist's net proceeds** | **€0.00** |

Stripe does not return its processing fee when a payment is refunded; under this addendum PlotArt bears it. The return cost the buyer pays on cancellation (clause 7.2) is not shown.

Example 4: a EUR 100 artwork with EUR 400 delivery, paid with an international card. Commission is €15.00. Stripe's fee on €500 is €16.50, with €3.80 VAT: €20.30 in all. PlotArt returns its €15.00 application fee in full and pays the remaining €5.30 directly (clause 3.5). The artist's net proceeds are €485.00.

3.8 **The artist's statement.** For each pilot order, PlotArt Studio shows the artist the following as separate lines:
  (a) the gross payment, and the artwork and delivery amounts within it;
  (b) PlotArt's commission, and commission returned;
  (c) Stripe's processing fee, the VAT Stripe charged on it, and the reimbursement;
  (d) any amount PlotArt has paid the artist directly;
  (e) other Stripe fees on the payment that are not reimbursed;
  (f) refunds to the buyer, disputes, and Stripe's dispute fees and any of them Stripe returns, as Stripe records them for the dispute;
  (g) the artist's net proceeds.
Amounts PlotArt owes the artist but has not yet paid are shown separately, with their due date, and are not included in net proceeds until paid. If Stripe has not supplied the fee for a dispute, the statement says so and does not include an estimate. Payout fees are not shown.

3.9 The artist remains responsible for any VAT or other tax on the sale of the artwork, and for any invoice or receipt the law requires the artist to give the buyer.

## 4. Quotes, approval and holding the work

4.1 PlotArt prepares each quote. It shows:
  (a) the seller information in clause 2.2;
  (b) the artwork and its price;
  (c) every delivery charge;
  (d) the delivery, insurance and installation arrangements and the delivery window;
  (e) the return arrangements in clause 7.2, including the estimated cost of returning the artwork if the buyer cancels;
  (f) the commission and the basis for it;
  (g) the date and time until which the quote can be paid.

4.2 Before any buyer can pay, the artist:
  (a) confirms that the work is available and not promised elsewhere;
  (b) confirms its location, condition, framing and glazing, dimensions and weight;
  (c) confirms the packing, transport, insurance and return arrangements shown;
  (d) approves that exact quote version.
Any later change creates a new version, which the artist must approve again.

4.3 **Holding the work.**
  (a) PlotArt issues only one open quote for a work at a time. It does not issue a second quote for the same work until the first has been paid, cancelled or has expired;
  (b) while a quote is open, the artist will not sell or promise the work elsewhere, and will record any outside sale in PlotArt Studio straight away. PlotArt then cancels the quote, and the buyer cannot pay;
  (c) once the buyer opens Stripe checkout, PlotArt's systems also block the work from any other sale for the checkout period, currently 31 minutes, plus 15 minutes for Stripe's confirmation to arrive;
  (d) a quote can be paid for up to 7 days, as shown on it.

## 5. Payment and payout

5.1 An order is complete only when Stripe confirms the payment to PlotArt directly. PlotArt then marks the work sold in PlotArt Studio, where the artist can see the order.

5.2 Payouts from the artist's Stripe balance to their bank follow the artist's Stripe payout schedule. PlotArt does not hold the money, does not delay payouts until delivery and does not offer escrow.

## 6. Delivery and insurance

6.1 Each quote names who:
  (a) packs;
  (b) books and contracts with the transporter;
  (c) pays for transport;
  (d) arranges installation;
  (e) bears any import costs.
It also gives the transporter's confirmation for that shipment.

6.2 **Every shipment in the pilot must have transit insurance confirmed for that shipment.** The cover must be for at least the artwork price, from collection to signed delivery. The quote names:
  (a) the insurer and who holds the cover;
  (b) the covered value and the valuation basis;
  (c) any excess and the main exclusions;
  (d) the period of cover and the claims contact;
  (e) a confirmation reference.
The carrier's limited liability alone is not accepted. The insurance confirmation is recorded in PlotArt Studio before dispatch.

6.3 The artist dispatches as quoted and records the dispatch and delivery evidence in PlotArt Studio: tracking, a signed receipt and condition photographs.

6.4 The buyer's rights do not depend on any carrier or insurance claim being made or completed.

## 7. Cancellations, returns, refunds and disputes

7.1 As seller, the artist is responsible to the buyer for cancellations, returns and refunds under consumer law, and for the artwork conforming to the contract. The buyer's rights are set out in the Buyer Purchase Terms PA-BPT-2026-11. The artist will honour them.

7.2 **Return information on each quote.** For each order, the artist gives PlotArt, and PlotArt shows on the quote:
  (a) how the artwork would be returned if the buyer cancels;
  (b) the address it is returned to;
  (c) the cost of returning it by that method, or a reasonable estimate of the maximum cost where it cannot be calculated in advance. Most original artworks cannot normally be returned by post, so this figure is required on every quote;
  (d) who arranges the return.
If the buyer cancels for a change of mind and this information was given, the buyer bears the stated return cost. If the artwork is damaged, faulty or not as described, the artist bears the return cost.

7.3 **Refund timing.** When a buyer cancels within the cancellation period, the artist refunds the price and the delivery charge shown on the quote within 14 days of being told of the cancellation. Where the artist has not offered to collect the artwork, the artist may withhold the refund until it has received the artwork back, or until the buyer has supplied evidence of having sent it back, whichever is earlier. The artist may deduct an amount for any loss in value caused by handling beyond what was needed to establish the artwork's nature and characteristics, where the law allows.

7.4 **How refunds are made.** Refunds are made to the buyer's card through Stripe, from the artist's Stripe balance. PlotArt makes a refund from the order on the artist's instruction or with the artist's agreement. Stripe does not return its processing fee on the original payment when a payment is refunded. PlotArt's reimbursement of that fee (clause 3.3) is not reversed.

7.5 **If the artist's Stripe balance is too low.** Depending on the balance, Stripe may:
  (a) complete the refund at once by drawing on payments not yet available to the artist, which can leave the artist's Stripe balance negative; or
  (b) hold the refund as pending until the balance can cover it, debit the artist's bank account where Stripe's terms allow, and cancel the refund if it is still pending after 45 days.
[To be confirmed before issue from the pilot's unfunded-refund test.]
A pending refund has not been completed: the buyer has not been repaid until Stripe completes it. PlotArt tells the artist when it becomes aware that a refund is pending [and tells the buyer, once buyer notifications are in place]. The artist funds the refund in one of these ways:
  (i) let Stripe complete it from new sales or by debiting the linked bank account;
  (ii) add funds to their Stripe balance;
  (iii) only after Stripe has cancelled the refund, repay the buyer directly and send PlotArt the confirmation.
The artist must not repay the buyer outside Stripe while a Stripe refund is still pending, because the buyer would then be repaid twice. The artist remains responsible for the buyer being repaid within the legal time limit (clause 7.3), so should fund a pending refund promptly.

7.6 **A refund Stripe reverses after confirming it.** Rarely, a refund that Stripe first confirmed fails later, for example because the buyer's card was closed. The money then returns to the artist's Stripe balance and the buyer has not been repaid. PlotArt tells the artist and the buyer. The artist repays the buyer once, by one route only: a new refund through PlotArt, or a direct repayment confirmed to PlotArt with evidence.

7.7 **Commission when a buyer is refunded.** PlotArt returns commission as the artist's agreement requires (PA-GAA cl. 9):
  (a) in full for a full refund;
  (b) in proportion to the artwork amount kept for a partial refund;
  (c) in full where a dispute is decided against the artist and the buyer keeps the refunded amount. PlotArt treats Stripe's notice that the dispute was lost as the notice under PA-GAA cl. 9.1, and returns the commission within 30 days of it.
On a pilot order, PlotArt returns commission through Stripe once Stripe confirms that the buyer's refund has succeeded, and in any case within the period in PA-GAA cl. 9. The amount already returned as the processing reimbursement is taken into account, so that the same money is not returned twice. Any part of the commission that cannot be returned through Stripe for that reason is paid directly under clause 3.5. Commission already returned for a refund that Stripe later reverses (clause 7.6) is not returned a second time when the buyer is repaid. Refunds of delivery charges do not change commission.

7.8 **Disputes (chargebacks).** A buyer's card issuer may reverse a payment. Stripe then debits the disputed amount and Stripe's dispute fees from the artist's Stripe account. Stripe's published EEA pricing at the time of drafting, before any VAT under clause 3.6, which will be re-checked when this addendum is issued:
  (a) a dispute received fee of €20 for each dispute, which is not returned even if the dispute is won;
  (b) a dispute countered fee of €20 if the dispute is responded to with evidence, which Stripe returns if the dispute is won.
Dispute fees, and any VAT Stripe charges on them, are not reimbursed by PlotArt (clause 3.4). They appear in the artist's statement as Stripe records them (clause 3.8). The processing reimbursement already made for the payment is not reversed. The artist responds to any dispute in their own Stripe Dashboard. PlotArt provides the order record, quote, terms acceptance and delivery evidence it holds.

7.9 **Losses with Stripe.** Under the account configuration, Stripe, not PlotArt, is responsible for any negative balance on the artist's Stripe account that cannot be recovered. Stripe recovers such a balance from the artist under Stripe's own terms. This does not affect what the artist owes the buyer as seller.

## 8. Records

8.1 PlotArt keeps the records of a pilot order: the order record, quote versions, approvals, payment events, ledger, refunds, reimbursements, statements and delivery evidence.
  (a) For an order that was paid, they are kept until 31 December of the sixth year after the order is settled. An order is settled when it is closed or fully refunded and nothing is outstanding: no refund pending, and no amount owed under clause 3.5 or 7.7. The date is fixed when the order is settled;
  (b) for an order that was never paid, they are kept for two years after it ended;
  (c) the artist's opt-in record and Stripe account link are kept for six years after the artist leaves the pilot, or for as long as any of the artist's pilot orders is kept, whichever is later. The artist's seller information is deleted when the artist leaves the pilot; each order keeps the copy shown on its quote.
The records include the exact quote shown to the buyer, the buyer's purchase confirmation and its delivery record, and the record of amounts PlotArt owes and has paid (without bank details). Records are never deleted while a dispute on the order is open. They are kept longer only under a legal hold for a stated reason, such as a dispute, claim or Revenue enquiry. A dispute that arises after the order is settled places a hold automatically. A hold is reviewed at least every 12 months and released when no longer needed. These records are kept separately from the artist's gallery records, so they remain available for refunds and reconciliation even after the artist leaves PlotArt. They are also separate from enquiry messages, which follow the artist's agreement (for example, PA-FAA cl. 5.5).

## 9. Information shared, and what each party can see

9.1 **PlotArt sends to Stripe:**
  (a) to open the artist's account: the artist's PlotArt Studio email address, their country and PlotArt's internal reference for the artist;
  (b) for each checkout: the buyer's email address, the order reference, the artwork title and the artist's name as shown to the buyer, and the amounts.

9.2 **PlotArt receives from Stripe and stores:**
  (a) whether the artist's account can take payments and receive payouts, and which onboarding items Stripe still needs (by name only);
  (b) payment, refund, fee and dispute identifiers, amounts, statuses and reasons, and Stripe's fee lines for the payment and any dispute;
  (c) a record of each Stripe event it processes, without the event's full contents.

9.3 **What PlotArt can view through its access to Stripe but does not store.** To operate the pilot, PlotArt's access to Stripe allows it to view some further details that it does not copy into its own records. Tested in Stripe's test mode:
  (a) about the artist: the email address, address, date of birth and name given to Stripe; identity details given during onboarding; the business phone and website; and the bank's name, country and branch code and the last four digits of the payout account. It cannot see the full account number;
  (b) about the buyer, for each payment: name, email address and billing country; card brand, card country, funding type, expiry month and year and last four digits; and a link to Stripe's receipt. It cannot see the full card number.
PlotArt looks at these details only when it needs to for a specific order, for example to resolve a dispute or a refund problem. [Founder decision: whether to ask Stripe to narrow PlotArt's access, if Stripe offers a narrower permission that still lets the pilot work.]

9.4 **What PlotArt stores about the artist:** the seller information in clause 2.2. It shows this to buyers on each quote and in each purchase confirmation, keeps a copy with each issued quote, and deletes the current version when the artist leaves the pilot.

9.5 **What PlotArt stores about the buyer:** the buyer's email address with the order, the quote they were shown and their purchase confirmation. PlotArt sends the buyer's email address to Stripe at checkout.

9.6 **The artist, as holder of the Stripe account, sees in their own Stripe Dashboard** the payment details Stripe records for each buyer payment. These can include the buyer's name, email address, billing country, and card brand and last four digits. PlotArt shows the artist the order but not the buyer's email address, unless the artist needs it to arrange delivery or a return.

9.7 Full card numbers are entered only on Stripe's page and never reach PlotArt or the artist.

9.8 **Bank details for direct payments.** PlotArt needs an artist's bank details only to pay an amount under clause 3.5 directly. It asks for them by telephone and enters them only in its business online banking, which the founder alone can access. They are never entered in PlotArt Studio, the payment pilot, PlotArt's application logs or emails. PlotArt's bank holds them:
  (a) as a saved payee, which PlotArt deletes once no further payment to the artist is expected; and
  (b) in the bank's record of each transfer (the account name and IBAN), which PlotArt keeps as part of its accounting records for six years after the end of the year of the payment, and which cannot be deleted from the bank's own records.
The bank processes them under its own terms and privacy notice. PlotArt's own pilot records keep only the amount, date and transfer reference.

9.9 PlotArt processes this information as described in its privacy notice, which will be updated before the pilot takes live payments.

## 10. Test mode and live payments

10.1 **Test mode is a sandbox.** While the pilot runs in Stripe's test mode, no real payment, refund or payout takes place. Test accounts, test acceptances and test consents exist only for testing.

10.2 **Live payments need fresh acceptance.** Accepting this addendum in test mode, or any test version of it, does not authorise live payments. Before any live payment, the artist must:
  (a) accept the issued live version of this addendum, identified by its own version, after it has been shown to them;
  (b) complete Stripe's live onboarding for a live Stripe account.
No test-mode acceptance or account becomes a live one.
