# PlotArt Buyer Purchase Terms — PA-BPT-2026-11 (DRAFT r1, 16 November 2026)

**Status: draft for founder review. Not issued. Not shown to any buyer.** Issued only as fingerprinted HTML in
`agreements/payment-issued/` and installed by an approved migration.

1. **Who you are buying from.** You are buying an original artwork from the artist named on your quote, who is the
   seller. PlotArt (PlotNua, operated by Colin Macaulay, CRO business name no. 794392) introduced you and coordinates the
   sale; PlotArt is not the seller.
2. **What you pay.** Your quote shows the artwork price and, separately, the charge for packing, transport and any
   cover, and the total in euro. Nothing else is added at checkout. Your quote is valid until the time shown on it.
3. **Payment.** You pay by card through Stripe's secure checkout. Your payment goes to the artist's Stripe account.
   PlotArt does not hold your payment; it is not escrow. Your order is confirmed only when Stripe confirms the payment
   to us; you will receive Stripe's receipt by email.
4. **One original.** Each original can be sold once. While you are in checkout it is reserved for you for a short time.
   If, exceptionally, a payment is completed for a work that has already been sold, you will be refunded in full.
5. **Delivery and cover.** Your quote states who packs, who transports, the delivery window, tracking, whether a signed
   receipt and condition photographs are provided, and the cover actually arranged (or that there is none). Please
   inspect the work on arrival and tell us within 48 hours of any damage, with photographs.
6. **Your rights.** If you are a consumer, you have the rights Irish and EU consumer law gives you, including — for a
   distance sale — the right to cancel within 14 days of receiving the artwork, and remedies if the artwork is not as
   described. Return arrangements and costs are set out on your quote. Your rights do not depend on any carrier or
   insurance claim being made or completed.
7. **Refunds.** Refunds are made to your original card. The seller is responsible for refunds; PlotArt will help.
8. **Questions and complaints.** hello@plotnua.ie, quoting your order reference. We reply within 14 days.
9. **Your data.** PlotArt keeps your email address and the order record for six years for accounting and legal
   reasons, and shares what is needed with the artist (to deliver) and Stripe (to take payment). See the PlotNua privacy
   notice. [Privacy notice update required before activation.]
10. **Law.** Irish law applies. If you are a consumer elsewhere you keep the protection of your own country's mandatory
    rules.
