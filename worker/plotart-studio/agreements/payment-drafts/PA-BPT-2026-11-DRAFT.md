## 2. What you pay

2.1 Your quote shows, in euro:
  (a) the artwork price;
  (b) separately, the charge for packing, transport and transit insurance;
  (c) the total.
Nothing else is added at checkout.

2.2 You can pay the quote until the date and time shown on it (Irish time).

## 3. Payment

3.1 You pay by card on Stripe's secure checkout page. Your payment goes to the seller's own Stripe account. PlotArt does not hold your payment, and it is not an escrow arrangement.

3.2 Your purchase is confirmed when Stripe confirms the payment. The page you return to shows whether payment has been confirmed.

3.3 **Your purchase confirmation.** Once your payment is confirmed, PlotArt prepares your purchase confirmation. It sets out the artwork, the seller and their contact details, what you paid, the delivery and return arrangements, these terms in full and the model cancellation form. You can open it from your quote link, and PlotArt keeps an exact copy. [Before live payments: PlotArt also emails it to you; confirm the sending service and wording.]

## 4. One original; holding the artwork; purchase limits

4.1 Each original can be sold only once. While your quote is open, PlotArt does not issue a quote for the same artwork to anyone else, and the seller has agreed not to sell or promise it elsewhere.

4.2 Your quote is not a guarantee that the artwork stays available. If, exceptionally, the seller sells it another way before you pay, PlotArt cancels your quote and you will not be able to pay. Once you open Stripe checkout, the artwork is also blocked from any other sale for the checkout period, currently 31 minutes, plus a short time for confirmation.

4.3 If, exceptionally, your payment completes for a work that has already been sold, you will be refunded in full.

4.4 During the pilot, PlotArt cannot arrange purchases of EUR 10,000 or more. This includes several purchases that together reach that amount.

## 5. Delivery and insurance

5.1 Your quote states:
  (a) who packs and who transports the artwork;
  (b) the delivery window and tracking;
  (c) whether a signed receipt and condition photographs are provided;
  (d) the transit insurance arranged for your shipment: the insurer, the insured value (at least the artwork price), the period of cover and the claims contact.

5.2 **Please inspect the artwork when it arrives.** If it is damaged, it helps if you tell the seller or PlotArt (hello@plotnua.ie) within 48 hours, with photographs, so that any insurance claim can be made promptly. This is a request, not a condition. Reporting later does not affect your rights under section 7.

## 6. Changing your mind: your right to cancel

This section applies if you are a consumer buying from a seller who is a trader (section 1.2).

6.1 **Cancellation period.** You may cancel your purchase without giving any reason within 14 days from the day you, or someone you name other than the carrier, receive the artwork.

6.2 **How to cancel.** Tell the seller, or PlotArt on the seller's behalf (hello@plotnua.ie), that you have decided to cancel. You can use the model cancellation form in section 11 or any other clear statement, for example by email. To meet the deadline, it is enough to send your notice before the 14 days end.

6.3 **Returning the artwork.**
  (a) Return the artwork to {returns address}, by the method shown on your quote {return method}, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after you tell us you are cancelling.
  (b) Unless your quote says the seller will collect it, you arrange the return.
  (c) Because original artworks cannot normally be returned by post, you bear the cost of returning it when you cancel for a change of mind. Your quote states that cost: {return cost, or estimated maximum return cost, EUR}. Please pack the work as it was delivered. We recommend insuring it for its value while it is in transit.

6.4 **Your refund.** The seller refunds everything you paid, including the delivery charge on your quote, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after being told that you are cancelling.
  (a) The seller may withhold the refund until it has received the artwork back, or until you have shown that you have sent it back, whichever is earlier.
  (b) The refund is made to the card you paid with, at no charge to you.

6.5 **Handling the artwork.** You may handle the artwork as you would in a gallery to establish its nature and characteristics. You may be liable for any loss in its value that results from handling beyond that.

## 7. If the artwork is damaged, faulty or not as described

7.1 The seller must supply an artwork that matches its description and the quote. Your rights in this section are separate from, and in addition to, your right to cancel in section 6.

7.2 If the artwork arrives damaged, or is not as described, tell the seller or PlotArt. If you do so within 30 days of receiving it, you are entitled to reject it and receive a full refund. Your other legal remedies continue after that.

7.3 In these cases the seller bears the cost of returning the artwork, or reimburses you if you have paid it. The seller tells you how the return will be arranged.

7.4 None of this depends on any carrier or insurance claim being made or completed.

## 8. Refunds

8.1 Refunds are made to your original card through Stripe. Your right to be repaid does not depend on how the seller's Stripe account is funded.

8.2 **If a refund is pending.** Stripe may hold a refund as pending until the seller's Stripe account can fund it. A pending refund has not been completed: you have not been repaid until Stripe completes it. While it is pending, the seller does not repay you another way as well, because you would then be repaid twice. The seller must make sure you are repaid within the time limits in sections 6 and 7.

8.3 **If Stripe cannot complete a refund,** or reverses one it first confirmed (for example because your card was closed), you have not been repaid. The seller then repays you once, either by a new refund through Stripe or directly, for example by bank transfer, and PlotArt confirms it with you.

8.4 [Once buyer notifications are in place:] PlotArt tells you when it becomes aware that a refund is pending, has failed or has been reversed.

## 9. Your data

9.1 **What PlotArt receives and keeps.** PlotArt receives your email address with your quote. If you paid, it keeps your email address and the order record until 31 December of the sixth year after the order is settled (completed or fully refunded, with nothing outstanding), for accounting and legal reasons. If you never paid, it keeps them until two years after the quote ended. They are never deleted while a payment dispute about the order is open. PlotArt keeps them longer only under a legal hold for a specific reason, such as a dispute, claim or tax enquiry about the order, reviewed at least every 12 months. The record includes the exact quote you were shown and your purchase confirmation. The order record is separate from any enquiry you sent through PlotArt, which follows its own retention period.

9.2 **Stripe.** Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  (a) Your card number is entered only on Stripe's page and is never seen by PlotArt or the seller.
  (b) Payment details Stripe records, such as your name, email address, billing country, and card brand and last four digits, are visible to the seller in their Stripe account.
  (c) PlotArt's access to Stripe also lets it view these details: your name, email address and billing country, and your card's brand, country, type, expiry date and last four digits. PlotArt does not copy them into its own records, and looks at them only when needed for your order, for example to resolve a refund or a payment dispute. PlotArt sends your email address to Stripe at checkout.

9.3 **The seller.** PlotArt shares with the seller what the seller needs to deliver the artwork and handle any return.

9.4 See the PlotNua privacy notice at plotnua.ie/privacy.html, which will be updated before the pilot takes live payments.
