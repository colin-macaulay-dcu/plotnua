# PlotArt Buyer Purchase Terms — PA-BPT-2026-11 (DRAFT r3, 16 November 2026)

**Status: draft for founder review. Not issued. Not shown to any buyer.** Issued only as fingerprinted HTML in
`agreements/payment-issued/` and installed by an approved migration.

r2 changes: transit insurance stated as included for every pilot shipment (cl. 5); refund timing and what happens if
a refund is delayed (cl. 7); linked-purchase limit (cl. 4).

1. **Who you are buying from.** You are buying an original artwork from the artist named on your quote, who is the
   seller. PlotArt (PlotNua, operated by Colin Macaulay, CRO business name no. 794392) introduced you and coordinates the
   sale; PlotArt is not the seller.
2. **What you pay.** Your quote shows the artwork price and, separately, the charge for packing, transport and
   transit insurance, and the total in euro. Nothing else is added at checkout. Your quote is valid until the time shown
   on it.
3. **Payment.** You pay by card through Stripe's secure checkout. Your payment goes to the artist's Stripe account.
   PlotArt does not hold your payment; it is not escrow. Your order is confirmed only when Stripe confirms the payment
   to us; you will receive Stripe's receipt by email.
4. **One original, and purchase limits.** Each original can be sold once. While you are in checkout it is reserved for
   you for a short time. If, exceptionally, a payment is completed for a work that has already been sold, you will be
   refunded in full. During the pilot PlotArt cannot arrange purchases of EUR 10,000 or more, including several
   purchases that together reach that amount.
5. **Delivery and insurance.** Your quote states who packs, who transports, the delivery window, tracking, whether a
   signed receipt and condition photographs are provided, and the transit insurance arranged for your shipment: the
   insurer, the insured value (at least the artwork price), the period and the claims contact. Please inspect the work on
   arrival and tell us within 48 hours of any damage, with photographs.
6. **Your rights.** If you are a consumer, you have the rights Irish and EU consumer law gives you, including — for a
   distance sale — the right to cancel within 14 days of receiving the artwork, and remedies if the artwork is not as
   described. Return arrangements and costs are set out on your quote. Your rights do not depend on any carrier or
   insurance claim being made or completed.
7. **Refunds.** The seller is responsible for refunds; PlotArt will help. Refunds are made to your original card through
   Stripe. If a refund is delayed on the seller's side, we will tell you and keep you informed; your right to be repaid
   does not depend on how the seller's Stripe account is funded. If Stripe cannot complete the refund, the seller repays
   you directly and we confirm it with you.
8. **Questions and complaints.** hello@plotnua.ie, quoting your order reference. We reply within 14 days.
9. **Your data.** PlotArt keeps your email address and the order record until the end of the sixth year after your last
   payment or refund (two years after the quote ended if you never paid) for accounting and legal reasons, or longer only
   while a specific dispute or claim about the order is open, and shares what is needed with the artist (to
   deliver) and Stripe (to take payment). See the PlotNua privacy notice. [Privacy notice update required before
   activation.]
10. **Law.** Irish law applies. If you are a consumer elsewhere you keep the protection of your own country's mandatory
    rules.
