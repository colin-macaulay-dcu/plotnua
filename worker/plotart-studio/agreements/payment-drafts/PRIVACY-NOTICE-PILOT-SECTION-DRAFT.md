## The PlotArt payment pilot

PlotArt runs an optional pilot in which a buyer can pay an artist for an original artwork by card, through a checkout provided by Stripe. The artist is the seller. PlotArt (PlotNua, operated by Colin MacAulay, registered as Colin Macaulay, business name no. 794392) introduces the buyer, prepares the quote and operates the checkout. [While the pilot runs in Stripe's test mode, no real payment is taken and these records are test records.]

### What we collect and why

**Buyers**
- **What we keep:** your email address with your quote, the exact quote you were shown, your acceptance of the purchase terms, your purchase confirmation, any notices about refunds, and the payment, refund and dispute records for your order (amounts, dates, statuses and Stripe reference numbers).
- **Why:** to perform the purchase contract and give you the information the law requires (contract), to keep accounting records (legal obligation), and to prevent fraud and apply our purchase limits (legitimate interests).

**Artists in the pilot**
- **What we keep:** your opt-in record; your Stripe account reference and its status, and the names of any onboarding items Stripe still needs; and the seller information you give us (legal and trading name, address, returns address, email, telephone, trader status and VAT number), which we show to buyers on each quote and in each purchase confirmation. We also keep each order's records, statements and the amounts we owe you and have paid.
- **Why:** to operate the pilot under your agreement with us (contract), and to keep accounting records (legal obligation).

### What we can see through Stripe but do not keep

Our access to Stripe lets us view some details that we do not copy into our own records. We look at them only when an order needs it, for example to resolve a refund or a payment dispute.
- **About buyers:** name, email address and billing country, and card brand, card country, card type, expiry date and last four digits. We never see full card numbers.
- **About artists:** the email address, name, address and date of birth given to Stripe, identity details given during Stripe's onboarding, business phone and website, and the bank's name, country and branch code and the last four digits of the payout account. We never see full account numbers.

### Bank details for payments we make to artists

We ask for an artist's bank details only when we must pay them an amount directly. We ask by telephone and enter the details only in our business online banking, which only the founder can access. They are never entered in PlotArt Studio, the payment pilot, our application logs or our emails.

Our bank keeps them:
- as a saved payee, which we delete once no further payment is expected; and
- in its record of each transfer, which is part of our accounting records.

### Who receives your information

| Recipient | What it does with your information |
|---|---|
| **Stripe** (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland) | Processes payments as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We send Stripe the buyer's email address, the order reference, the artwork title, the artist's name and the amounts. Artists open their own Stripe account with Stripe. |
| **The artist (seller)** | Sees the payment details Stripe records in their own Stripe account, and the order details needed for delivery and returns. |
| **Our bank** | Holds artists' bank details for the transfers we make, under its own terms. |
| **Cloudflare** | Hosts the pilot as our processor. |
| [**Resend**] | [Sends our pilot emails as our processor, once email sending is switched on. Until then, pilot messages are kept by us and shown on the buyer's quote page; nothing is emailed.] |

### How long we keep it

| Record | How long we keep it |
|---|---|
| Paid orders: the order, quote, confirmation, notices, ledger and amounts owed | Until 31 December of the sixth year after the order is settled |
| Unpaid quotes | Two years after the quote ended |
| Any order with an open payment dispute | Never deleted while the dispute is open |
| Records under a legal hold (for example, a dispute, claim or tax enquiry) | Longer, for as long as the hold lasts; holds are reviewed at least every 12 months |
| An artist's opt-in record and Stripe account link | Six years after they leave the pilot, or as long as any of their orders is kept |
| An artist's seller information | Deleted when they leave the pilot; each order keeps the copy shown on its quote |
| Bank transfer records at our bank | Six years after the end of the year of the payment |
| Enquiry messages | Their own, shorter, period, described elsewhere in this notice |
