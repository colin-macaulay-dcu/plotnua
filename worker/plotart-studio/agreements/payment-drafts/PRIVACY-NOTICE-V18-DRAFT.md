## Change 2: Section 8, new subsection (after "Enquiries to PlotArt artists (buyers)")

> **The PlotArt payment pilot (buyers and artists)**
>
> PlotArt runs an optional pilot in which a buyer can pay an artist for an original artwork by card, through a checkout provided by Stripe. The artist is the seller and is paid into their own Stripe account. PlotArt prepares the quote, operates the checkout and receives its commission from the artist's payment.
>
> **Buyers.**
> - **What we keep:** your email address with your quote; the exact quote you were shown; your acceptance of the purchase terms; your purchase confirmation; any notices about refunds; and the payment, refund and dispute records for your order (amounts, dates, statuses and Stripe reference numbers).
> - **Why:**
>   - to take steps at your request before the sale, and to provide the checkout, confirmation and notices you are sent (contract);
>   - to keep accounting records (legal obligation);
>   - to prevent fraud, apply our purchase limits and be able to establish or defend legal claims (legitimate interests).
> - **What we send to Stripe:** your email address, the order reference, the artwork title, the artist's name and the amounts.
> - **What we share with the artist:** what they need to deliver the artwork and handle any return.
>
> **Artists in the pilot.**
> - **What we keep:**
>   - your opt-in record;
>   - your Stripe account reference and its status, and the names of any onboarding items Stripe still needs;
>   - the seller information you give us (legal and trading name, address, returns address, email, telephone, whether you sell as a trader, and VAT number). We show it to buyers on each quote and in each purchase confirmation;
>   - each order's records and statement, and the amounts we owe you and have paid.
> - **Why:**
>   - to operate the pilot under your agreement with us (contract);
>   - to keep accounting records (legal obligation);
>   - to give buyers the seller information the law requires (legal obligation).
> - **What we send to Stripe to open your account:** your PlotArt Studio email address, your country and our internal reference for you.
>
> **What we can see through Stripe but do not keep.** Our access to Stripe lets us view some details that we do not copy into our own records. We look at them only when an order needs it, for example to resolve a refund or a payment dispute.
> - **About buyers:** name, email address, billing country, and card brand, country, type, expiry date and last four digits. We never see full card numbers.
> - **About artists:**
>   - the name, email address, address and date of birth given to Stripe;
>   - identity details given during Stripe's onboarding;
>   - business phone and website;
>   - the bank's name, country and branch code, and the last four digits of the payout account.
>
>   We never see full account numbers.
>
> **Bank details for payments we make to artists.**
> - We ask for an artist's bank details only when we must pay them an amount directly.
> - We ask by telephone and enter the details only in our business online banking, which only the founder can access.
> - They are never entered in PlotArt Studio, the payment pilot, our application logs or our emails.
> - Our bank keeps them as a saved payee, which we delete once no further payment is expected, and in its record of each transfer, which is part of our accounting records.

## Change 3: Section 10, recipients (add after "Enquiry emails to artists and confirmations to buyers are sent by…")

> The PlotArt payment pilot described in Section 8 uses:
> - Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, Ireland), which processes payments as an independent controller under its own privacy policy;
> - PlotNua's bank, for transfers we make to artists, under its own terms;
> - services provided by Cloudflare, which host the pilot as our processor;
> - the same email delivery service, which sends pilot confirmations and notices as our processor under its data processing terms.
>
> The email delivery service (Resend) is based in the United States. Resend states that it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Its Data Processing Addendum also includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers from the European Economic Area. Cloudflare's safeguards are described in Section 7.

## Change 5: Section 12, retention (add after the PlotArt gallery records line)

> PlotArt payment pilot records:
> - paid orders (the order, quote, confirmation, notices, ledger and amounts owed) are kept until 31 December of the sixth year after the order is settled;
> - unpaid quotes are kept for two years after the quote ended;
> - records are never deleted while a payment dispute about the order is open, and are kept longer only under a legal hold for a stated reason, reviewed at least every 12 months;
> - an artist's opt-in record and Stripe account link are kept for six years after they leave the pilot, or for as long as any of their orders is kept;
> - an artist's seller information is deleted when they leave the pilot (each order keeps the copy shown on its quote);
> - bank transfer records at our bank are kept for six years after the end of the year of payment.

## Change 2a: Section 8, "The PlotArt payment pilot", add after the "Buyers" list

> **Buying several artworks together (the basket).**
> - **What we keep:** your name, email address and delivery address; the artworks, sellers and amounts shown to you before you paid; your acceptance of the basket purchase terms; and, for each artwork, the order and payment records described above.
> - **Your card:** you enter it once, on Stripe's page. Stripe saves it on PlotArt's Stripe account and copies it to each seller's own Stripe account so that each seller can be paid for their artwork. It is used only for the payments in that basket. We never see the full card number.
> - **What we share with each seller:** your name and delivery address and, where needed, your email address, for their own artworks only. A seller is not told what you bought from other sellers.
> - **Why:** as for the pilot above (contract; legal obligation; legitimate interests).

## Change 2b: Section 8, "Artists in the pilot", add to "What we keep"

> - if you take part in the basket: your acceptance of the basket addendum; your confirmation that your seller information may be shown to buyers before they pay; any consent to Stripe's processing fees on commission-free sales and any withdrawal; and your delivery offers. Your seller information is shown on the basket page, which anyone can open, only after you confirm it.

## Change 5a: Section 12, retention (add to the payment pilot line)

> Basket records follow the same periods as pilot orders. An artist's basket acceptance, confirmations and consents are kept for six years after the artist leaves the basket, or as long as any of their basket orders is kept, whichever is later.
