Buyer Purchase Terms

Identifier PA-BPT-2026-11 · Text of 20 November 2026

1. Who you are buying from

1.1You are buying an original artwork from the artist named on your quote, who is the seller. Your contract of sale is with the artist. Your quote and your purchase confirmation show:

  • (a) the seller's legal name and any trading name;
  • (b) the geographical address at which they are established, and any different address for returns and complaints;
  • (c) the seller's email and telephone number;
  • (d) the seller's VAT number, if they are registered.

1.2The seller's declaration. The seller has declared to PlotArt that they sell as a trader, in the course of their business, craft or profession. PlotArt offers its managed checkout only to sellers who have made that declaration. If you are buying as a consumer, you have consumer rights under EU consumer protection law, including the right to cancel; section 6 explains how to cancel.

1.3PlotArt's role. PlotArt (PlotNua, operated by Colin Macaulay, CRO business name no. 794392, hello@plotnua.ie) introduced you to the seller. It prepares the quote and provides the checkout. PlotArt is not the seller.

  • (a) The seller is responsible for the artwork, its delivery, cancellations, returns and refunds, and for the artwork matching its description.
  • (b) PlotArt is responsible for the accuracy of the information it presents on the quote and for operating the checkout.
  • (c) PlotArt can receive your cancellation notice, complaint or damage report on the seller's behalf at hello@plotnua.ie, and will pass it on to the seller.
  • (d) PlotArt's commission. The seller pays PlotArt a commission out of the artwork price, under the seller's agreement with PlotArt. It is not added to what you pay and does not change your rights.
  • (e) PlotArt selects and orders artists and artworks by hand, for curatorial reasons. No seller pays for placement.

2. What you pay

2.1Your quote shows, in euro:

  • (a) the artwork price;
  • (b) separately, the charge for packing, transport and transit insurance;
  • (c) the total.

Nothing else is added at checkout.

2.2You can pay the quote until the date and time shown on it (Irish time).

3. Payment

3.1You pay by card on Stripe's secure checkout page. Your payment goes to the seller's own Stripe account. PlotArt does not hold your payment, and it is not an escrow arrangement.

3.2Your purchase is confirmed when Stripe confirms the payment. The page you return to shows whether payment has been confirmed.

3.3Your purchase confirmation. Once your payment is confirmed, PlotArt prepares your purchase confirmation. It sets out the artwork, the seller and their contact details, what you paid, the delivery and return arrangements, these terms in full and the model cancellation form. You can open it from your quote link, and PlotArt keeps an exact copy. PlotArt also emails it to you at the address you gave, as your confirmation of the contract.

4. One original; holding the artwork; purchase limits

4.1Each original can be sold only once. While your quote is open, PlotArt does not issue a quote for the same artwork to anyone else, and the seller has agreed not to sell or promise it elsewhere.

4.2Your quote is not a guarantee that the artwork stays available. If, exceptionally, the seller sells it another way before you pay, PlotArt cancels your quote and you will not be able to pay. Once you open Stripe checkout, the artwork is also blocked from any other sale for the checkout period, currently 31 minutes, plus a short time for confirmation.

4.3If, exceptionally, your payment completes for a work that has already been sold, you will be refunded in full.

4.4During the pilot, PlotArt cannot arrange purchases of EUR 10,000 or more. This includes several purchases that together reach that amount.

5. Delivery and insurance

5.1Your quote states:

  • (a) who packs and who transports the artwork;
  • (b) the delivery window and tracking;
  • (c) whether a signed receipt and condition photographs are provided;
  • (d) the transit insurance arranged for your shipment: the insurer, the insured value (at least the artwork price), the period of cover and the claims contact.

5.2Please inspect the artwork when it arrives. If it is damaged, it helps if you tell the seller or PlotArt (hello@plotnua.ie) within 48 hours, with photographs, so that any insurance claim can be made promptly. This is a request, not a condition. Reporting later does not affect your rights under section 7.

6. Changing your mind: your right to cancel

This section applies if you are a consumer buying from a seller who is a trader (section 1.2).

6.1Cancellation period. You may cancel your purchase without giving any reason within 14 days from the day you, or someone you name other than the carrier, receive the artwork.

6.2How to cancel. Tell the seller, or PlotArt on the seller's behalf (hello@plotnua.ie), that you have decided to cancel. You can use the model cancellation form in section 11 or any other clear statement, for example by email. To meet the deadline, it is enough to send your notice before the 14 days end.

6.3Returning the artwork.

  • (a) Return the artwork to the returns address shown on your quote, by the return method shown on your quote, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after you tell us you are cancelling.
  • (b) Unless your quote says the seller will collect it, you arrange the return.
  • (c) Because original artworks cannot normally be returned by post, you bear the cost of returning it when you cancel for a change of mind. Your quote states that cost, or a reasonable estimate of the maximum cost. Please pack the work as it was delivered. We recommend insuring it for its value while it is in transit.

6.4Your refund. The seller refunds everything you paid, including the delivery charge on your quote, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after being told that you are cancelling.

  • (a) The seller may withhold the refund until it has received the artwork back, or until you have shown that you have sent it back, whichever is earlier.
  • (b) The refund is made to the card you paid with, at no charge to you.

6.5Handling the artwork. You may handle the artwork as you would in a gallery to establish its nature and characteristics. You may be liable for any loss in its value that results from handling beyond that.

7. If the artwork is damaged, faulty or not as described

7.1The seller must supply an artwork that matches its description and the quote. Your rights in this section are separate from, and in addition to, your right to cancel in section 6.

7.2If the artwork arrives damaged, or is not as described, tell the seller or PlotArt. If you do so within 30 days of receiving it (or, if it was installed, of installation being completed), you may end the contract and receive a full refund, including the delivery charge, to the card you paid with. You may instead ask for a repair or a replacement where that is possible. Your other legal remedies continue after that.

7.3In these cases the seller bears the cost of returning the artwork, or reimburses you if you have paid it. The seller tells you how the return will be arranged.

7.4None of this depends on any carrier or insurance claim being made or completed.

8. Refunds

8.1Refunds are made to your original card through Stripe. Your right to be repaid does not depend on how the seller's Stripe account is funded.

8.2If a refund is pending. Stripe may hold a refund as pending until the seller's Stripe account can fund it. A pending refund has not been completed: you have not been repaid until Stripe completes it. While it is pending, the seller does not repay you another way as well, because you would then be repaid twice. The seller must make sure you are repaid within the time limits in sections 6 and 7.

8.3If Stripe cannot complete a refund, or reverses one it first confirmed (for example because your card was closed), you have not been repaid. The seller then repays you once, either by a new refund through Stripe or directly, for example by bank transfer, and PlotArt confirms it with you.

8.4PlotArt tells you when it becomes aware that a refund is pending, has failed or has been reversed. The notice is emailed to you and also appears on your quote page.

9. Your data

9.1What PlotArt receives and keeps. PlotArt receives your email address with your quote. If you paid, it keeps your email address and the order record until 31 December of the sixth year after the order is settled (completed or fully refunded, with nothing outstanding), for accounting and legal reasons. If you never paid, it keeps them until two years after the quote ended. They are never deleted while a payment dispute about the order is open. PlotArt keeps them longer only under a legal hold for a specific reason, such as a dispute, claim or tax enquiry about the order, reviewed at least every 12 months. The record includes the exact quote you were shown and your purchase confirmation. The order record is separate from any enquiry you sent through PlotArt, which follows its own retention period.

9.2Stripe. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy.

  • (a) Your card number is entered only on Stripe's page and is never seen by PlotArt or the seller.
  • (b) Payment details Stripe records, such as your name, email address, billing country, and card brand and last four digits, are visible to the seller in their Stripe account.
  • (c) PlotArt's access to Stripe also lets it view these details: your name, email address and billing country, and your card's brand, country, type, expiry date and last four digits. PlotArt does not copy them into its own records, and looks at them only when needed for your order, for example to resolve a refund or a payment dispute. PlotArt sends your email address to Stripe at checkout.

9.3The seller. PlotArt shares with the seller what the seller needs to deliver the artwork and handle any return.

9.4See the section "The PlotArt payment pilot" in the PlotNua privacy notice at plotnua.ie/privacy.html.

10. Questions, complaints and law

10.1Contact the seller at the contact details on your quote, or PlotArt at hello@plotnua.ie, quoting your order reference.

10.2Irish law applies. If you are a consumer living elsewhere, you keep the protection of the mandatory rules of your own country.
