PLOTART

Artist Basket Addendum

Identifier PA-BAA-2026-11 · Text of 21 November 2026

0. How this addendum relates to the artist's agreement

0.1This addendum is optional. It applies only to basket orders: purchases of the artist's artworks made by a buyer through the PlotArt basket, alone or together with other artists' works. It sits beside the artist's PlotArt agreement (PA-FAA-2026-11 Founding Artist Agreement or PA-GAA-2026-11 Gallery Artist Agreement), the Gallery Publication and Image Licence PA-GPIL-2026-11 and the Payment Pilot Addendum PA-PAY-2026-11. An artist who does not accept it loses nothing: their gallery listing, enquiries, direct sales and any quote under PA-PAY continue exactly as before.

0.2Nothing in this addendum reduces any right the artist has under their agreement. In particular:

  • (a) Founding Artists. Every Founding Enquiry stays commission-free permanently, and commission can arise only under separate commission terms that the artist has expressly accepted (PA-FAA cl. 3, 5 and 6). This addendum is not such terms. PlotArt charges no commission and no fee of any kind on a basket order that carries no commission under the artist's agreement. The only cost on such an order is Stripe's own processing fee, which applies only if the artist gives the separate processing consent in clause 4 and which the artist can avoid at any time by withdrawing it;
  • (b) Gallery Artists. The commission rate, Commission Base and refund rules remain those of PA-GAA cl. 5 and 9, including that the artist keeps at least 85% of the Commission Base. On a basket order, as on a pilot order, the artist's combined deduction for PlotArt commission and Stripe's payment processing is never more than that commission (clause 3.3);
  • (c) commission is never charged on delivery, packing, transport, insurance, installation or import costs;
  • (d) if any part of this addendum could be read as reducing such a right, the artist's agreement prevails on that point.

0.3How this addendum fits the artist's agreement.

  • (a) For a Gallery Artist, this addendum is a written variation under PA-GAA clause 13, applying only to basket orders. For those orders only: PA-GAA clause 4.2 applies as modified by clauses 2, 5 and 6 of this addendum; PlotArt's commission is collected under clause 3.2 instead of being reported and invoiced under PA-GAA clauses 8.1 to 8.4; and commission is returned under clause 7, within the period in PA-GAA clause 9. PA-GAA continues to apply in full to every other sale, and to basket orders in every respect not changed here.
  • (b) For a Founding Artist, this addendum changes nothing in PA-FAA. It introduces no commission and no compulsory fee (PA-FAA cl. 3 and 13.2). Taking part, and giving the processing consent, are the artist's free choice and can be withdrawn at any time.

0.4PA-PAY clauses that apply to basket orders. To take part in the basket, the artist must also have joined the payment pilot under PA-PAY-2026-11, which provides the artist's Stripe account, seller information and records. The following clauses of PA-PAY apply to every basket order as if it were a pilot order, with the changes made in this addendum:

  • (a) clause 2 (the artist is the seller; seller information; trader declaration; the artist's Stripe account; receiving notices);
  • (b) clauses 3.2 to 3.9 (collection of commission, Stripe processing fees and their reimbursement on commission orders, VAT, the artist's statement, tax);
  • (c) clause 5.2 (payouts);
  • (d) clause 6.3 and 6.4 (dispatch evidence; buyer's rights not depending on claims);
  • (e) clauses 7.1 to 7.9 (cancellations, returns, refunds, disputes and negative balances), reading "Buyer Purchase Terms PA-BPT-2026-11" as "Basket Purchase Terms PA-BBT-2026-11" and "quote" as "basket page and purchase confirmation";
  • (f) clause 8 (records) and clause 9 (information shared), with the additions in clause 9 of this addendum;
  • (g) clause 10 (test mode and live payments).

PA-PAY clause 1.3, which excludes multi-artwork orders and sales that carry no commission from the pilot, does not apply to basket orders. It continues to apply to quotes under PA-PAY.

1. What the basket is

1.1The basket lets a buyer buy, online and without a quote, one or more of the artist's available artworks for which the artist has a current delivery offer (clause 5), alone or together with other artists' works.

1.2The buyer enters their card once. PlotArt then makes a separate card payment for each artwork, directly to its artist's own Stripe account (clause 6). Two artworks by the same artist are two separate payments and two separate orders.

1.3Scope. The basket covers existing original artworks priced from EUR 1.00 to EUR 9,999.99, sold in euro, for delivery in the Republic of Ireland. It does not cover prints, commissions of new work, hire, discounts, gift cards or instalments. PlotArt will not accept a basket that would bring a buyer's apparently linked purchases through PlotArt to EUR 10,000 or more in twelve months (PA-PAY 1.4 applies). These are limits on the scope of the basket, not a statement about anyone's legal obligations.

1.4Taking part is optional. An artist who has joined the payment pilot (clause 0.4) joins the basket by accepting this addendum in PlotArt Studio, and can leave at any time there (clause 10). For a Founding Artist, joining the payment pilot changes nothing in PA-FAA (PA-PAY 0.4(b)).

2. The artist is the seller

2.1The artist is the seller of each of their artworks bought through the basket and contracts directly with the buyer. PlotArt introduces the buyer, provides the basket and checkout and coordinates the payments. It is not the seller, and does not take ownership or possession of any artwork.

2.2Seller information and trader declaration. PA-PAY clauses 2.2 and 2.3 apply. The law requires a buyer to be given the seller's identity, the geographical address at which the seller is established, and contact details before paying. Because the basket page can be opened by anyone, PlotArt shows the artist's seller information there only after the artist has separately confirmed, in PlotArt Studio, that it may be shown before payment. The confirmation is recorded with the exact information it covers; if the artist changes that information, their artworks are withdrawn from the basket until the artist confirms again. The VAT number is shown only if the artist is registered. An artwork cannot be offered in the basket while the artist's seller information, trader declaration or this confirmation is missing. The confirmation statement is:

I confirm that my seller information — legal name and any trading name, the geographical address at which I am established (and any separate returns address), my email and telephone for buyers, and my VAT number if I am registered — may be shown to anyone viewing the PlotArt basket before they buy one of my artworks, and in each purchase confirmation. I understand that the law requires buyers to be given the address at which I am established before they pay. If I do not want that address shown in this way, I will not confirm, and my artworks will not be offered in the basket; my gallery listing, enquiries and direct sales are unaffected.

2.3Buyer terms. The buyer's rights are set out in the Basket Purchase Terms PA-BBT-2026-11. The artist will honour them for each of their artworks, including:

  • (a) the buyer may cancel one artwork and keep others (PA-BBT 7.1);
  • (b) where the buyer bought two or more of the artist's artworks in one basket and they arrive separately, the cancellation period for each runs from receipt of the last of them (PA-BBT 7.2). Artworks by other artists in the same basket do not affect the artist's cancellation period;
  • (c) if the artist cannot deliver within the stated window, the buyer may end the contract for that artwork and be refunded in full (PA-BBT 6.2).

3. Commission, processing fees and tax

3.1PlotArt commission on a basket order is exactly what the artist's agreement provides:

  • (a) none on a sale arising from a Founding Enquiry (PA-FAA cl. 5);
  • (b) for a Founding Artist, none unless separate commission terms that the artist has expressly accepted were in effect when the buyer was introduced (PA-FAA cl. 6);
  • (c) for a Gallery Artist, the rate and Commission Base in PA-GAA cl. 5 (currently 15% of the artwork price).

Commission is never charged on delivery, packing, transport, insurance, installation or import costs. PlotArt charges the buyer no fee.

3.2Which rate applies. PlotArt records, for each basket order, whether it carries commission and on what basis, and shows it in the artist's statement. Where PlotArt cannot show that a sale by a Founding Artist carries commission under terms the artist expressly accepted, it carries none (PA-FAA cl. 5.6).

3.3Orders that carry commission. PA-PAY clauses 3.2 to 3.8 apply in full. PlotArt receives its commission as a Stripe application fee on that artwork's payment, reimburses the artist Stripe's actual processing fee on that payment and the VAT Stripe charges on it, within the time limits in PA-PAY 3.3, and pays any shortfall directly under PA-PAY 3.5. The artist's combined deduction for commission and Stripe's processing is never more than the commission.

3.4Orders that carry no commission. On a basket order that carries no PlotArt commission:

  • (a) PlotArt receives nothing: no commission, no application fee and no other fee;
  • (b) Stripe charges its processing fee on the whole amount the buyer pays for that artwork, including delivery, and deducts it, and any VAT Stripe charges on it, from the artist's Stripe balance. PlotArt does not reimburse it, because there is no application fee from which to do so;
  • (c) such an order can be made only if the artist has given the processing consent in clause 4, and it remains in effect when the buyer checks out.

3.5Fees on payments that are not taken. If a basket is cancelled after an artwork's payment has been authorised but before it is taken (clause 6.4), no payment is made to the artist and no commission is due. If Stripe charges the artist any fee for that authorisation, PlotArt reimburses it under PA-PAY 3.5.

3.6VAT and tax. PA-PAY clauses 3.6 and 3.9 apply. The artist remains responsible for any VAT or other tax on the sale of each artwork, and for any invoice or receipt the law requires the artist to give the buyer.

3.7Examples (illustration only). Stripe's published standard pricing for EEA businesses on 20 November 2026 is used (standard EEA cards 1.5% + €0.25), with VAT on Stripe's fee shown for a Stripe account based in Ireland (PA-PAY 3.6). PlotArt and the artist use the amounts Stripe actually charges.

4. Processing consent for orders that carry no commission

4.1What the consent is. A Founding Artist, or any artist whose sale would carry no commission, may agree that their commission-free artworks can be bought through the basket on the basis that the artist bears Stripe's actual processing fee on those payments (clause 3.4). PlotArt asks for this consent separately from acceptance of this addendum, with its own statement and tick box, and records the artist, the account that gave it, the version and fingerprint of this addendum, the statement shown, the time and whether it is given or withdrawn. A consent counts only for the version of this addendum it names, and only in live mode if given in live mode.

4.2Without the consent, the artist's commission-free artworks are not offered in the basket. Buyers can still enquire about them, and the artist sells them directly, commission-free and with no PlotArt charge, as before. The artist's commission-bearing artworks, if any, can still be offered in the basket.

4.3Withdrawing the consent. The artist can withdraw the consent at any time in PlotArt Studio. From then, their commission-free artworks are no longer offered in the basket. A basket already being paid when the consent is withdrawn is completed on the basis that applied when the buyer checked out.

4.4The consent does not change commission. It is not commission terms under PA-FAA cl. 6, and it cannot make any sale, including any sale arising from a Founding Enquiry, carry PlotArt commission.

4.5The consent statement. The statement shown to the artist, with an unticked box, is:

I agree that buyers may buy my artworks that carry no PlotArt commission through the PlotArt basket, under the Artist Basket Addendum PA-BAA-2026-11. I understand that:

• PlotArt charges 0% commission on these sales during my agreed commission-free period, and takes no fee of any kind from them;

• each payment goes to my own Stripe account, and Stripe deducts its actual card processing fee, and any VAT it charges on that fee, from my Stripe balance (for example about 1.5% + €0.25 for a standard EEA card; more for premium, UK and international cards). PlotArt does not reimburse it;

• if I refund a buyer, Stripe does not return its processing fee; if a buyer disputes a payment, Stripe may also charge me its dispute fees;

• I can withdraw this consent at any time in PlotArt Studio. My commission-free artworks are then no longer offered in the basket; baskets already being paid are completed;

• declining or withdrawing this consent does not affect my gallery listing, enquiries, direct sales or my Founding Artist Agreement.

5. Delivery offers and availability

5.1The delivery offer. An artwork is offered in the basket only while the artist has a current delivery offer for it, approved by the artist in PlotArt Studio. The delivery offer states, for delivery anywhere in the Republic of Ireland:

  • (a) the delivery charge, which the buyer pays in full to the artist;
  • (b) who packs, who books and contracts with the transporter, who pays for transport, and any installation;
  • (c) the delivery window, tracking, and whether a signed receipt and condition photographs are provided;
  • (d) the transit insurance: the insurer and who holds the cover, the covered value (at least the artwork price) and valuation basis, any excess and the main exclusions, the period of cover (from collection to signed delivery) and the claims contact;
  • (e) the return arrangements in PA-PAY 7.2: how the artwork would be returned, the returns address, the cost of return or a reasonable estimate of the maximum, and who arranges it.

The carrier's limited liability alone is not accepted as transit insurance.

5.2The delivery offer is the artist's approval. By approving a delivery offer, the artist confirms that the artwork is available and not promised elsewhere, that its description (location, condition, framing and glazing, dimensions and weight) is accurate, and that they will sell and deliver it as offered, at the gallery price, to any buyer who completes a basket. This takes the place, for basket orders, of approving each quote under PA-PAY 4.2. Any change to the offer creates a new version, which the artist approves again; a basket already being paid uses the version shown to the buyer.

5.3Keeping it current. The artist keeps the availability, price and description of each offered artwork, and its delivery offer, accurate and up to date. The artist can withdraw a delivery offer at any time; the artwork is then no longer offered in the basket, except to a buyer whose basket is already being paid.

5.4One original. While an artwork is offered in the basket, the artist will not sell or promise it elsewhere without first withdrawing its delivery offer or recording the sale in PlotArt Studio straight away. Once a buyer selects "Checkout", PlotArt's systems hold the artwork for that buyer for the checkout period, currently 31 minutes, plus 15 minutes for Stripe's confirmation, and for up to 30 minutes more while the buyer's bank asks them to confirm a payment. If, exceptionally, a payment is taken for an artwork the artist had already sold another way, the artist refunds that buyer in full (PA-BBT 5.3).

6. How payment is taken

6.1Authority. The artist authorises PlotArt, for each basket order for one of the artist's artworks:

  • (a) to have Stripe make a copy, on the artist's Stripe account, of the card the buyer entered once on Stripe's page, for use only for that buyer's payments to the artist in that basket;
  • (b) to create, on the artist's Stripe account, a card payment for exactly the artwork price and delivery charge shown to the buyer;
  • (c) to have the payment authorised first, and to take it only once every payment in the basket has been authorised;
  • (d) to cancel the authorisation if any payment in the basket cannot be authorised, if the buyer does not confirm a payment their bank asks them to confirm within 30 minutes, or if the basket is cancelled before payment is taken;
  • (e) to receive PlotArt's commission, if any, from that payment as a Stripe application fee;
  • (f) to issue refunds from that payment on the artist's instruction or with the artist's agreement (PA-PAY 7.4).

6.2All or nothing. No payment in a basket is taken until every payment in it has been authorised. If any cannot be, none is taken and every authorisation is cancelled. An artist is not paid, and owes nothing, for a basket that was not completed.

6.3An order is complete only when Stripe confirms to PlotArt that the payment for that artwork has been taken. PlotArt then marks the artwork sold in PlotArt Studio, where the artist sees the order, the buyer's name and delivery address, and the delivery offer version that applies.

6.4If a payment is taken but the basket cannot be completed. If, exceptionally, one or more payments in a basket have been taken but another cannot be, PlotArt tells the affected artists and the buyer straight away. Each order whose payment was taken stands as a completed sale of that artwork, and the buyer may cancel it under PA-BBT section 7 at no return cost if the artwork has not been dispatched; the artist then refunds the buyer in full. Commission on such a refund is returned in full under clause 7.

6.5Payouts from the artist's Stripe balance follow the artist's Stripe payout schedule. PlotArt does not hold the money, does not delay payouts until delivery and does not offer escrow.

7. Cancellations, returns, refunds and disputes

7.1PA-PAY clauses 7.1 to 7.9 apply to each basket order separately. A buyer's cancellation, return, refund or dispute for one artwork does not affect any other order in the basket.

7.2Commission when a buyer is refunded. On an order that carries commission, PlotArt returns commission as PA-GAA cl. 9 and PA-PAY 7.7 require. On an order that carries no commission there is no commission to return, and Stripe does not return its processing fee on a refunded payment.

7.3Disputes. PA-PAY 7.8 applies. Stripe's dispute fees are not reimbursed by PlotArt, on any order. PlotArt provides the order record, the basket page details shown to the buyer, the buyer's acceptance of PA-BBT and the delivery evidence it holds.

8. Dispatch and delivery

8.1The artist dispatches each artwork as stated in its delivery offer, within its delivery window, and records the dispatch and delivery evidence in PlotArt Studio: tracking, a signed receipt and condition photographs, where the offer provides for them.

8.2The transit insurance stated in the delivery offer must be in place for each shipment, and its confirmation reference is recorded in PlotArt Studio before dispatch.

8.3If the artist cannot dispatch within the delivery window, the artist tells the buyer and PlotArt straight away (PA-BBT 6.2).

9. Records and information

9.1PA-PAY clauses 8 and 9 apply to each basket order, with these additions:

  • (a) PlotArt keeps the basket record: the artworks, the amounts and basket page details shown to the buyer, the buyer's acceptance of PA-BBT, each authorisation, payment and cancellation, and the delivery offer version used;
  • (b) PlotArt shares with the artist the buyer's name and delivery address, and the buyer's email address where needed for delivery or a return. It does not tell the artist what the buyer bought from other artists;
  • (c) Stripe saves the buyer's card on PlotArt's Stripe account for the basket and copies it to the artist's Stripe account for that buyer's payments to the artist (clause 6.1(a)). The card number is never seen by PlotArt or the artist.

9.2The artist's acceptance of this addendum, their seller-information confirmation, any processing consent, each withdrawal, and each delivery offer version are kept for six years after the artist leaves the basket, or for as long as any of the artist's basket orders is kept, whichever is later.

10. Leaving the basket

10.1The artist can leave the basket at any time in PlotArt Studio. Their artworks are then no longer offered in the basket. Baskets already paid, or being paid, are completed under this addendum.

10.2Leaving the basket does not affect the artist's agreement, their gallery listing, enquiries, direct sales, any PA-PAY quote, or anything owed to a buyer or by PlotArt under this addendum.

10.3PlotArt may pause or close the basket for everyone, or stop offering a particular artwork, with notice in PlotArt Studio. Baskets already paid, or being paid, are completed.

11. Changes

11.1PlotArt can change this addendum only by issuing a new version with its own identifier. A new version applies to the artist only when the artist expressly accepts it in PlotArt Studio. Until then, the artist's artworks remain offered under the version they accepted, or, if PlotArt can no longer offer them under it, are withdrawn from the basket without any other effect.

12. Test mode and live payments

12.1PA-PAY clause 10 applies. Accepting this addendum, or any test version of it, in test mode does not authorise live payments. Before any live basket order, the artist must have joined the live payment pilot (PA-PAY), accept the issued live version of this addendum after it has been shown to them, confirm their seller information (clause 2.2), give the processing consent separately if they wish to offer commission-free artworks (clause 4), and complete Stripe's live onboarding, including payouts. No test-mode acceptance, confirmation, consent or account becomes a live one.
