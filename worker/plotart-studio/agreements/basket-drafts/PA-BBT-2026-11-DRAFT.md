# PlotArt Basket Purchase Terms — PA-BBT-2026-11 (DRAFT r2, 22 November 2026)

**Status: unissued draft for founder approval. Not shown to any buyer.** Derived from the issued Buyer Purchase Terms PA-BPT-2026-11, which are unchanged and continue to apply to single-artwork quotes. Review notes: `PA-BBT-PA-BAA-r2-REVIEW-NOTES.md`. Issued only as fingerprinted HTML (`agreements/build-basket.cjs`) and installed by migration 0021 with the founder's approval.

These terms apply when you buy one or more original artworks together through the PlotArt basket. Each artwork in your basket is sold to you by its own artist.

## 1. Who you are buying from

1.1 **Each artwork has its own seller.** Each artwork in your basket is sold to you by the artist named with it, who is the seller of that artwork. You make a separate contract of sale with each seller, for each artwork. Before you pay, the basket page shows these details for each seller under "Seller and returns information"; your purchase confirmation repeats them:
  (a) the seller's legal name and any trading name;
  (b) the geographical address at which they are established, and any different address for returns and complaints;
  (c) the seller's email and telephone number;
  (d) the seller's VAT number, if they are registered.

1.2 **The seller's declaration.** Each seller has declared to PlotArt that they sell as a trader, in the course of their business, craft or profession. PlotArt offers the basket only for artworks whose seller has made that declaration. If you are buying as a consumer, you have consumer rights under EU consumer protection law for each artwork, including the right to cancel; section 7 explains how to cancel.

1.3 **PlotArt's role.** PlotArt (PlotNua, operated by Colin Macaulay, CRO business name no. 794392, hello@plotnua.ie) introduces you to the sellers. It shows the artworks, provides the basket and the checkout, and coordinates the payments. PlotArt is not the seller of any artwork.
  (a) Each seller is responsible for their artwork, its delivery, cancellations, returns and refunds, and for the artwork matching its description.
  (b) PlotArt is responsible for the accuracy of the information it presents on the basket page and in your purchase confirmation, and for operating the basket and the checkout.
  (c) PlotArt can receive your cancellation notice, complaint or damage report on any seller's behalf at hello@plotnua.ie, and will pass it on to that seller.
  (d) **PlotArt's commission.** Some sellers pay PlotArt a commission out of the artwork price, under their own agreement with PlotArt; others pay none. A commission is never added to what you pay and does not change your rights.
  (e) PlotArt selects and orders artists and artworks by hand, for curatorial reasons. No seller pays for placement.

1.4 **One basket, separate purchases.** Buying several artworks together does not make PlotArt, or any seller, responsible for another seller's artwork. Your rights for each artwork are against that artwork's seller, and are not affected by what happens to any other artwork in your basket, except as section 4.3 says for payment.

## 2. What you pay

2.1 For each artwork, your basket page shows, in euro:
  (a) the artwork price;
  (b) separately, the seller's charge for packing, transport and transit insurance to your address in the Republic of Ireland;
  (c) the total for that artwork.

2.2 Your basket page also shows the total for all the artworks in your basket. Nothing else is added at checkout. PlotArt charges you no fee.

2.3 **Where we deliver.** The basket delivers only to addresses in the Republic of Ireland. For delivery elsewhere, please enquire about the artwork instead.

2.4 **Prices are fixed when you pay.** The price and delivery charge you are shown when you go to checkout are the amounts you pay. If either has changed since you added the artwork to your basket, the basket page shows the new amount before you continue, and you will not be charged an amount you were not shown.

## 3. Placing your order

3.1 **Steps.** To buy the artworks in your basket you:
  (a) check the artworks, sellers, amounts, delivery and return arrangements on the basket page;
  (b) give your name, email address and delivery address;
  (c) confirm that you have read these terms;
  (d) select "Buy now", which shows the total you will pay, then enter your card on Stripe's secure page and select "Save" there. Selecting "Save" on Stripe's page completes your order, with an obligation to pay the total shown. Your card is saved only to take the payments for your basket.

3.2 Until you select "Save" on Stripe's page, you can go back, correct your details or remove artworks, and nothing is bought. Your order is an offer to buy each artwork in your basket from its seller. Each contract of sale is made when your purchase is confirmed under section 4.4.

3.3 **Limits.** A basket can hold up to 10 artworks. PlotArt cannot arrange purchases of EUR 10,000 or more in total, including purchases that, together with other purchases by you or anyone PlotArt knows to be acting with you in the previous twelve months, reach that amount. An artwork priced at more than EUR 9,999.99 cannot be bought through the basket.

## 4. Payment

4.1 **You enter your card once.** You enter your card once, on Stripe's secure page. Stripe saves it for your basket so that PlotArt can make a separate card payment for each artwork, to that artwork's seller's own Stripe account. PlotArt does not hold your payments, and it is not an escrow arrangement.

4.2 **On your card statement**, each artwork appears as its own payment to its seller, so a basket of several artworks shows several payments. Your purchase confirmation lists each one.

4.3 **All or nothing.** PlotArt first asks your card issuer to authorise the payment for every artwork in your basket. Only when every payment has been authorised does PlotArt take them. If any payment cannot be authorised, for example because your card is declined or a seller can no longer accept payment:
  (a) no artwork in your basket is bought, and no payment is taken;
  (b) PlotArt cancels every authorisation already made. Your bank may show a cancelled authorisation as pending for a few days before it disappears; it is not a payment;
  (c) the basket page tells you, and you can try again.

4.4 **Your purchase is confirmed** when every payment in your basket has been taken. The page you return to shows whether your purchase has been confirmed.

4.5 **If your bank asks you to confirm a payment.** Your bank may ask you to confirm one or more of the payments, for example in your banking app. The basket page shows this. If you do not confirm within 30 minutes, no artwork is bought and every authorisation is cancelled under section 4.3.

4.6 **Your saved card.** Your card is saved and used only for the payments in this basket. PlotArt does not use it for any other payment. Once your basket is completed or cancelled, PlotArt removes the saved card from use for future payments.

4.7 **Your purchase confirmation.** Once your purchase is confirmed, PlotArt prepares a purchase confirmation for each artwork. It sets out the artwork, the seller and their contact details, what you paid for it, the delivery and return arrangements, these terms in full and the model cancellation form. You can open each confirmation from your order page, and PlotArt keeps an exact copy. PlotArt also emails them to you at the address you gave, as your confirmation of each contract.

## 5. One original; holding the artworks

5.1 Each original can be sold only once. When you select "Checkout", PlotArt holds every artwork in your basket for you for the checkout period, currently 31 minutes, plus a short time for confirmation, and longer while your bank is asked to confirm a payment (section 4.5). While an artwork is held, no one else can buy it through PlotArt, and its seller has agreed not to sell or promise it elsewhere.

5.2 An artwork in your basket is not held until you select "Checkout". If it has been sold or become unavailable before then, the basket page tells you, and you can remove it and continue with the others.

5.3 If, exceptionally, your payment is taken for an artwork that its seller had already sold another way, that seller refunds you in full for that artwork. Your purchases of the other artworks are not affected, unless you choose to cancel them under section 7.

## 6. Delivery and insurance

6.1 Each seller delivers their own artwork. Your artworks may therefore arrive separately, from different places and at different times. Before you pay, the basket page shows for each artwork its delivery charge, delivery window and tracking, that the shipment is insured, and the return arrangements. Your purchase confirmation sets out in full:
  (a) who packs and who transports the artwork;
  (b) the delivery window and tracking;
  (c) whether a signed receipt and condition photographs are provided;
  (d) the transit insurance arranged for the shipment: the insured value (at least the artwork price), the period of cover and how a claim is made.

6.2 The seller delivers to the address you gave, within the delivery window stated for that artwork. If a seller cannot deliver within that window, they will contact you, and you may agree a new date or end the contract for that artwork and be refunded in full for it.

6.3 Please inspect each artwork when it arrives. If it is damaged, it helps if you tell its seller or PlotArt (hello@plotnua.ie) within 48 hours, with photographs, so that any insurance claim can be made promptly. This is a request, not a condition. Reporting later does not affect your rights under section 8.

## 7. Changing your mind: your right to cancel

This section applies if you are a consumer buying from a seller who is a trader (section 1.2).

7.1 **Each artwork separately.** You may cancel your purchase of any one or more artworks in your basket, and keep the others. Cancelling one artwork does not cancel any other.

7.2 **Cancellation period.** You may cancel without giving any reason within 14 days from the day you, or someone you name other than the carrier, receive that artwork. If you bought two or more artworks from the same seller in one basket and they arrive separately, the 14 days for each of them run from the day you receive the last of them.

7.3 **How to cancel.** Tell the seller of the artwork, or PlotArt on the seller's behalf (hello@plotnua.ie), that you have decided to cancel, saying which artwork or artworks. You can use the model cancellation form in section 12 or any other clear statement, for example by email. To meet the deadline, it is enough to send your notice before the cancellation period ends.

7.4 **Returning an artwork.**
  (a) Return the artwork to the returns address shown for it, by the return method shown for it, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after you tell its seller or PlotArt that you are cancelling.
  (b) Unless the seller has said that they will collect it, you arrange the return.
  (c) Because original artworks cannot normally be returned by post, you bear the cost of returning an artwork when you cancel for a change of mind. The basket page and your purchase confirmation state that cost for each artwork, or a reasonable estimate of the maximum cost. Please pack the work as it was delivered. We recommend insuring it for its value while it is in transit.

7.5 **Your refund.** The seller refunds everything you paid for that artwork, including its delivery charge, without undue delay and no later than 14 days after being told that you are cancelling.
  (a) The seller may withhold the refund until it has received the artwork back, or until you have shown that you have sent it back, whichever is earlier.
  (b) The refund is made to the card you paid with, at no charge to you.
  (c) Cancelling one artwork does not change the price or delivery charge of any artwork you keep.

7.6 **Handling an artwork.** You may handle an artwork as you would in a gallery to establish its nature and characteristics. You may be liable for any loss in its value that results from handling beyond that.

## 8. If an artwork is damaged, faulty or not as described

8.1 Each seller must supply an artwork that matches its description and the information on the basket page. Your rights in this section are separate from, and in addition to, your right to cancel in section 7.

8.2 If an artwork arrives damaged, or is not as described, tell its seller or PlotArt. If you do so within 30 days of receiving it (or, if it was installed, of installation being completed), you may end the contract for that artwork and receive a full refund for it, including its delivery charge, to the card you paid with. You may instead ask for a repair or a replacement where that is possible. Your other legal remedies continue after that.

8.3 In these cases the seller bears the cost of returning the artwork, or reimburses you if you have paid it. The seller tells you how the return will be arranged.

8.4 None of this depends on any carrier or insurance claim being made or completed.

## 9. Refunds

9.1 Refunds are made per artwork, to your original card through Stripe, from the seller's own Stripe account. Your right to be repaid does not depend on how the seller's Stripe account is funded.

9.2 **If a refund is pending.** Stripe may hold a refund as pending until the seller's Stripe account can fund it. A pending refund has not been completed: you have not been repaid until Stripe completes it. While it is pending, the seller does not repay you another way as well, because you would then be repaid twice. The seller must make sure you are repaid within the time limits in sections 7 and 8.

9.3 If Stripe cannot complete a refund, or reverses one it first confirmed (for example because your card was closed), you have not been repaid. The seller then repays you once, either by a new refund through Stripe or directly, for example by bank transfer, and PlotArt confirms it with you.

9.4 PlotArt tells you when it becomes aware that a refund is pending, has failed or has been reversed. The notice is emailed to you and also appears on your order page.

## 10. Your data

10.1 **What PlotArt receives and keeps.** PlotArt receives your name, email address and delivery address with your basket. If you paid, it keeps them and the order record for each artwork until 31 December of the sixth year after that order is settled (completed or fully refunded, with nothing outstanding), for accounting and legal reasons. If no payment was taken, it keeps them until two years after the basket ended. They are never deleted while a payment dispute about the order is open. PlotArt keeps them longer only under a legal hold for a specific reason, such as a dispute, claim or tax enquiry about the order, reviewed at least every 12 months. The record includes the exact basket page details you were shown, your acceptance of these terms and your purchase confirmations.

10.2 **Stripe.** Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy.
  (a) Your card number is entered only on Stripe's page and is never seen by PlotArt or any seller.
  (b) To make a payment to each seller, Stripe saves your card on PlotArt's Stripe account and, for each seller, makes a copy of it on that seller's own Stripe account. These saved cards are used only for the payments in your basket (section 4.6).
  (c) Payment details Stripe records, such as your name, email address, billing country, and card brand and last four digits, are visible to each seller in their Stripe account, for that seller's payments only.
  (d) PlotArt's access to Stripe also lets it view these details: your name, email address and billing country, and your card's brand, country, type, expiry date and last four digits. PlotArt does not copy them into its own records, and looks at them only when needed for your order, for example to resolve a refund or a payment dispute. PlotArt sends your email address to Stripe at checkout.

10.3 **The sellers.** PlotArt shares with each seller only what that seller needs to deliver their artwork and handle any return: your name, delivery address and, where needed, your email address. A seller is not told what you bought from other sellers.

10.4 See the section "The PlotArt payment pilot" in the PlotNua privacy notice at plotnua.ie/privacy.html.

## 11. Questions, complaints and law

11.1 Contact the seller of the artwork at the contact details on your purchase confirmation, or PlotArt at hello@plotnua.ie, quoting your basket or order reference.

11.2 Irish law applies. If you are a consumer living elsewhere, you keep the protection of the mandatory rules of your own country.
