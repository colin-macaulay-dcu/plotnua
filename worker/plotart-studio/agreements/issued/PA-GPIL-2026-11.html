1. Who this licence is between, and what it covers

1.1This licence is between Colin MacAulay (registered as Colin Macaulay), an individual trading as PlotNua under Business Name Registration No. 794392, who operates PlotArt as an initiative of PlotNua (“PlotArt”), and the artist who accepts it in PlotArt Studio (“you”).

1.2It applies to every artist with a PlotArt gallery. For a Founding Artist it forms part of the PlotArt Founding Artist Agreement PA-FAA-2026-11.

1.3It covers what PlotArt may publish about you and your work, and how: your gallery; your biography; your portrait, if you supply one; your artworks’ images and details; and any room views made from them.

1.4You keep ownership and copyright of your works, images and words, and your moral rights. This licence lets PlotArt use them only as set out here.

2. Approval comes first

2.1PlotArt publishes nothing until you have approved a preview of exactly what will appear, by submitting it in PlotArt Studio.

2.2Changes you make in PlotArt Studio count as your approval. They are published straight away, after a quick check, or after review, as your artist agreement sets out (for Founding Artists, clause 9.3 of PA-FAA-2026-11).

2.3PlotArt may make purely technical changes without asking: resizing, compressing, converting file formats, converting colours to the standard web colour space (sRGB), removing hidden file information such as camera details and location data, and making thumbnails without cropping into the artwork. PlotArt will never otherwise alter the artwork’s appearance, colour balance or content.

3. The licence

3.1You grant PlotArt a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence, for as long as your gallery is listed (and, after removal, only as clause 6 allows), to reproduce, display and make available the content you have approved:

  • on PlotArt and PlotNua web pages: your gallery, your artwork pages, the Virtual Gallery, and PlotArt pages that feature artists in fair rotation;
  • as room views, if any are made: digital illustrations showing an image of your work at its true proportions in a room setting. Each is made only from images you supply or approve, is labelled as an illustration, and is shown to you before publication;
  • as thumbnails and link previews that show your work in search results and when a PlotArt page is shared.

3.2Promotional use beyond the website needs your separate yes for each use: social media posts, newsletters, press, print, advertising or partner features. There is no standing opt-in. You can say no without any effect on your gallery.

3.3PlotArt will not sell, license or give your images to anyone else; make prints or products from them; add your work to stock libraries; or use your images to train or develop artificial-intelligence models, or allow others to do so, so far as this is within PlotArt’s control.

3.4PlotArt credits you as the artist wherever your work appears on PlotArt. Credit in link previews depends on the platform.

4. Your permissions and declarations

4.1You confirm that you have the right to grant this licence for every image and text you supply. For each photograph you upload, you will say whether you took it or another photographer took it with their permission, giving their name for the credit. For works shown with other people’s property or likeness, you have any permission needed.

4.2If a gallery or agent represents you, you confirm that they agree to this licence.

4.3For each work you will say whether AI played a meaningful part in making it. Where it did, a short disclosure is shown on the work’s page.

6. Changing your mind, removal and deletion

6.1You can withdraw any work, image or detail, or your whole gallery, at any time in PlotArt Studio or by emailing hello@plotnua.ie. PlotArt removes it from its public website within 14 days, and usually much sooner.

6.2Withdrawing your choice to show your county or portrait takes effect within the same period.

6.3Originals. PlotArt keeps your original photographs privately in PlotArt Studio to prepare the web versions. A work you withdraw from your gallery stays in your private Studio account, with its photographs, so that you can show it again, until you delete it.

6.4Deleting. When you delete a photograph, delete a work that is not on show, or replace your portrait in PlotArt Studio, PlotArt deletes the original and its private copies within 30 days.

6.5Leaving. If you leave PlotArt, PlotArt keeps your account, gallery records and original photographs for 12 months after you stop taking part, as its privacy notice explains, and then deletes them. You can ask PlotArt to delete your original photographs sooner by emailing hello@plotnua.ie.

6.6Records PlotArt keeps. PlotArt keeps a record of what was published and when, without images. The web versions of published images also remain in PlotArt’s private version history of its website, which is not public and is not used for any other purpose. PlotArt may also keep anything the law requires, or that it needs to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

6.7After your gallery is removed, PlotArt names you publicly as a Founding Artist only with your agreement.

6.8PlotArt cannot control copies that others have already made, such as search-engine caches, screenshots or shares. It will ask search engines to refresh where it reasonably can.

7. General

7.1This licence is read with your PlotArt artist agreement (for Founding Artists, PA-FAA-2026-11). That agreement’s clauses on responsibility, governing law, changes and notices apply to this licence.

7.2PlotArt will not change this licence to widen how your content is used unless you accept a new, identified version in PlotArt Studio.

7.3This version applies from the date you accept it. It does not change any earlier acceptance, and no earlier acceptance is treated as acceptance of this wording.

Acceptance in PlotArt Studio

Founding Artists accept this licence together with the Founding Artist Agreement, in one statement:

“I have read and accept the PlotArt Founding Artist Agreement PA-FAA-2026-11, including the Gallery Publication and Image Licence PA-GPIL-2026-11.”

PlotArt Studio records the licence identifier, the fingerprint of this exact text, the date and time, your Studio account and the method of acceptance, and keeps this version available for you to download.
