None of that happens in Ireland yet. Eleven short questions, and we will tell you what your property has, what is still to establish, and what nobody can answer yet.

      Why PlotNua is saying this

      This check reads your eleven answers against what PlotNua can actually stand over. It is built around five things: the property and who controls it, the electricity supply, the energy already here, the connectivity, and the physical space and access.

      It holds no provider’s technical requirements, because none have been published. That is why there is no percentage and no compatibility finding anywhere in this result: a score would need a standard to score against, and there isn’t one. Where PlotNua cannot know something, it says so rather than filling the gap.

      What your property could do with its own electricity is a different question, and a longer one. It is set out in The House as a Power Station.

      PlotNua does not size equipment, quote for it, estimate what anyone would be paid, or speak for any provider. If this model ever reaches Ireland, the technical assessment would be the provider’s to make, not ours.
