Stage one

A homeowner discovers it

Homeowners arrive at PlotNua with a question about their property — not a product in mind. Someone exploring additional space in the garden would meet the TRIQ CUBE among other approaches, presented as what it is: a different answer to the same question, built from solid timber rather than panels.

What PlotNua has not established

PlotNua states gaps as plainly as it states facts. A homeowner sees this panel too. These are questions for TRIQBRIQ, and PlotNua will not answer them on TRIQBRIQ's behalf.

  • Irish availability is not yet confirmed. PlotNua holds no confirmation that the TRIQ CUBE can be supplied to the Republic of Ireland.
  • Delivery to Ireland is not established. Atlas records where a building is made, not who will deliver and install it here. Those are different facts and PlotNua will not treat one as the other.
  • No Irish price. The published price is a German-market price stated as including VAT and logistics. What an Irish buyer would pay, and under which VAT treatment, is not established.
  • Installation. Whether the kit is self-assembled or installed, and by whom in Ireland, is not established.
  • Configuration. Whether the published headline price includes the three listed options is not stated unambiguously on the page, so PlotNua does not assert either reading.

Stage five

The homeowner decides

PlotNua's work ends with a decided homeowner — someone who has understood their property, weighed the alternatives and chosen to pursue the TRIQ CUBE. That is the person who would arrive at TRIQBRIQ: informed, self-selected, and already past the questions a general enquiry has to start with.

Proposed · not yet agreed

The handoff to TRIQBRIQ

At the point of decision, PlotNua would pass the homeowner to TRIQBRIQ by an agreed, attributable route. TRIQBRIQ handles all product-specific and technical consultation, the transaction and fulfilment — exactly as Christian set out, and purchases complete on the official TRIQ product page.

PlotNua's role stops here. It does not advise on the TRIQ CUBE, does not quote for it, and does not take payment.
