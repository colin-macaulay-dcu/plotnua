# H5 HOMEPAGE · FOUNDER DECISION: ANIMATED CHIMNEY SMOKE

**Decision reference: `FX-H5-SMOKE-ANIMATION-20261021`**
Authorised by Colin MacAulay, Founder, 21 October 2026.

---

## 1 · What was decided

The code-generated chimney smoke animation (`assets/possibility-reveal/h5c-smoke.js`) is approved for
publication on the plotnua.ie homepage, above the Garden Retreat (L6) illustration.

It may be published as it stands in the H5C3 preview:
- it runs only while the Garden Retreat switch is on;
- it never runs under reduced motion.

**No additional correspondence with TimberIN is requested.**

## 2 · What this decision is NOT

**It is not supplier permission.** The decision is the founder's own design and risk decision. It is
**not additional written permission from TimberIN**, and nothing on the site, in any record or in any
report may describe it as one.

**TimberIN's permission is unchanged.** Its written permission and scope stay exactly as recorded in
`image-adaptation-licences.json` (`L6-TIMBERIN-TWO-PHOTOS-20261019`, evidence `PN-EVID-L6-TIMBERIN-20261020`):
- two photographs, adapted (size, angle, colour, lighting) into one PlotNua garden illustration;
- credited "Hot tub: TimberIN", with a link to https://www.timberin.com/.

That record is not edited by this decision.

## 3 · The facts the decision rests on

**What the animation is.** It is drawn entirely in code:
- procedural "value-noise" puffs on a canvas;
- placed in picture coordinates above the chimney's mouth.

**What it does not do.** It reads, copies and alters no image pixels. The locked L6 layer, including
TimberIN's adapted smoke photograph, is untouched underneath.

**What the audit found** (21 Oct 2026, `claude/possibility-reveal-h5c3-release-restriction-audit-2026-10-21.md`):
- **Covered:** TimberIN's written grant covers the finished still image.
- **Not expressly covered:** an animated overlay above the credited composite is not expressly within the
  described adaptations.
- **The founder's decision:** having seen that finding, the founder decided to publish the animation
  without seeking further confirmation.

## 4 · Obligations that remain in force

1. **The credit:** "Hot tub: TimberIN", with a working link, shown whenever the composite is visible.
2. **Labelling:** labelled as an illustration, with no wording implying endorsement by TimberIN.
3. **The live link:** TimberIN is sent the link once the image is published, as they requested.
4. **A supplier objection:** if TimberIN ever objects to the animation, it is switched off at once by not
   loading `h5c-smoke.js`. The still composite is unaffected.
