01 · The Reinvented Interior

Walls without limits

The wall becomes the canvas

Think bigger than a frame.

The most surprising change might be the wall itself. A specialist printer can bring a digital artwork into your home and print it directly onto a suitable surface. No frame is needed: the wall becomes the canvas.

It could be a giant whale across a charcoal wall, an abstract composition or a piece created especially for your room. The effect can be bold or quiet. A design that leaves space around it may work better than covering every centimetre.

Already possible in Ireland.

Irish operators already offer wall printing, including Wall Print Ireland, The Wall Printing Company and DMC Wall and Floor Printing. International equipment examples include wallPen and The Wall Printer.

Start with the surface.

Start with a wall assessment and a sample. The surface, image quality and space around the printer all matter. Dark walls may need a white printed layer, and the machine will usually leave margins near edges. Confirm those limits before approving the design.

02 · The Reinvented Interior

Surfaces reimagined

A new dimension.

A flat wall can gain depth. Existing fitted furniture can take on a different finish. Some changes begin with what is already there.

Three-dimensional wall panels create relief and shadow. Orac's range includes paintable wall coverings and profiles designed for indirect lighting, with Irish suppliers already offering the brand.

Architectural films offer another route. Specialist installers can apply finishes to suitable existing surfaces, including selected doors and furniture. 3M DI-NOC is one example available through Irish channels such as Park Graphics.

Worth considering

Keep what works.

Ask to see and touch samples in your own light. The finish depends on preparation, corners and edges as well as the material. Confirm the chosen product's suitability near heat or moisture and its fire performance where relevant.

Sources and product examples

Explore: https://www.oracdecor.com/en_ie · https://parkgraphics.ie/3m-di-noc/

03 · The Reinvented Interior

Light changes everything

Clear. Private. Different.

Some surfaces respond to light. Others let you decide when to see through them.

Switchable privacy film can change suitable glass from clearer to frosted at the touch of a control. It may be useful for an internal home-office partition or a room that sometimes needs privacy. Irish suppliers include CEV Filmbase and Advanced Window Films; Gauzy also describes an installer route in Dublin.

Ask for a demonstration in both states. Check the view when lights are on behind the glass, the remaining haze, wiring and what happens if power is lost. Privacy film should not be assumed to provide blackout or replace an insulation upgrade.

Worth considering

See it in your own light.

For a different effect, backlit stone can turn a feature wall into a source of patterned light. Lithos Design's modular marble surfaces are an international example. These require specialist design, installation and an Irish availability check, with access for future lighting repairs planned from the beginning.

Sources and product examples

Explore: https://filmbaseireland.ie/ · https://advancedwindowfilms.ie/commercial/smart-film-commercial/ · https://www.lithosdesign.com/backlit-walls/

04 · The Reinvented Interior

Art that lives with you

Beautiful, with a purpose.

Art can do more than change the view. A printed acoustic panel combines an image with material designed to absorb reflected sound. It may help soften the echo in a hard-surfaced room while looking like a framed artwork.

DB Sound Panel in Galway offers custom-image panels. AKUART's Imagine is an international example combining art and acoustic treatment. The right size and placement depend on the room. These panels address sound within a room; they should not be presented as a way to block neighbours or outside traffic.

Digital displays offer another kind of flexibility. Meural presents changing art on a display, while InkPoster uses colour E Ink for a paper-like appearance. E Ink changes slowly and looks different from a bright television. See a sample before deciding which approach suits your space. Irish availability and support should be confirmed for the chosen product.

Worth considering

A future Irish-art connection.

PlotArt, an initiative within PlotNua, could offer a future route to specially licensed Irish artwork for murals, panels or displays. Each use would require the artist's separate agreement. Buying an original painting does not automatically include permission to reproduce it on a wall.

05 · The Reinvented Interior

Behind the transformation

The room behind the image.

The most useful question is simple: what will it take to achieve this in your room?

For wall printing, ask about preparation, printable area, image proofing, moving furniture and the time the room will be unavailable. UV ink cures during printing, but safe installation still requires the contractor's handling and ventilation arrangements. Ask when the room can be used again and how the finished surface should be cleaned.

Compare complete quotations. A square-metre price may leave out artwork, licensing, repairs, travel or VAT. The Wall Printing Company advertises standard printing from €140/m² and a €250 minimum; these are supplier guide figures, not a PlotNua quotation. A current written price should set out every inclusion.

Worth considering

Ask for the whole picture.

For powered glass and illuminated surfaces, plan wiring, controls and repair access. Where the electrical work is restricted domestic work, use a Registered Electrical Contractor. For all products, confirm who supplies, installs and honours the warranty.

Robots are also changing painting and wall finishing overseas. Okibo and MYRO are examples worth watching, although this is currently a contractor-led possibility rather than a routine service for a furnished Irish living room.

06 · The Reinvented Interior

Make it happen

Begin with one idea.

Begin with one room and one purpose. Do you want a personal artwork, a calmer-sounding space, more privacy or a new finish for something you already own?

Photograph the space and note its dimensions. Gather a few references. Then ask an appropriate supplier for a sample, a feasibility check and a complete quotation before committing.

If an artwork is involved, agree the reproduction rights, scale and final proof with the artist. If installation is involved, confirm access, preparation, disruption, maintenance and responsibility for the finished result.
